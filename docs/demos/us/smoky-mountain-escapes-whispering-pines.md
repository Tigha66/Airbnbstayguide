# Demo guide: Smoky Mountain Escapes, Whispering Pines (Pigeon Forge, TN)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Smoky Mountain Escapes' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Whispering Pines:** Smoky Mountain Escapes lists about 25 condos at Whispering Pines (unit codes
WP101 to WP653) on their Escapia booking site, all at one resort in downtown Pigeon Forge with the same
pools, lazy river, pavilion and fitness room. One guide fits a big slice of their inventory. The sample
unit is **WP241 "Perfection"** (1 bedroom, sleeps 4, fourth floor).

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- https://www.yoursmokymountainescape.com/rentals-at-whispering-pines-in-pigeon-forge/
- https://www.yoursmokymountainescape.com/whispering-pines-condos-in-pigeon-forge/
- https://bookings-yoursmokymountainescape.escapia.com/Unit/Details/262866 (WP241 "Perfection", plus WP231 at /Unit/Details/273635 for comparison)
- https://www.yoursmokymountainescape.com/policies/ (check-in/out, pets, smoking, parking passes, vehicles)
- https://www.yoursmokymountainescape.com/free-tickets/ (Xplorie free attraction tickets)
- https://www.yoursmokymountainescape.com/packages-add-ons/ (real add-on prices)
- https://www.yoursmokymountainescape.com/condo-and-cabin-management/ (locally owned, 25+ years)

Every fact below comes from those pages. Not published anywhere: door codes, Wi-Fi name/password,
pool and hot tub hours, outdoor pool season dates, and the "78 cabins" figure (from FindRentals, not their
own site, so it is left out of the email). The policies page header shows 866-265-0902 but every other
page says 865-265-0902; the kit uses 865. Their owners/team are not named on the site.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Whispering Pines 241 — Pigeon Forge`
   - **City, country:** `Pigeon Forge, Tennessee`
   - **Street address:** `205 Ogle Drive, Pigeon Forge, TN 37863` (published on the listing).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Whispering Pines 241, "Perfection", a one-bedroom condo on the fourth floor of Whispering Pines in downtown Pigeon Forge, managed by the locally owned team at Smoky Mountain Escapes. Your covered balcony with rocking chairs looks out over the city lights of Pigeon Forge and the Smoky Mountains.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is anytime after 4:00 PM. We do our best to have every condo ready by 4:00 PM, but on very busy turnover days or in bad weather it can take a little longer.
Check-in is contactless. Your door code and arrival details come in your arrival message from Smoky Mountain Escapes. Please don't share them.
Smoky Mountain Escapes is not on-site at Whispering Pines. The office at the resort belongs to a different rental company, so for anything about your stay please call or text us on 865-265-0902. We are available 7 days a week.
Whispering Pines sits between traffic lights 7 and 8, just off the Pigeon Forge Parkway near the Old Mill District.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: The condo sleeps 4 guests at most. The queen sleeper sofa in the living room is for children only.
The person who signed the rental agreement must stay for the whole reservation, and must be 25 or older.
Every condo is strictly non-smoking and non-vaping, including the balcony. Tennessee law requires smokers to be 25 feet away from the building. Smoking or vaping in the unit carries a fine of at least $500.
No pets. Only licensed service animals are allowed, and emotional support animals are not. An unauthorized pet means a non-refundable fee of at least $1,000.
No parties, weddings or events unless approved in advance by Smoky Mountain Escapes.
Please follow the Whispering Pines resort rules. Breaking them can end your stay without a refund.
If you need a package mailed to you during your stay, please arrange it with our office first.

THE CONDO: One king bedroom with its own flat-screen TV, one bathroom with a walk-in shower, and a leather sleeper sofa and recliner in the living room.
The fully equipped kitchen has stainless-steel appliances, a dishwasher, microwave, coffee maker, ice maker, toaster and cookware. There is seating for four.
Washer and dryer in the condo, plus an iron and board and a hair dryer.
Smart TV with cable, air conditioning and heating. The gas fireplace is seasonal.
We supply starter essentials such as paper towels, toiletries and dish soap. Towels and linens are provided. Pool towels are not, so bring your own.
There is an elevator in the building.

POOLS: Whispering Pines has an indoor heated pool and indoor hot tub, open all year.
In season there is also an outdoor pool, outdoor hot tub and lazy river.
The covered picnic pavilion has both gas and charcoal grills, and there is a fitness room.
Swimming is at your own risk. Pool and hot tub closures are set by the resort, not by Smoky Mountain Escapes.

PARKING: This condo comes with 1 parking pass. Please keep it visible in your car at all times, because parking is strictly enforced.
Park only in marked spaces, never on the road or the grass. Cars parked in the wrong place can be towed at the owner's expense.
Trailers, U-Hauls, motor homes, commercial and recreational vehicles are not allowed.

FREE TICKETS: As our guest you get one free adult admission every day of your stay to top local attractions, such as Dollywood, Dolly Parton's Stampede, Pirates Voyage, the Hatfield and McCoy Dinner Feud, Rowdy Bear Snow Park and the Biltmore Estate.
About 48 hours after booking you get an email from Xplorie with your personal booking link. You can also call 850-502-5217 or visit bookactivities.com/mylink.
Unused admissions expire at the end of each day and can't be saved or transferred.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
Please lock all doors when you leave and take your valuables with you.
If you leave something behind, call us. We can ship it back on request, with a minimum $50 shipping charge.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the condo, including maintenance problems, call or text Smoky Mountain Escapes on 865-265-0902, 7 days a week.
Our office is at 1250 Middle Creek Road, Suite A, Sevierville, TN 37862. Email Info@YourSmokyMountainEscape.com.

LOCAL TIPS: You are moments from the Pigeon Forge Parkway and minutes from the Old Mill District, Dolly Parton's Stampede, Cal Ripken's Experience and the LeConte Convention Center.
Dollywood is about a 10-minute drive.
Great Smoky Mountains National Park, with its trails, waterfalls and views, is within an hour's drive.
In the evening, the balcony is a lovely spot to watch the Pigeon Forge city lights.
Want more? Smoky Mountain Escapes can add a birthday surprise, a grill pack or wine and roses to your stay. Call at least 48 hours before you arrive.
```
