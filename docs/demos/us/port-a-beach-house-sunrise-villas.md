# Demo guide: Port A Beach House Company, Sunrise Villas (Port Aransas, TX)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Port A Beach House Company's homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Sunrise Villas:** it's the only complex where they list several near-identical units on their own site
(Sunrise Villas 101, 105, 207 and 208 at Banyan Beach): one-story, 1-bedroom condos that sleep 4, with a
shared pool, poolside charcoal grills and a boardwalk to the beach. One guide fits all four listings.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- visitporta.com/port-aransas-vacation-rentals/sunrise-villas-101-boardwalk-ocean-view (main source)
- visitporta.com/port-aransas-vacation-rentals/sunrise-villas-208-boardwalk-beach and
  visitporta.com/port-aransas-vacation-rentals/sunrise-villa-105-boardwalk-beach (same complex, cross-checked)
- visitporta.com/port-a-beach-house-company-terms-conditions (check-in/out times, $50 early/late fees, smoking, pets, HVAC, emergencies)
- visitporta.com/about-us (team names), visitporta.com/activities, visitporta.com/getting-port-aransas, visitporta.com/beach-gear

Every fact below comes from those pages. Not published (so left out or handed over): street address and unit
directions, door codes, Wi-Fi name and password, golf cart prices. Unit layouts differ slightly (101 and 105
have two bathrooms, 208 has one), so the manual keeps the bathroom count general.

---

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Sunrise Villas at Banyan Beach — Port Aransas`
   - **City, country:** `Port Aransas, Texas`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Sunrise Villas at Banyan Beach! Your one-story condo is just steps from the Gulf, with a private boardwalk to the sand and a sparkling community pool right outside. Port A Beach House Company is your local team, and we're so glad you're here.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 4:00 PM. Housekeeping and maintenance do everything they can to be ready by 4:00 PM, but the time is approximate, so please don't arrive early unless we've approved it.
This is a self check-in. Your check-in instructions and door code are sent in your arrival message, once we've received a copy of the driver's license of the person who booked. Please don't share the code.
Early check-in may be available for $50. It must be arranged in advance and depends on housekeeping, so it isn't guaranteed.
Driving in? The free Port Aransas Ferry from Aransas Pass runs 24 hours a day. In summer, Spring Break and on holiday weekends lines can be long in the afternoon, so allow extra time, or come over the JFK Causeway from Corpus Christi and follow SH 361 north. Tune to AM 530 for ferry updates.

WI-FI: Wi-Fi and cable are included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.
Please note that island internet can be spotty and outages happen from time to time, so it isn't suitable for remote work. We suggest downloading movies and shows before you arrive.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 4 guests. This is set by City ordinance, and only the guests on your reservation may stay.
Sunrise Villas is not pet friendly. Pets found at a non-pet-friendly home mean losing the full security deposit and possible eviction.
No smoking inside. A $500 cleaning fee applies if anyone smokes indoors. Oil lamps and incense are not allowed either.
No parties or events, and no fraternity, sorority, bachelor or bachelorette groups. Please keep noise down for your neighbors.
Please keep towels, sheets, pillows and blankets inside the condo. Bring your own beach and pool towels.
Lock all doors and windows whenever you go out, and please supervise children at all times.

CONDO: A one-story condo with no elevator, a king bed in the bedroom and a queen sleeper sofa in the living room.
Fully equipped kitchen with the basics, a standard coffee maker, blender and toaster, plus flat-screen TVs and board games.
Washers and dryers are on the premises. They are shared with the community and run by coin or app.
Air conditioning is provided. Please keep the A/C at 72°F or above, the heat at 78°F or below, the fan on Auto, and the doors and windows closed while it's running.
We leave a starter set of soap, one toilet paper roll per bathroom, one paper towel roll, dishwasher pods and two trash bags. Please bring extra, plus shampoo, conditioner, sunscreen, food, coffee and spices.

POOL AND GRILL: The community pool is shared and unheated. Swimming is at your own risk, and children must be supervised.
Charcoal grills are at the pool. Grilling is at the pool only.

SAND AND SURF: Your private boardwalk leads from the complex to the beach, just a short stroll away.
Need gear? Bron's Beach Rentals has three locations along the Port A beaches with umbrellas, tents, chairs and games, and they can also sell you a beach parking permit if you want to drive on the sand.
Want a golf cart? Ask us about adding a 6-seater golf cart to your stay. Our partner Beachin Buggies will contact you for your driver's license and insurance.
Remember you're on an island. Mosquitoes can be plentiful at times of year, even though we treat every property monthly.

PARKING: Parking for one standard vehicle.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 11:00 AM sharp. Thank you for staying with us!
Late check-out may be available for $50, but it must be approved in advance. Leaving after 11:00 AM without approval may be charged by the hour.
Before you go, please wash and put away the dishes, put furniture back where you found it, turn off all the lights, take all trash to the outdoor bins, and lock the door.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the condo, including trouble getting in or something not working, call Port A Beach House Company on 361-749-0027. Our answering service is available 24/7.
Please tell us about any problem during your stay so we can fix it.

LOCAL TIPS: Port Aransas sits on the northern tip of Mustang Island, with miles of sandy shoreline, world-class fishing and more than 500 bird species passing through each year.
- Explore the quiet trails at Mustang Island State Park.
- Take the Red Dragon Pirate Cruise, or book a bay fishing trip with Dirty 3rd Fishing Guides.
- Visit the historic Aransas Pass (Lydia Ann) Lighthouse.
- Spend a rainy day at the Port Aransas Museum, with exhibits on maritime history and the Tarpon Era.
- Roberts Point Park (301 J C Barr Blvd) has a playground, volleyball, shuffleboard, picnic tables and an amphitheater.
- For seafood, try FINS Grill and Icehouse (they'll cook your own catch), Grumbles Seafood Company, Castaways Seafood & Grill, or Tortuga's Saltwater Grill for Sunday brunch.
- For a special dinner, Lisabellas is at 5009 TX-361, phone 361-749-4222.
Corpus Christi International Airport (CRP) is about 30 minutes away.
```

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The two $50
prices are **their real published fees** (Terms & Conditions, sections 5 and 6). The golf cart price is a
**demo example**: they don't publish one, so tell them they set their own.

| Name | Price | Description |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | 4:00 PM (approximate); early check-in $50 if arranged in advance, not guaranteed |
| Can we check out at 1 PM? | Check-out is 11:00 AM sharp; late check-out $50, must be pre-approved |
| Can we bring our dog? | No, Sunrise Villas isn't pet friendly |
| Where can we grill? | Charcoal grills at the community pool; grilling at the pool only |
| Is the pool heated? | No, it's a shared, unheated community pool |
| Is the Wi-Fi good enough for Zoom calls? | Island internet is spotty; not suitable for remote work |
| Is the ferry open at night? | Yes, free and 24 hours a day; allow extra time in summer |
| ¿Cuál es la contraseña del wifi? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: it's in your arrival message |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the team |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish, useful in South Texas) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the Wi-Fi/remote-work answer, the Spanish answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
