# Demo guide: Abode Vacation, Treetop Lodge at The Snow Bear (Taos Ski Valley, NM)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Abode Vacation's condos *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why The Snow Bear:** Abode lists 12 condos in this one community at Taos Ski Valley (the Aspen,
Breckenridge, Chamonix, Davos and Vail units), and most are pet-friendly. They share the same
directions, parking lot, winter 4WD rule and company policies, so one guide fits a dozen listings with
small edits. We use **Treetop Lodge - Breckenridge 1** (2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, sleeps 8, pets OK).

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- https://www.abodevacation.com/vrp/unit/Treetop_Lodge_Breckenridge_1 (layout, amenities, stairs, 4WD note)
- https://www.abodevacation.com/community/the-snow-bear/ (address, directions, parking, distances)
- https://www.abodevacation.com/terms-conditions/ (check-in/out times, QR check-in, checkout steps, rules, pets, fires, after-8 PM calls)
- https://www.abodevacation.com/faq/ (grocery stocking fee, mid-stay cleans, cell service, winter driving, shipping)
- https://www.abodevacation.com/community/powderhorn_condominiums/ (base-area markets, airports)
- https://www.abodevacation.com/concierge-services/ and https://www.abodevacation.com/ ("founded here by people who live here", living wage)

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not verified:** whether the fireplace is wood or gas (the
listing says "stone fireplace"), the exact ski storage and laundry arrangements (both appear only as
photo captions), and the pet fee amount. Lockbox code and Wi-Fi password are left as "in your arrival
instructions". The site names no staff, so the email goes to "Abode Vacation team".

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Treetop Lodge at The Snow Bear — Taos Ski Valley`
   - **City, country:** `Taos Ski Valley, New Mexico`
   - **Street address:** `22 Firehouse Road, Taos Ski Valley, NM` (published on the Snow Bear page)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Treetop Lodge, Breckenridge 1 at The Snow Bear, a top-floor ski-in/ski-out condo overlooking Taos Ski Valley, cared for by Abode Vacation. Abode was founded here by people who live here, and we only serve our local area. Wake up in a secluded, lodge-like setting and still be first to the lifts on a powder day.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is any time after 4:00 PM Mountain Time. Your arrival instructions are emailed 48 hours before you arrive, and the lockbox details are in them.
When you get in, please check in by scanning the QR code on the welcome card near the entry. If you can't find the card or can't scan it, text 575-224-6032 to let us know you've arrived.
The condo is up two flights of outdoor stairs, which can be snowy in winter.
Want to arrive early? Text 575-224-6032 at least 48 hours ahead. On holiday dates and at some locations a fee may apply.
Getting here. From Hwy 150 you crest the hill and drop into the ski valley. Stay right on the main road past the public lots, then turn right onto Firehouse Road at the blue sign for the Children's Ski School and Rio Hondo Learning Center. At the traffic circle bear right past the firehouse and continue up Firehouse Road until it levels out below the Snow Bear's two wood-clad buildings. Turn left up the mountain, keep the buildings on your right, swing wide left past the one-story building and turn right into the parking lot.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi, plus smart TVs. The network name and password are in your arrival instructions.
For security reasons, please don't download software or files on the guest network.
Cell signal in the Taos area can be spotty. Verizon is usually the best, and Taos Ski Valley has better coverage than the valley villages.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 8 guests, including children and infants. The person booking must be at least 25, and we may ask for photo ID.
No smoking or vaping inside.
Please take your shoes off inside the home.
Pets are welcome here. Up to two pets, declared at booking, with the pet fee paid. Keep dogs on a leash, clean up after them, keep them off the furniture and don't leave them alone in the condo.
Respect the neighbors and local noise rules. We may use smart sensors to measure volume levels. Disturbances that bring a call from a neighbor or a visit from law enforcement can mean a charge of at least $500.
No fireworks, explosives or illegal firearms.
Grills must be used outside, in uncovered areas, and must stay where they are. Please follow any local burn bans.
If you use the fireplace, follow the safe-use instructions in the house manual in the condo. Always use the fire screen, and never take burning or smoldering wood out of the fireplace.

THE CONDO: About 1,000 sq ft with high ceilings, wood floors and a great room with a clerestory window and a stone fireplace.
The first bedroom has two queen beds. The second bedroom has a queen bed and a bunk bed. Each bedroom has its own private bathroom, TV and entrance, and a view of the valley.
The full kitchen opens onto the dining and family room, with a dishwasher, oven, microwave, coffee maker, blender, toaster and pots and pans.
Washer and dryer, iron and ironing board, hair dryer, extra pillows and blankets.
A narrow private balcony looks across the valley, perfect for an evening drink at sunset.
Need more room? The neighboring condo, Breckenridge 4, can be booked with this one for four bedrooms.

PARKING: Park in our main lot next to the one-story building, angled into the mountain. Only one car per condo can stay overnight in this lot.
In winter you need four-wheel drive or all-wheel drive to reach The Snow Bear. Two-wheel drive is fine in summer.
Between late November and late April, everyone driving to Taos Ski Valley should have 4WD, AWD or at least tire chains. Check the forecast before you set off.

SKIING: The Snow Bear is ski in/ski out, just above the Taos Ski Valley Ski School and the gondola.
Ask our on-site manager for the best runs of the day, or for a dinner suggestion.
In summer you have easy access to Taos Ski Valley's hiking trails, and you can walk to the ski village for meals, shopping and entertainment.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is before 11:00 AM Mountain Time. Thank you for staying with us!
Before you go, please turn off the lights and set the heat to 65°F (November to April) or off in summer.
Start the dishwasher if needed, put the furniture back where it was, close the windows and doors, and lock up.
Bag and tie your trash and dispose of it as the house manual in the condo explains.
Text or email us to say you've left.
Want to stay later? Text 575-224-6032 at least 48 hours ahead. A fee may apply.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the condo, call or text Abode Vacation on 575-224-6032, or email reservations@abodevacation.com. We're a small team, so texts are often fastest. After 8:00 PM Mountain Time, please save non-emergencies until 8:00 AM.
The condo has smoke and carbon monoxide detectors and a fire extinguisher.
High alpine areas are harsh on the unprepared, and many places around Taos have no cell coverage. Tell someone your plans before you hike or drive somewhere remote.

LOCAL TIPS: The ski village shops, restaurants and small grocery markets are about 0.3 miles away. Cid's Mountain Market and Bumps Market in the base area are small with limited stock. For a big grocery run, head down to Taos.
We can stock your groceries before you arrive or during your stay. You pay the cost of the order plus a delivery fee, usually $50 to $80.
Want a restaurant, activity or day trip set up? Abode's concierge can recommend and book. Call 575-224-6032.
Shipping something? UPS and FedEx won't deliver to Taos Ski Valley addresses, so ask us for an alternate address before you send anything.
Taos and Taos Ski Valley are dog-friendly, but there aren't many places for dogs to run off-leash safely.
Flying in? Taos airport has limited flights (JSX). Most visitors fly into Santa Fe, Albuquerque or Denver.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `4:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `11:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival instructions` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `575-224-6032` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. Abode
publishes a real grocery-stocking fee (cost of the order plus $50–80 delivery). The other two prices
are **demo examples**: Abode only says fees "may apply", so tell them they set their own prices.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Grocery stocking | $65 + groceries | Arrive to a stocked kitchen. You pay for the order plus delivery (usually $50–80, billed after delivery). |
| Early check-in (from 1:00 PM) | $50 | Get on the mountain sooner. Request 48 hours ahead; subject to availability. |
| Late checkout (until 1:00 PM) | $50 | One last run before the drive home. Request 48 hours ahead; subject to availability. |
