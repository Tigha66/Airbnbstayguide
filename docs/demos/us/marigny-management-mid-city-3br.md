# Demo guide: Marigny Management, Mid-City 3BR by the Streetcar (New Orleans, LA)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Marigny Management's homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why this home:** "Brand New Clean Modern 3br by Streetcar Line!" is a typical Marigny Management
listing (hosted by Jordan, Superhost, 10 years hosting): a whole house for 6 in Mid-City, a block from
the streetcar. Marigny Management doesn't have property pages on its own site (its "Book Now" goes to
Jordan's Airbnb profile), so this Airbnb listing is the best public source.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- marignymanagement.com (home page, /team, /laws): company, Jordan Jacobs (founder, 2016), phone, email.
- airbnb.com/rooms/52773640 ("Brand New Clean Modern 3br by Streetcar Line!"): description, house
  rules, check-in/out, amenities, registration number.

Every fact below comes from those pages. Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders.
**Unverified / check:** the listing shows **Registration Details 22-XSTR-17493** (the New Orleans
short-term rental permit field); the page data also contains another permit number (21-CSTR-15560)
that seems to belong to a different listing, so confirm the right number with Jordan before showing it
to guests. The description says 2.5 baths while the header says 3 baths. Airbnb's "Property info"
also says "Some spaces are shared", which conflicts with "complete access to the house", so it's left out.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Mid-City 3BR by the Streetcar — New Orleans`
   - **City, country:** `New Orleans, Louisiana`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the city).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to your brand-new 3-bedroom house in beautiful Mid-City New Orleans, managed by the local team at Marigny Management. The house is crisp and modern, with a white gourmet kitchen, white granite baths, a garden tub and tasteful Mardi Gras art. It's a block from the streetcar line, with a walkability score of 93.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is after 4:00 PM. Your entry instructions and door code are sent in your arrival message from Marigny Management. Please don't share them.
You have the whole house to yourselves, with a private entrance. Please note there are stairs to climb.
Arriving early or leaving late? Luggage drop-off is allowed, so ask us and we'll arrange it.

WI-FI: Fast Wi-Fi throughout the house, and there's a dedicated workspace if you need to work. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests.
No smoking.
No pets.
No parties or events.
You'll be staying in someone's home, so please treat it with care and respect.

THE HOUSE: Three bedrooms with four beds, including queen beds.
Full kitchen with stove, oven, microwave, dishwasher, refrigerator, toaster, coffee maker, pots and pans, oil, salt and pepper, dishes and cutlery.
Washer and dryer, iron, hair dryer, towels, bed linens, shampoo and room-darkening shades.
Air conditioning and heating.
A backyard with an open grassy space.

PARKING: Free street parking. There is no parking on the property itself.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is before 11:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
Please lock the door when you leave.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
The house has a smoke alarm, a carbon monoxide alarm, a fire extinguisher and a first aid kit.
For anything about the house, call Marigny Management on (504) 294-7924 or email manager@marignymanagement.com.

LOCAL TIPS: The streetcar line is a block away and can take you to the French Quarter.
The Lafitte Greenway runs alongside the neighborhood and leads into the French Quarter. It's great for walking and cycling.
Right nearby are the Broad Theater, the Refresh Project and Marji's Grill.
Mid-City is very walkable (walkability score 93), so you may not need a car at all.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `4:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `11:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `(504) 294-7924` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples**: Marigny Management doesn't publish extra fees, so tell Jordan he sets his own.

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | After 4 PM; luggage drop-off is allowed if you arrive early |
| Where do we park? | Free street parking; no parking on the property |
| How do we get to the French Quarter? | Streetcar a block away, or the Lafitte Greenway on foot or by bike |
| Can we have a few friends over for a party? | No parties or events; maximum 6 guests |
| Can I bring my dog? | No pets |
| Is there a washer? | Yes, washer and dryer |
| ¿A qué hora es la salida? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: before 11 AM |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** and the **Extras** tab.
