# Demo guide: Living4Malaga, Seafront Duplex (Pedregalejo, Málaga, Spain)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Living4Malaga's holiday homes *before* contacting
them, so the first message says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why the Seafront Duplex:** a 170 m², 3-bedroom duplex for 6 guests with sea views, 200 m from the
Baños del Carmen beach in Pedregalejo. Its listing plus Living4Malaga's general terms cover the
questions every guest asks: the strict 16:00 check-in, what to do if you arrive after 19:00, online
check-in and ID, the €300 deposit, towel changes, and what to leave tidy at checkout.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): living4malaga.com/en/vacation-rentals/seafront-duplex/
(description, equipment, "Please note" block: key safe, 16:00 check-in, €300 deposit, linen and
towels), living4malaga.icnea.net/en/availability-h1079 (booking page, 6 guests), and
living4malaga.com/en/terms-and-conditions/ (arrivals after 19:00, online check-in, ID for guests over
16, check-out before 12:00, parties, departure tidy-up). Every fact below comes from those pages.
**Unverified:** the check-out time is the company's *general* rule ("before 12:00, in some properties
this schedule may vary"), not stated on this listing, so confirm it. No tourist licence number, no
street address and no parking information are published for this home. The key-safe code and Wi-Fi
password are placeholders.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Seafront Duplex — Pedregalejo, Málaga`
   - **City, country:** `Málaga, Spain`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (not published).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to the Seafront Duplex, a bright 3-bedroom, 170 m² holiday home in Pedregalejo with fantastic views of the sea and the Bay of Málaga, managed by Living4Malaga. You are just 200 metres from the sandy beach of El Balneario de los Baños del Carmen, facing the Pablo Ruiz Picasso Promenade. Beach and city, with all the comforts of home.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 4:00 PM. Please note that the apartment cannot be accessed before 4:00 PM.
The apartment has a key safe. The address and the key-safe code are in your welcome email and arrival message.
Please tell us your approximate arrival time and complete the online check-in before you arrive.
Arriving after 7:00 PM? Please contact us in advance so we can organise your arrival.
A security deposit of €300 applies for any unforeseen incidents during your stay.

GUEST REGISTRATION: Spanish law requires holiday rentals to register the identity of their guests with the authorities. That is why we ask you to complete the online check-in before arrival. On arrival, please have the ID card or passport of the person who booked and of every companion aged over 16 ready. Thank you, it keeps everyone's stay legal and safe.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

THE APARTMENT: A duplex for up to 6 guests (babies under 2 do not count).
- One bedroom with a double bed and an en-suite bathroom with a shower, one bedroom with two single beds, and one bedroom with a double bed. There is a separate bathroom with a shower.
- On the ground floor, a living-dining room and a fully equipped separate kitchen.
- Kitchen with fridge, freezer, microwave, oven, dishwasher, coffee maker, toaster, blender, kettle, dinnerware and utensils.
- Washing machine, iron and hairdryer, a terrace with sea views, and TV.
- Air conditioning and split heating in all bedrooms and the living room.
Clean sheets and one set of towels per person are provided. Cleaning is not included during your stay, and sheets and towels are not changed for stays of under 7 nights. Extra towels are available for a charge, so ask the reception team.
- Stays of 8 to 12 nights, you will find extra bed linen and towels in the apartment.
- Stays of 13 nights or more, our team visits once a week to change sheets and towels.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests (babies under 2 not counted).
No parties of any kind, including hen and stag parties.
Please respect the quiet hours and your neighbours, and follow the usage instructions for the apartment.
When you go out, lock the door and close all the windows.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is before 12:00 noon. Leaving later without our permission has an extra charge.
Before you leave, please
- take the rubbish to the outdoor containers,
- wash the dishes and kitchen utensils,
- leave the apartment tidy,
- leave the keys where we told you at check-in. Never leave the keys in the lock on the inside, because any cost this causes is charged to the guest.
Thank you for staying with Living4Malaga!

EMERGENCY: For any emergency in Spain, call 112 (police, ambulance, fire). Operators speak English.
For anything about the apartment, call Living4Malaga on +34 951 196 922 or +34 951 204 240, or email info@living4malaga.com. Our office is at Calle Manuel José García Caparrós 2, Málaga.

LOCAL TIPS: El Balneario de los Baños del Carmen beach is just 200 metres away. It is a favourite with locals because of its history (it opened as a seaside spa in 1918) and its lovely sea views.
Walk along the Pablo Ruiz Picasso Promenade right in front of the beach.
The historic centre of Málaga is only about 10 minutes by bus, or about 40 minutes on foot along the coast.
```

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. Living4Malaga
mentions extra towels "for an additional charge" and late departures "with an additional charge" but

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| We land at 1 PM, can we get in early? | No, the apartment can't be accessed before 4:00 PM |
| We arrive at 9 PM, is that OK? | Yes, but contact Living4Malaga in advance (arrivals after 7 PM) |
| Do you change towels? | Not for stays under 7 nights; extra towels for a charge; weekly from 13 nights |
| Can we have a birthday party? | No parties of any kind |
| How far is the beach? | 200 m to Baños del Carmen |
| Wie kommen wir in die Altstadt? (German) | Answers in German: about 10 minutes by bus, or 40 minutes on foot |
| ¿Qué tenemos que hacer antes de salir? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: rubbish out, dishes washed, keys where indicated, before 12:00 |
| Is there parking? | Doesn't invent anything; hands over to the host |
| What's the key-safe code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to German) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the early-arrival answer, the German answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up.
