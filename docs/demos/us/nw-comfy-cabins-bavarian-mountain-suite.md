# Demo guide: NW Comfy Cabins, Bavarian Mountain Suite (Leavenworth, WA)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of NW Comfy Cabins' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Bavarian Mountain Suite:** it's their most "Leavenworth" listing: a 2-bedroom main-floor condo two
blocks from Front Street and next door to the Oktoberfest Festhalle, so the demo shows festival, parking
and winter questions. The listing page is unusually detailed, and their new Rental Policies page gives
clear company-wide times and rules that apply to all 55+ homes.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- comfycabins.com/vacation-rentals/rental/BavarianMountain/ (description, "Important notes", amenities list)
- comfycabins.com/nw-comfy-cabins-rental-policies/ (check-in 4:00 PM, check-out 10:00 AM, early/late
  requests, quiet hours, fireworks, smoking, wildlife, winter driving)
- comfycabins.com/about-us/ (the "If our name is on the experience…" line, in-house services since 2018)
- comfycabins.com/faq/ (info@comfycabins.com) and the site footer (office address and phone)

Note: the site is **comfycabins.com** (nwcomfycabins.com doesn't resolve). Every fact below comes from
those pages. **Not verified / left out:** door code, Wi-Fi network and password, the exact street
address, office hours and an after-hours number (the FAQ answers load dynamically and couldn't be read).

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Bavarian Mountain Suite — Leavenworth`
   - **City, country:** `Leavenworth, Washington`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Bavarian Mountain Suite, a luxurious two-bedroom, two-bath condo in the very centre of Leavenworth, cared for by NW Comfy Cabins, a family-owned local company whose own team does the housekeeping, trash pickup and property care. Front Street is two blocks away and the Oktoberfest Festhalle is right next door.

ARRIVAL: Standard check-in is 4:00 PM Pacific Time. Check-in is contactless. Your door code and arrival details are sent in your arrival information. Please don't share the code.
Early check-in can be requested but isn't guaranteed. Requests are usually reviewed about 24 hours before arrival. Please don't arrive early without approval, as an unauthorised early arrival can be charged half a day's rate.
This is a main-floor condo.

WI-FI: Wireless internet is included and the condo is laptop friendly. The network name and password are in your arrival information.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests, including children. All overnight guests must be on the reservation.
No pets in this condo.
No smoking or vaping indoors, including marijuana.
No outdoor burning. Fireworks of any kind (including sparklers) are illegal in Chelan County and prohibited.
Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM.
No house parties, bachelor or bachelorette parties, or weddings, and no gatherings above the permitted occupancy.
Never approach or feed wildlife, and keep food and garbage secured.

THE SUITE: The living room has a gas fireplace, a large flat-screen TV with surround sound, Netflix and Blu-Ray, and a cabinet of classic games.
The kitchen has stainless steel appliances, a gas stove, a breakfast bar, a dishwasher, a microwave, a coffee maker, cookware and spices.
The primary bedroom has a king bed and its own bathroom with a tub and shower. The second bedroom has two sets of XL bunk beds (sleeps 4) next to the second full bathroom, also with a tub and shower.
Air conditioning, heating, washer and dryer, linens and towels are provided.
The covered deck has seating, a ceiling fan and a gas grill, plus a front-row view of Leavenworth's summer parades.
Guests also get exclusive access to the "Wall of Sleds", a fun bonus for snowy days.

PARKING: Free parking for 1 vehicle in the private garage. Please don't park on the street, on shoulders or at neighbouring properties unless we've authorised it. Tents and RVs aren't permitted.
In winter, come prepared for mountain travel: a winter-capable vehicle, traction devices you know how to fit, and extra time. Check the Stevens Pass and Blewett Pass reports before you drive.

CHECKOUT: Standard check-out is 10:00 AM Pacific Time. Thank you for staying with us!
Late check-out can be requested but isn't guaranteed. Requests are usually reviewed about 24 hours before departure. An unauthorised late departure can be charged half a day's rate.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the condo, call NW Comfy Cabins on (509) 763-0180 or email info@comfycabins.com. Please report any maintenance or safety issue promptly.
Use stairs, decks and icy paths with care in winter.

LOCAL TIPS: Distances from the suite:
- Next door: the Oktoberfest Festhalle
- 2 blocks: Front Street shops, restaurants and tasting rooms
- 2 blocks: Wenatchee Riverfront trails
- Walkable: the Christmas Lighting Festival
- About 5 miles: Leavenworth Golf Course
- About 30 miles: Stevens Pass Ski Resort
- About 55 miles: Mission Ridge Ski Resort
Leavenworth's festivals run all year, from Oktoberfest and Christmastown to Maifest and the Autumn Leaf Festival.
Summer ideas include whitewater rafting, hiking, fly fishing, paddle boarding, e-bike tours and the Leavenworth Summer Theater (July to September).
Winter ideas include skiing at Stevens Pass, cross-country skiing and snowshoeing, sledding, horse-drawn sleigh rides, the Leavenworth Reindeer Farm and snowmobile tours.
Ask us for local recommendations any time.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `4:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `10:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival information` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `(509) 763-0180` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 2 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples**: NW Comfy Cabins says early check-in and late check-out "may be requested" but
publishes no price, so tell them they set their own.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Early check-in (from 1:00 PM) | $50 | Start your Leavenworth weekend sooner. Confirmed about 24 hours before arrival, if the schedule allows. |
| Late check-out (until 12:00 PM) | $50 | One more slow Bavarian morning. Confirmed about 24 hours before departure, subject to availability. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | 4:00 PM Pacific; early check-in on request, reviewed ~24h before, not guaranteed |
| How many cars can we bring? | One, in the private garage; no street parking |
| Can we bring sparklers for the Fourth of July? | No: fireworks, including sparklers, are illegal in Chelan County |
| Can we bring our dog? | No pets in this condo |
| How far is Front Street? | Two blocks; the Festhalle is next door |
| Do we need chains to get there in January? | Come with a winter-capable vehicle and traction devices; check the pass reports |
| What time are quiet hours? | 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM |
| ¿Cuál es la contraseña del wifi? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: it's in your arrival information |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.
