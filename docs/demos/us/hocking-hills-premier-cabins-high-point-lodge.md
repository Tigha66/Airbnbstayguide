# Demo guide: Hocking Hills Premier Cabins, High Point Lodge (Rockbridge, OH)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Hocking Hills Premier Cabins' rentals *before*
contacting them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why High Point Lodge:** their flagship (sleeps 26, indoor saltwater pool, hot tub, fire ring), featured
in "17 Most Luxurious Cabin Rentals in America". Big groups plus a hot tub, pool, fire ring, well water,
septic and a steep driveway mean a lot of the same questions every weekend.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- hockinghillspremiercabins.com/High-Point-Lodge.html (description, beds, features, house rules, quiet hours, fees, driveway)
- hockinghillspremiercabins.com/pdf/HHPC-OR-Agreement-10.03.23.pdf (renter agreement dated 3 Oct 2023: check-in 4 PM for lodges / check-out 11 AM, checkout list, fires, hot tub, well water, septic, cell service, office hours)
- hockinghillspremiercabins.com/about-us.html (High Point Lodge is in Rockbridge, 3.8 miles from Rock House)
- hockinghillspremiercabins.com/spa-dining-activities.html (local ideas)

Every fact below comes from those pages. Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders.
**Check:** quiet hours are **10 PM–10 AM** on the property page but **10 PM–9 AM** in the 2023 agreement;
the kit uses the property page. The agreement is from 2023, so confirm times and fees are still current.
The site lists the lodge under Logan, but the About page places it in Rockbridge.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `High Point Lodge — Hocking Hills`
   - **City, country:** `Rockbridge, Ohio`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to High Point Lodge, the Premier choice for large gatherings in the Hocking Hills. This completely renovated 6,500 sq ft lodge, built around the original hand-hewn log cabin, sits in a secluded park-like setting atop a hill. Hocking Hills State Park and the area's attractions are only minutes away.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is at 4:00 PM. Access instructions are emailed to you late the night before you arrive. Please don't share them.
The guest named on the reservation must be present at check-in and for the whole stay.
Early check-in and late check-out usually aren't possible, because the lodge is so popular.
The driveway is steep. A four-wheel drive or front-wheel drive vehicle is needed in snowy months. We plow, but driveways can still be snowy and icy.
High Point Lodge is wheelchair friendly. Guests with special needs can park close to the front door and use the ramp.

WI-FI: Starlink satellite internet. It works well, but service isn't guaranteed. The network name and password are in your arrival instructions.
Cell phone service isn't guaranteed everywhere in the Hocking Hills. If the Wi-Fi has a problem, call our office.

HOUSE RULES: Sleeps up to 26 guests. Only guests listed on the reservation may stay, with no exceptions, and visitors need our approval.
No parties or large gatherings.
Quiet hours are 10 PM to 10 AM, strictly enforced.
No smoking or vaping inside. Smoking is allowed outside, so please use the cigarette receptacles.
No pets.
No fireworks, no shooting of any kind (including paintball, air and BB guns), no hunting and no ATVs.
No candles or open flames inside.
Please don't move furniture, inside or out.
Exterior security cameras cover the parking area. NoiseAware decibel-only monitors (no audio recording) are in the main gathering area and outside.

THE LODGE: Eight private bedrooms and a loft. The lower level has 1 king and 3 queen rooms. The main level has 2 king rooms, a double queen room and a double bunk room. The sleeping loft has 6 twin beds.
Five full bathrooms, including a jetted jacuzzi tub, and a main-level shower with a shower seat and grab bars.
Two well-equipped kitchens with stainless steel appliances, and a private dining room with a custom rustic table seating 20.
Two great rooms with wood-burning fireplaces. Fireplaces aren't for use from April 15 to September 1. Keep the damper open when the fire is lit.
Game room with a pool table, a 60-game arcade table and a bar, plus shuffleboard and a bowling table.
Air conditioning and gas heat. Smart TVs for streaming.
Bed linens and bath towels are provided. Please bring extra towels for the hot tub and pool, plus shampoo, a hair dryer and coffee, which aren't provided.
The lodge has well water, which may taste of minerals, so you may prefer bottled water. It's on a septic system, so please flush only toilet paper.

POOL: The heated indoor saltwater pool is in the detached pool house, with a bathroom, patio furniture and a full-size fridge. Open the sliding glass doors in warm months.
Never leave children unattended around the pool or hot tub. No glass near the water.

HOT TUB: The covered hot tub may not be fully heated when you arrive. It heats about 5 degrees per hour.
Shower before and after. No bubble bath, and keep the temperature at 104 degrees or below.
Each evening after using it, sprinkle one cup of the sanitizer (in the cabinet above the range), run the jets and close the cover.
Please don't sit or stand on the cover. A replacement costs $450.

YARD AND GRILLS: Enjoy the outdoor fire ring and the covered shelter with picnic tables. Kids love the large play set with swings, a slide, monkey bars and a fort. Bring your own firewood and starters. You may gather wood from the ground, but please don't cut any.
Fires only in the fire ring, and never leave a fire unattended. No cans, glass or plastic in the fire.
There are charcoal and propane grills. Bring your own charcoal, foil, starter fluid and lighters.

PARKING: Park in the lodge's parking area. Guests with special needs can park close to the front door.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
- Empty the fridge and wipe it clean.
- Leave the stove and oven clean.
- Load and run the dishwasher.
- Leave linens on the beds and towels in the bathrooms.
- Put all trash in tied bags in the outside containers, lids latched. Loose trash may cost a $75 cleaning fee.
- Set the thermostats to 60 if the heat is on, or 78 if the AC is on.
- Lock doors and windows and return the keys to the lockbox. Lost keys cost $25.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the lodge, call Hocking Hills Premier Cabins on 740-380-3242. The office is open 10 AM to 6 PM daily.
Report any equipment problem straight away and we'll fix it as soon as possible.

LOCAL TIPS: Rock House is about 3.8 miles away, and Hocking Hills State Park is just minutes from the lodge.
Rock climbing, rappelling, ziplining, canoeing with Hocking Valley Canoe Livery, and the Hocking Valley Scenic Railway are all close by.
For mountain biking try Tar Hollow, Great Seal, Lake Hope, Hocking Hills State Park or the Monday Creek trails in Wayne National Forest.
Hocking Hills Market has shops and dining. For a beer and a burger, try Home Tavern Bar & Grill, or the Boot Factory Grill at the Rocky Boot Outlet.
Treat yourself with Blue Valley Massage & Traveling Spa.
```

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| The hot tub is cold, is it broken? | Not fully heated on arrival; heats about 5 degrees per hour |
| Do we need to bring firewood? | Yes, bring your own; you may gather fallen wood, don't cut any |
| Can we use the fireplace in July? | No, fireplaces aren't used April 15 – September 1 |
| Do we need 4WD in January? | 4WD or front-wheel drive needed in snowy months; steep driveway |
| Can we set off fireworks? | No |
| Is the tap water OK to drink? | Well water, may contain minerals; you may prefer bottled water |
| What time are quiet hours? | 10 PM to 10 AM |
| ¿Hay toallas para la piscina? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: no, bring extra towels for pool and hot tub |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the access instructions / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** and the **Extras** tab.
