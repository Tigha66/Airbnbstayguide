# Demo guide: Vacay Lettings, Amazing Palm Marina Views 2BR in Iris Blue (Dubai Marina)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Vacay Lettings' apartments *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Iris Blue:** the first "top property" on their homepage, a 16th-floor, 2-bedroom, 1,400 sq ft
apartment for 5 guests in Iris Blue, right above Marina Walk. The listing and its house rules publish
nearly everything a Dubai guest asks: check-in and check-out times, passport copies for DTCM and
building security, the credit-card deposit hold, exact late-checkout fees, lost-card and smoking
fines, free covered parking and the building's pool, gym, jacuzzi and sauna.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): vacaylettings.com/listings/102254 ("Amazing Palm Marina
views metro walk to JBR beach": description, things to note, house rules, check-in 3 pm / check-out
12 pm, AED 2,000 refundable damage deposit in the booking widget) and vacaylettings.com (homepage and
footer: office 912F Ibn Battuta Gate Office Building, tel +971 4 334 9609, mobile +971 55 404 8793,
info@vacaylettings.com). Every fact below comes from those pages. **Caveats:** the "80 homes" figure in
INTERNATIONAL-PROSPECTS.md could not be re-confirmed (the homepage now shows about 15 featured
listings), so the email doesn't use it. The listing's public reviews are mixed (mould, deposit
disputes), so don't quote reviews. The listing mentions both an AED 2,000 smoking fine inside the unit
and AED 500–1,000 fines in non-smoking areas; both are kept as written. **Not published:** Tourism
Dirham amount, pool/gym hours, early check-in fee, Wi-Fi password, access details.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Iris Blue 2BR Marina View — Dubai Marina`
   - **City, country:** `Dubai Marina, Dubai, United Arab Emirates`
   - **Street address:** `Iris Blue, Dubai Marina` (building name as published)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to your 16th-floor, 2-bedroom apartment in Iris Blue, Dubai Marina, managed by Vacay Lettings. It's about 1,400 sq ft with floor-to-ceiling windows and a full 180-degree view over the Marina, the Palm and Jumeirah Beach. Iris Blue sits right on the waterfront next to the Grosvenor House Hotel, above Marina Walk.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 3:00 PM.
Our GRO (guest relations officer) is available for check-in, check-out and any help you need during your stay.
Your access cards, keys and arrival instructions are in your arrival message from Vacay Lettings. Please don't share them.
Guests under 18 must be with an adult who is staying in the apartment.

GUEST REGISTRATION: Please share a passport or ID copy for every guest staying, at least 48 hours before arrival. This is required by building security and by the Dubai Tourism Department (DTCM), because we are a licensed holiday home company.
Before check-in, please sign the Holiday Home Guest Check-in document. It sets out the terms of your stay.
A refundable security deposit (AED 2,000 for this apartment) is required before check-in. Pay it online with a credit card. For stays shorter than 21 days it's a card hold, released within 48 hours after check-out, and usually back in your account within 7 business days. The pre-authorisation link is sent 24 hours before arrival. Please don't use a debit card, because the amount will be charged instead of held.
Dubai's Tourism Dirham fee is charged per room per night. Ask us how it applies to your booking.

WI-FI: High-speed Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

THE APARTMENT: Two en-suite bedrooms, one with a king bed and one with twin beds, plus a couch in the living room for one more guest. Up to 5 guests.
The living and dining area has a large sofa, a 6-seat dining table, a smart TV with soundbar and cable channels.
The kitchen is fully equipped, with a coffee machine and dishwasher. There's a washer and dryer.
The spacious balcony looks over the Marina.
Housekeeping is every 2 weeks. Extra cleaning is available for a fee.
A baby cot is available for an additional fee.

POOL AND GYM: Iris Blue has a large swimming pool with marina views, a jacuzzi, sauna, modern gym, outdoor kids' play area, BBQ area and landscaped gardens, plus 24-hour security.
The building also offers laundry pick-up and drop-off.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 5 guests.
Strictly no smoking inside. A fine of AED 2,000 is charged for odour removal if any smoking smell is found. Smoking in other non-smoking areas can bring fines of AED 500 to AED 1,000.
No parties.
Please keep noise down, especially between 10:00 PM and 8:00 AM. Noise shouldn't be heard by neighbours inside their homes with windows and doors closed. Building management can issue fines, and these are passed on.
Please put rubbish in the garbage chute or the building's allocated bins, never in common areas.
Lost or damaged keys and access cards cost a minimum of AED 500.
This is a residential building. Please respect the amenities, building security and neighbours.
Please keep your valuables safe. We can't be held liable for loss or damage.

PARKING: A free covered parking spot is available. Please follow the building's parking rules and be considerate to other drivers.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is at 12:00 PM (noon). Thank you for staying with Vacay Lettings!
Late checkout costs AED 500 for the first hour after 12:00, then AED 1,000 for each additional hour after 1:00 PM.
Please return all access cards and keys in working condition. Your deposit hold is then released.

EMERGENCY: For police call 999. For an ambulance call 998.
For anything about the apartment, call Vacay Lettings on +971 4 334 9609 or mobile +971 55 404 8793, or email info@vacaylettings.com.

LOCAL TIPS: Marina Walk is right below the building, with boutiques, restaurants and groceries nearby.
JBR Beach is a 15 to 25 minute walk.
The Dubai Tram is a 15 to 20 minute walk and connects to the Dubai Metro.
The RTA water bus stops just behind the building on the marina canal.
You can hire e-bikes and e-scooters nearby.
```

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | From 3:00 PM; the GRO helps at check-in |
| Can I pay the deposit with a debit card? | No, use a credit card; it's a hold released 48h after check-out |
| How much is late checkout? | AED 500 for the first hour after 12:00, then AED 1,000 per hour |
| When do you need our passports? | Copies for every guest at least 48 hours before arrival |
| Is there a sauna? | Yes, plus pool, jacuzzi, gym and kids' play area |
| How do I get to the beach? | JBR Beach is a 15–25 minute walk |
| هل يوجد موقف سيارات مجاني؟ (Arabic: is there free parking?) | Answers in Arabic: yes, a free covered spot |
| Можно ли курить на балконе? (Russian: can I smoke on the balcony?) | Answers in Russian: no smoking inside; fines apply (doesn't invent a balcony rule, or hands over) |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Arabic) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the deposit answer, the Arabic answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
