# Demo guide: Sedona Premier, Sunrise Cliffs (Sedona, AZ)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Sedona Premier's homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Sunrise Cliffs:** a featured luxury home on their site (Soldier Pass area, Coffee Pot Rock views,
hot tub, fire pit, 3 king suites) with a detailed listing, a strict quiet-hours rule and a published STR
permit. Those are exactly the things guests ask about.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- sedonapremier.com/rentals/sunrise-cliffs-luxury-retreat-in-soldiers-pass (description, rules, STR permit, amenities, guest reviews)
- sedonapremier.com/terms-and-conditions (arrival 4:00 PM / departure 10:00 AM, emergency line, noise, wildlife, fridge tip)
- sedonapremier.com/property-management (the "24/7 guest support… curated guidebooks" line used in the email)
- sedonapremier.com/things-to-do/concierge (in-home massage and private chef)

Every fact below comes from those pages. Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders.
**Check:** quiet hours are **9 PM–7 AM** on the property page but **9 PM–8 AM** in the Terms; the kit
uses the property page. The property page says no smoking anywhere on the property (stricter than the
Terms), so the kit uses that. The Soldier Pass "half-mile walk" comes from guest reviews on their page.
**STR Permit #010162** is shown on the property page.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Sunrise Cliffs — Sedona`
   - **City, country:** `Sedona, Arizona`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Sunrise Cliffs, a 3,200 sq ft retreat in a peaceful cul-de-sac with breathtaking views of Coffee Pot Rock, managed by Sedona Premier. Sip coffee on the deck at sunrise, gather around the fire pit, and soak in the six-person hot tub under a sky full of stars. Sedona STR Permit #010162.

ARRIVAL: The home is ready for you from 4:00 PM. Self check-in. Your door code is sent in your arrival message from Sedona Premier. Please don't share it.
Early check-in may be possible, subject to availability and possibly a fee. Ask us and we'll check.
You have full access to the home, except for the locked supply closets.

WI-FI: High-speed Wi-Fi throughout, with a private office and desk if you need to work. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests. Only the people listed on the reservation may stay.
Quiet hours are strictly 9 PM to 7 AM. This is a quiet residential neighborhood and the city enforces quiet hours.
Parties are strictly prohibited. Excessive noise can lead to a $500 fee or the end of your stay without a refund.
Absolutely no smoking anywhere on the property.
Pets are not allowed in this home.
Please don't leave doors or windows open in bad weather or while the heating or air conditioning is on.

THE HOME: Three private king suites, each with an en-suite bathroom. The primary suite has a private patio, a deep soaking tub and a walk-in shower. The third suite has its own entrance to a secluded patio.
Gourmet kitchen with an electric range, double oven and French door fridge. Dining table for six, plus island seating for four.
Living room with a stone fireplace, 65-inch Smart TV and Sonos surround sound.
A new fridge full of groceries can take up to 24 hours to cool down again. If it's still warm after 24 hours, let us know.

HOT TUB: The six-person hot tub faces the red rocks. The custom fire pit is on the front patio, and there's a high-end gas BBQ for grilling.
Please use the fire pit and fireplace only in line with current local fire rules.

PARKING: Free private parking at the home.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
Please put all trash in the bins provided, close the windows and lock up.
Late check-out may be possible, subject to availability and possibly a fee. Ask us the day before.

EMERGENCY: For any life-threatening emergency, call 911.
Sedona Premier's emergency line is (928) 399-7888, 24/7, for gas leaks, fire, lockouts and whole-house power outages.
For anything else (hot tub, appliances, TV, internet), message us through your booking channel during business hours.
Sedona is desert country. Javelina, coyotes, bobcats, snakes and scorpions live here, so give wildlife space and watch where you step at night.

LOCAL TIPS: Soldier Pass Trail is about a half-mile walk from the house, guests tell us. Sunrise Trail is close by too.
Restaurants, shops, groceries and more hiking are just minutes away by car, and it's easy to get a ride to dinner.
After booking, we can arrange an in-home massage or a private chef dinner for you.
You'll also get our curated list of Sedona's best hiking trails, restaurants and must-see sights.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `4:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `10:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `(928) 399-7888` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples**: Sedona Premier only says early/late "may be subject to a fee" and doesn't publish
massage prices, so tell them they set their own.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Early check-in (from 1:00 PM) | $75 | More time on the trails on arrival day. Subject to availability. |
| Late check-out (until 12:00 PM) | $75 | One more slow red-rock morning. Subject to availability. |
| In-home massage (60 min) | $150 | A professional local massage therapist comes to the house. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Can we have a birthday party here? | No, parties are strictly prohibited |
| Can I smoke on the patio? | No smoking anywhere on the property |
| Is there a trail we can walk to? | Soldier Pass, about a half-mile walk |
| The hot tub isn't heating, who do I call? | Not an emergency: message via the booking channel during business hours |
| We're locked out at midnight! | Emergency line (928) 399-7888, 24/7 |
| Can you book us a massage? | Yes, in-home massage can be arranged |
| ¿A qué hora es el check-out? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: by 10 AM |

Also try **switching the guide's language** and the **Extras** tab.
