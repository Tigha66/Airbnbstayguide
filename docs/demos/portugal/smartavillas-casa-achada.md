# Demo guide: Smartavillas, Casa Achada (Fonte Salgada, Tavira)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Smartavillas' villas *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Casa Achada:** a 3-suite countryside villa with a private pool, 5 km outside Tavira. It's a
typical East Algarve rental (car needed, beach by ferry, rural sounds) and its listing page has
everything: check-in window, late-arrival fees, AL number, parking, pool size, food packs, and the
24-hour phone line. That makes the guide rich, and the questions are the ones Rachel's team gets
every week.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- booking.smartavillas.com/rentals/villa-tavira-casa-achada-fonte-salgada-725547.html (description, 3 king ensuites, private 8 m x 4 m pool, parking for 4, 6 km to beach, AL number 175465/AL, check-in 16:00–19:00, check-out before 10:00, no smoking, no pets, no groups of young people up to 24, food packs, and the 2026 booking terms shown on the same page: late check-in, early check-in and late checkout fees, checkout requirements, AIMA, 24-hour line +351 916 359 710)
- smartavillas.com/faqs/ (check-in, cots and highchairs, what's included, pool supervision, EV charging, rural wildlife)
- smartavillas.com/guest-services/ (airport transfers, private chef, Smarta+ vouchers)
- smartavillas.com/about-us/ (Rachel Robinson; "When Covid hit, we kept every one of our 16 employees")

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Conflicts and gaps:** the FAQ quotes fees in pounds (£30,
£50) while the 2026 terms quote euros (€30, €50); the kit uses the euros from the terms. The listing
page showed us prices in pounds (the site converts by visitor location). Several FAQ answers on
smartavillas.com/faqs/ are pasted under the wrong questions (for example "Is there a tourist tax?"
shows the smoking answer), which is worth mentioning gently. Maximum guest count isn't stated in
text (3 king bedrooms). Tavira isn't in their tourist tax table, so the manual just says they'll
tell you if one applies. Key safe codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Casa Achada — Tavira`
   - **City, country:** `Tavira, Portugal`
   - **Street address:** `Fonte Salgada, 8800-602 Tavira` (the hamlet and postcode on their listing)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Casa Achada, a peaceful countryside villa in the small hamlet of Fonte Salgada, just 5 km outside Tavira, managed by Smartavillas. It has three colourful en-suite bedrooms, wooden ceilings and exposed beams, a private pool and green, shady grounds. Tavira's restaurants, historic streets and the ferry to the beach are about 5 minutes away by car or Uber. Accommodation registration number 175465/AL.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM. One of our Property Guest Managers will welcome you whenever possible. Before you arrive we'll send your full check-in instructions and key collection details.
Arriving between 7:00 PM and 10:00 PM? An in-person check-in costs €30, or we can give you key safe codes for self-entry where available.
Arriving after 10:00 PM? We'll give you key safe codes where available, or direct you to our key collection box at our Tavira office. An in-person check-in after 10:00 PM costs €50.
Early check-in can sometimes be arranged for €30. A free bag drop may be possible while the villa is being prepared. Please ask in advance.
Any key safe code is in your arrival message. Please don't share it.

GUEST REGISTRATION: Portuguese law requires us to send the passport details of all non-Portuguese guests to AIMA, the borders authority. Please complete our online check-in before your arrival date. It's quick and secure.
Some East Algarve councils charge a tourist tax. If one applies to your stay, we'll tell you when you book.
Before your stay our partner Truvi will contact the lead guest to verify your details and let you choose a refundable damage deposit or a damage waiver.

WI-FI: Wi-Fi is included for standard household use. The network name and password are in your arrival message.
Signal strength can vary from room to room in the countryside. If you need a reliable connection for work, let us know.
There's one TV with satellite channels.

HOUSE RULES: Only the guests on your booking may stay, and the maximum occupancy in your booking must not be exceeded.
No smoking or vaping inside the villa.
No pets.
This villa doesn't accept groups of young people (up to 24 years).
Please be considerate and keep noise down. We can end a booking for behaviour that causes danger, distress or annoyance to others.
Don't charge an electric car from the villa's normal sockets. It isn't allowed for safety reasons.
You're in the Algarve countryside, so you may hear cicadas, frogs, cockerels and other wildlife. That's part of the charm!

THE VILLA: Three en-suite bedrooms, each with a king-size bed and its own shower room. Two bedrooms are downstairs with no steps, and the upstairs suite has lovely countryside views.
The open-plan lounge has big sofas and flows into the dining area, with a large wooden table overlooking the pool. There's also a fireplace.
Air conditioning in all rooms, plus a heat pump.
The kitchen (gas) has a fridge, freezer, oven, microwave, dishwasher, coffee machine, toaster and kettle.
Bed linen and towels are included, plus pool towels where available. On arrival you'll find complimentary bottled water and wine, a kitchen starter pack and some toilet paper.
A travel cot and highchair are free on request. Please ask before you arrive.

POOL: The private pool is 8 m by 4 m, surrounded by sunbeds, mature trees and a shaded al fresco dining area with a BBQ.
Children must be supervised by an adult at all times in the pool.

PARKING: The villa is in private grounds with open-air parking for several cars (4 spaces).
You'll want a car, taxi or Uber here. Tavira town centre is about 5.7 km away.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM. A late checkout can sometimes be arranged for €30. Please ask in advance.
Before you leave, please follow these steps.
- Put all rubbish in the roadside containers or recycling points.
- Clean the BBQ if you used it.
- Switch off all air conditioning units.
- Close all parasols, shutters, doors and gates.
- Return all keys. Lost keys and locksmith costs are charged to the guest.
Thank you for staying with us!

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 112 (the European emergency number).
For anything about the villa, our 24-hour guest line is +351 916 359 710. During office hours you can also call +351 281 027 089 or email reservas@smartavillas.com.
If something isn't right, please tell us straight away rather than after checkout, so we can fix it.

LOCAL TIPS: A short walk from the house there's a local taberna serving good home-cooked food. Perfect for a lazy lunch or a simple dinner.
For supermarkets, shops and more restaurants, Tavira town centre is a few minutes' drive.
The nearest sandy beach is about 6 km away. From Tavira you can take the ferry boat across to the beach.
We can arrange private airport transfers, car hire with pick-up at Faro Airport, food packs (breakfast basket, fruit, a traditional Portuguese treat box) and a private chef. Just ask.
Book direct and you get Smarta+ vouchers for partner restaurants, surf lessons, boat trips, tours and wellness around Tavira. They're valid until checkout.
```

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. Early
check-in and late checkout use **their published €30 fees**; the breakfast basket is shown on their
listing at £33.89, so €40 is a **demo approximation**. Tell them they set their own prices.

Open the link on your phone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| How far is the beach? | About 6 km; ferry from Tavira to the beach |
| Do we need a car? | Yes, car, taxi or Uber; Tavira centre about 5.7 km; parking for 4 |
| Can I charge my electric car? | Not from the normal sockets |
| What do we do before we leave? | Rubbish to roadside bins, clean BBQ, A/C off, close shutters and gates, return keys, out by 10:00 |
| Can we get a cot? | Yes, free on request |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Portuguese) and the **Extras** tab.
