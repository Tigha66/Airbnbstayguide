# Demo guide: Coast 'N Currents Realty, Captain's Cove (Nags Head, NC)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Coast 'N Currents Realty's homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Captain's Cove:** a 7-bedroom, 5.5-bath oceanfront house in Nags Head (their home town) that sleeps 14,
with a private saltwater pool, hot tub, game room and theater room. It's exactly the kind of large group home
Coast 'N Currents is known for, and big groups ask the most questions (pool dates, linens, grills, hot tub
rules for kids, late checkout). Their company-wide rental policy fills in everything the listing doesn't say.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): the property page coastncurrentsobx.com/rentals/captains-cove,
the rental policy at coastncurrentsobx.com/rental-policy, the FAQ at coastncurrentsobx.com/faqs,
coastncurrentsobx.com/what-to-bring, coastncurrentsobx.com/planning-your-trip and coastncurrentsobx.com/about-us.
Every fact below comes from those pages. Things only the host knows (door code, Wi-Fi name and password,
trash-day details) are left as placeholders, and you should say so in the email. Not verified: driving
distances from the house (none are published), so Local tips stay general.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Captain's Cove — Nags Head`
   - **City, country:** `Nags Head, North Carolina`
   - **Street address:** `9317 S Old Oregon Inlet Rd, Nags Head, NC` (published on their listing).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Captain's Cove, a 7-bedroom oceanfront home in Nags Head with sunrises over the Atlantic, a private saltwater pool, a hot tub and direct access to miles of beach just steps from the door. It's cared for by the local team at Coast 'N Currents Realty, and we're so glad your group is here.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 4:00 PM on your arrival day, and our policy asks you to arrive no later than 6:00 PM. The home has a keyless lock, and your door code and entry instructions are sent before your stay.
Please don't go onto the property or park at the house before 4:00 PM unless you've booked early check-in. It delays the cleaning and inspection, and ultimately your check-in.
We recommend doing your grocery shopping after you've checked in.
If your code doesn't work, call or text 252-256-7296 and we'll get you in.
Please report any damage or problems within 24 hours of arrival so you aren't held responsible for them.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi throughout the home. The network name and password are in your arrival message.
If the internet goes down, tech support is only available from the local internet provider's help line.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 14 guests. Occupancy limits are set by local law and the home's septic permit.
No smoking inside the home or anywhere on the property.
No pets. This is not a pet-friendly home.
No grilling on the wooden decks. Please use the gas grill away from the decks, and clean it after each use. If you exchange the propane bottle, keep the receipt and we'll reimburse you.
No tents, campers, RVs or motor homes on the property.
No large parties or events without prior approval.
Please don't rearrange the furniture, and don't open the owner's locked closet.
For your information, a video-only security camera (audio disabled) overlooks the driveway and beach access. There are no other cameras inside or outside the home.

THE HOUSE: Seven bedrooms sleep 14 (three kings, three queens and one room with two full beds), with five full baths and one half bath.
On the top level you'll find the kitchen, dining area, breakfast table for four, the main living room with a smart TV, a half bath and a king bedroom with its own bathroom.
The mid level has a quiet office with a desk (great for remote work or school), two king bedrooms with private baths, two queen bedrooms, a hall bath and a laundry room with two washers and two dryers.
The ground level has the game room (pool table, Ms. PacMan/Galaga arcade game, TV, wet bar and fridge), the theater room, a queen bedroom, a bedroom with two full beds and a hall bath.
There are 10 TVs, plus books, games and puzzles. A high chair and pack and play are available for little ones.
Beds have pillows, comforters and mattress pads. Sheets and towels are not included unless you booked the linen package.
Your starter kit has paper towels, a roll of toilet paper per full bath, dish soap, a sponge, a laundry pod, hand soap and a trash bag. Anything beyond that (paper goods, detergent, foil, seasonings) you'll need to bring or buy.

POOL AND HOT TUB: The 14 x 28 private saltwater pool is fenced and open from May 15 to October 15. Pools and hot tubs are serviced on turnover day and may not be ready until 4:00 PM.
Babies and toddlers in diapers must wear proper swim diapers in the pool.
Children under 5 should not use the hot tub, and children under 12 need adult supervision.
A mild natural ground current is occasionally felt in some Outer Banks pools. If anyone in your group has a pacemaker or similar device, please take care.

OCEANFRONT: The beach is right outside, with direct access from the house. Beach chairs, a beach umbrella and a beach cart are provided. Rinse off at the outdoor shower before heading inside.

PARKING: Please don't park at the house before 4:00 PM on arrival day unless you've booked early check-in. Some Outer Banks communities don't allow parking on the street, so please use the driveway.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM (or noon if you booked late checkout). After that, guests can't re-enter the house.
Please clean the grill, take out all your trash (including any cigarette butts outside), and leave the home clean and in good order.
Put any moved furniture back where it was.
Detailed checkout instructions are sent to you by email and text before you leave.
Check carefully for belongings. Items left behind are held for ten days and can be shipped to you for the shipping cost plus a $25 handling fee.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
Our team is available 24/7 for emergencies and urgent maintenance. Call or text 252-256-7296.
For anything that isn't urgent, please contact us during office hours and we'll handle it the next morning.
Smoke detectors and fire extinguishers are in the home.
Our office is at 3324 S Virginia Dare Trail, Nags Head. Office phone 855-629-7829.

LOCAL TIPS: South Nags Head has that classic Outer Banks feel, with wide, uncrowded beaches.
Fish from one of the area's piers or from the surf, or book an offshore fishing trip.
Kayak on the sound, explore nature trails, or go horseback riding on the beach.
Head north to Corolla to see the wild horses, or visit the charming waterfront in Manteo.
Fresh-caught seafood, waterfront dining and locally owned shops are all close by.
Beach chairs and umbrellas can also be rented from several local companies if your group needs more.
For local events, see outerbanks.org/events.
Please don't have mail sent to the house. Send it to your name and reservation number, c/o Coast N Currents Realty, 3324 S. Virginia Dare Trail, Nags Head, NC 27959, and we'll call you when it arrives.
```
