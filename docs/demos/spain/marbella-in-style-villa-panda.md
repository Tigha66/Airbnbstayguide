# Demo guide: Marbella in Style, Villa Panda (Sierra Blanca, Marbella, Spain)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Marbella in Style's villas *before* contacting them,
so the first message says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Villa Panda:** a 4-bedroom villa for 8 with a heated pool and sea views in the gated Sierra Blanca
community, and one of the few Marbella in Style listings that publishes check-in and check-out times,
the deposit and the exact location. It's pet-friendly and family-friendly, and Marbella in Style already
sells chefs, transfers, BBQ parties and tours, so the Extras tab tells a strong story.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
stay.marbellainstyle.com/en/5046251/villa-panda---heated-pool-sea-views-in-sierra-blanca (their
Lodgify booking page: 8 guests, check-in 4:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM, pets and children welcome, events
not allowed, €1,500 deposit, location "Urb. Cascada de Camojan, 9, Marbella"),
marbellainstyle.com/services/villas/villa-panda-sea-views-heated-pool-in-sierra-blanca/ (bedrooms,
features, private covered parking, "Malaga Airport just 40 minutes away", hospitality services) and
marbellainstyle.com/contact-us/ (+34 951 983 679, WhatsApp; office Mon–Sat 9:30–18:30 Madrid time).
Every fact below comes from those pages. **Conflicts / unverified:** bathrooms are given as 3 (Lodgify
header), 4 (Lodgify text) and 3.5 (main site), so the manual says 3.5; the booking page says the airport
is 45 km, the main site says 40 minutes; no tourist licence, no smoking rule and no transfer prices are
published. The gate/door codes and Wi-Fi password are placeholders.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Villa Panda — Sierra Blanca, Marbella`
   - **City, country:** `Marbella, Spain`
   - **Street address:** `Urb. Cascada de Camojan, 9, Marbella` (published on their booking page)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

