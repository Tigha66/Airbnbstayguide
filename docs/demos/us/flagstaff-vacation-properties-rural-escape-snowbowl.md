# Demo guide: Flagstaff Vacation Properties, Rural Escape Near Snowbowl (Flagstaff, AZ)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Flagstaff Vacation Properties' homes *before*
contacting them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Rural Escape Near Snowbowl:** it's the clearest "mountain cabin" listing in their 47-home
portfolio — a single-level home on 1.8 acres right on the Snowbowl road, with a fire pit, self
check-in and real Flagstaff winter weather to plan around. That snow/cabin angle is a strong hook
for a family-owned, hands-on local manager, and the same manual → guide approach works for all 47
of their homes.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): flagstaffvacationproperties.com (homepage, the
Properties list, the property page itself, and The Team page) and the property's own Airbnb
listing. Every fact below comes from those pages. Check-in/check-out times, a full house-rules
list and the pet policy are not published anywhere for this home, so those are left for the host
to confirm — and the email says so. Things only the host knows (door code, Wi-Fi password) are
left as placeholders.

---

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Rural Escape Near Snowbowl`
   - **City, country:** `Flagstaff, Arizona`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Rural Escape Near Snowbowl, a bright single-level home on 1.8 acres of open meadow in Baderville, just outside Flagstaff and close to the Snowbowl road, managed by the local, family-owned team at Flagstaff Vacation Properties. San Francisco Peaks views fill the windows, and downtown Flagstaff is a short drive away.

ARRIVAL: This is a self check-in home, so there is no one to meet at the door. Your check-in time is in your booking confirmation. Your door code and any other arrival details are sent in your arrival message from Flagstaff Vacation Properties. Please don't share it.
The home sits a few minutes outside town on the Snowbowl road, which is handy for an early start on ski days, but it does mean a longer drive than a downtown address, especially after dark or in bad weather.

WI-FI: Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: This home sleeps up to 6 guests, in a king bedroom, a queen bedroom and a bunk room with two queen beds.
There are exterior security cameras on the property covering the outside areas. They do not record inside the home.
The listing does not say whether pets are welcome here, so please check with us before bringing an animal.
Please treat the home and the quiet meadow setting around it with care, and leave it as you found it.

THE HOME: The vaulted-ceiling living room has an electric-recliner sectional and a media nook, and the eat-in kitchen has a picture window that frames the mountain. The kitchen is fully equipped for cooking, and there is a washer and dryer for longer stays.
Outside, a multi-level deck has a BBQ grill, an outdoor dining table and a stone fire pit with Adirondack chairs on a paver patio, good for evenings under big skies.

PARKING: Free parking is included, with a two-car garage and driveway space right at the home. No permits are needed.

SNOW AND WINTER: Flagstaff gets real mountain snow, and roads out here near Snowbowl can be icy or snow-packed from November through April. Please drive slowly, leave extra following distance, and use a low gear on hills.
If the driveway or walkway is icy, take it slowly and wear boots with good grip. Let us know if snow or ice right at the home ever needs clearing.

CHECKOUT: Your check-out time is in your booking confirmation. Before you leave, please make sure the outdoor fire pit is completely out and cold, turn off the lights, close up the home, and lock the door with the self check-in lock. Thank you for staying with Flagstaff Vacation Properties.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911. For anything about the home itself, call or text Flagstaff Vacation Properties on 928-224-9155.

LOCAL TIPS: The home is right on the Snowbowl road in Baderville, so it works well if you want an early start for first chair on a ski day at Arizona Snowbowl.
Downtown Flagstaff, with its restaurants, shops and Route 66 history, is a short drive away.
Flagstaff sits at around 7,000 feet, so give yourself a little time to adjust if you have come from lower elevation, and drink plenty of water.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `Confirm` |
| Check-out | `Confirm` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `928-224-9155` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The
prices are **demo examples**: Flagstaff Vacation Properties doesn't publish fees for these on their

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | It's a self check-in home; the time is in your booking confirmation |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message, hands over to the host |
| How many people can stay here? | Up to 6 guests (king, queen, and a bunk room with two queen beds) |
| Is this place pet-friendly? | Not confirmed; check with the host before bringing an animal |
| Where do we park? | Free parking, two-car garage and driveway, no permits needed |
| Do you have security cameras? | Yes, exterior only; they don't record inside the home |
| Is it safe to drive here in winter? | Roads near Snowbowl can be icy Nov–Apr; drive slowly, use a low gear on hills |
| ¿Cuál es la contraseña del wifi? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: it's in your arrival message |
| What's nearby for skiing? | Right on the Snowbowl road, handy for first chair at Arizona Snowbowl |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the winter-driving answer, the Spanish answer, the Extras tab) to use in
