# Demo guide: Málaga Sun Apartments, Central Free Parking Rooftop Pool (Málaga, Spain)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Málaga Sun Apartments' flats *before* contacting
them, so the first message says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why this apartment:** "-MalagaSunApts- Central\*FreeParking\*RooftopPool" is their largest city-centre
flat (3 bedrooms, 6 guests) and its listing answers almost every classic Málaga question: check-in
window and late-arrival surcharges, pool opening hours, free garage with a lift, air conditioning,
airport transfer, ID registration and quiet hours. Their team answers guests 9:00–21:00 (the footer
says 9:00–22:00), so the concierge covering the night is an easy story.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
malagasunapartments.com/en/rentals/apartment-malaga-malagasunapts-central-freeparking-rooftoppool-405765.html
and the Spanish version malagasunapartments.com/alquiler/apartamento-malaga-malagasunapts-central-freeparking-rooftoppool-405765.html
(description, "Important" notes, check-in/out times, deposit, cleaning fee, distances), plus
malagasunapartments.com/en/about-us/ (founded 2017). Every fact below comes from those pages.
**Conflicts / unverified:** the description says 110 m², the details say 100 m²; contact hours are
"09:00 to 21:00" in the listing but "09.00 a 22.00" in the footer; the listing shows tourist
registration **VUT/MA/48928** but the Registro Único number ends in **VUT/MA/577406**, so the licence is
left out of the manual (ask them). Key collection is arranged with the agency before arrival; no street
address of the flat is published (only postcode 29012). Door codes, key details and the Wi-Fi password
are placeholders.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Central Apartment with Pool & Free Parking — Málaga`
   - **City, country:** `Málaga, Spain`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (only the postcode 29012 is published).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.
