# Demo guide: York Boutique Lets, Minster's Keep (York)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of York Boutique Lets' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Minster's Keep:** a 2-bedroom, sleeps-6 apartment on Goodramgate with Minster views from the
kitchen, seconds from the Shambles, rated 4.9 from 106 reviews on their own site. It has the classic
York city-centre questions: a private parking space (rare inside the walls), "can we drop our bags?",
"where's the Wi-Fi password?" and a paid late checkout.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
yorkboutiquelets.co.uk/properties/minster-s-keep-stylish-apartment-near-york-minster (description,
beds, kitchen, parking space, Wi-Fi, location), yorkboutiquelets.co.uk/faqs (check-in 3pm–4pm by
property, check-out 10am, late checkout 12pm for £25, early check-in on the day, no luggage storage /
Yorbag, starter supplies, key-safe arrival, Wi-Fi details on the router or in the black welcome
folder, reporting issues within 24 hours, extras packages, "within 1 hour" enquiry promise),
yorkboutiquelets.co.uk/legal/terms (house rules, maximum occupancy, quiet hours; marked "Draft") and
yorkboutiquelets.co.uk/about (team of 7; Laura and Dean, Directors). **Not published:** this
apartment's exact check-in time (3pm or 4pm), pets and smoking rules, specific quiet hours, the
key-safe code and the car park access details. The contact email is published (obfuscated) on the
site as bookings@yorkboutiquelets.co.uk.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Minster's Keep — York`
   - **City, country:** `York, United Kingdom`
   - **Street address:** `Goodramgate, York` (street named on their listing; no number published)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Minster's Keep, a stylish two-bedroom apartment on Goodramgate in the very heart of historic York, looked after by York Boutique Lets. It sleeps up to six, and from the kitchen window you can see York Minster. The Shambles is just seconds away.

ARRIVAL: Your check-in time is in your booking confirmation. Check-in times are between 3:00 PM and 4:00 PM, depending on the property.
Once your payment is complete, we send you detailed arrival instructions, including how to get in using the key safe. Please don't share the code.
Early check-in can only be confirmed on the day. Call us on 01904 765531 on your arrival day and we'll tell you the earliest time.
We can't store luggage at the apartment. Yorbag, a luggage shop in York, will look after your bags for a small hourly charge.

WI-FI: Free super-fast Wi-Fi. You'll find the network name and password on the router itself or in the black welcome folder in the apartment.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests.
Please respect the quiet hours. This is a residential building in the city centre, so keep noise down in the evenings and on the stairs.
We may charge for damage or for extra cleaning if needed.
If anything isn't right, please tell us within 24 hours of arrival so we can put it right quickly.

THE APARTMENT: Two king-size bedrooms. The master bedroom has its own en-suite shower room, and the family bathroom has a large bath with an overhead shower.
The living room has a double sofa bed, seating for six and a large smart TV.
Fully equipped kitchen with dishwasher, washer-dryer, cookware and utensils, and a dining table for six.
Hotel-quality bed linen and towels are provided for all the beds and the sofa bed.
There's a starter stock of tea, coffee, sugar, toilet rolls, handwash, washing-up liquid, cleaning spray and cloths. The apartment is self-catering, so we can't top these up during your stay.

PARKING: The apartment comes with a private parking space for one car in a secure car park, a rare thing in central York. Access details are in your arrival instructions.
York is compact and most of the sights are a short walk away, so you can leave the car parked for your whole stay.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM.
A late checkout until 12:00 PM costs £25. It depends on the property and on availability, so call the office to ask.
Please leave the keys as described in your arrival instructions and make sure the door is locked. Thank you for staying with York Boutique Lets!

EMERGENCY: In an emergency, call 999 for fire, police or ambulance. For urgent medical advice that isn't an emergency, call NHS 111.
For anything about the apartment, call York Boutique Lets on 01904 765531 or email bookings@yorkboutiquelets.co.uk. We're a small local team, so you'll always speak to someone who knows York.

LOCAL TIPS: Everything here is a short walk from your door.
- The Shambles is seconds away, leading on to Whip-Ma-Whop-Ma-Gate, York's shortest street.
- York Minster is just up Goodramgate.
- The Theatre Royal, Bettys Café Tea Rooms and the Yorkshire Museum are all within a short walk.
- Fossgate, often called "Restaurant Street", is nearby, with a great choice of international restaurants.
We can arrange birthday packages, romance packages and hen party decorations. Just ask before you arrive.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `Confirm` (3pm–4pm depending on property; not stated for this flat) |
| Check-out | `10:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `On the router / in the black welcome folder` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `01904 765531` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. Late
checkout is **their real published price** (£25); the other two are **demo examples** (their FAQ
mentions birthday and romance packages without prices), so tell them they set their own prices.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Late checkout (until 12:00 PM) | £25 | A slower last morning in York. Subject to availability; we'll confirm. |
| Romance package | £45 | Prosecco, chocolates and petals waiting when you arrive. |
| Birthday package | £40 | Balloons, banner and a cake from a local bakery, set up before you arrive. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.
