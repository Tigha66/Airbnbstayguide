# Demo guide: Your Lake Vacation, The Knolls Resort (Osage Beach, MO)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Your Lake Vacation's condos *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why The Knolls Resort:** their website lists 17 Knolls condos (Knolls 0123, 0133, 0213 … 1433) on one
wooded peninsula in Osage Beach, all sharing the same resort amenities and the same company-wide rental
policies. The demo uses **Knolls 0123** (2 bedrooms, 2 baths, sleeps 6, waterfront on the main channel),
but most of the guide fits every Knolls unit, so it's the most convincing demo.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- https://www.yourlakevacation.com/vacation-rentals/rental/knolls_0123/ (unit description and amenity list)
- https://www.yourlakevacation.com/search-by-complex/knolls-resort-rentals/ and
  https://www.yourlakevacation.com/search-by-complex/knolls-resort-rentals/amenities/ (resort amenities)
- https://www.yourlakevacation.com/rental-policies/ (check-in/out, checkout steps, fireplaces, smoking,
  pets, parking, emergencies, rules)
- https://www.yourlakevacation.com/vacation-rental-faqs/ (keyless entry, Wi-Fi, boat launch, early/late
  requests, ages, "paperless, keyless and stress-less")
- https://www.yourlakevacation.com/lake-area-attractions/ and https://www.yourlakevacation.com/about-us/

Every fact below comes from those pages. Things only the host knows (the 4-digit door code, Wi-Fi name and
password, the exact unit address) are left out or as placeholders, and you should say so in the email.

Not verified / caveats: hot tub, pool and game-room opening hours are not published (the manual says to
follow the posted Knolls rules). The site's FAQ says "do NOT drive your vehicle with the trailer into any
condominium complexes", while the Knolls page lists a boat launch and trailer parking on site; the manual
keeps both and points guests to the directions in their confirmation. The FAQ and the policies page also
give different pet fees and damage deposits; the manual uses only the "no pets in condos" rule, which both
agree on. No drive times are published, so local tips have no distances.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Knolls 0123 — The Knolls Resort, Osage Beach`
   - **City, country:** `Osage Beach, Missouri`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Knolls 0123, a recently updated 2-bedroom, 2-bath waterfront condo at The Knolls Resort, on its own wooded peninsula in Osage Beach. Your deck looks out over the main channel of Lake of the Ozarks (around the 22 mile marker), and the resort's pools, hot tub and game room are just a short walk away. Your stay is managed by the local, family-owned team at Your Lake Vacation.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is between 3:00 PM and 4:00 PM on your arrival date.
All our properties are keyless. On the day of your arrival we send you your 4-digit entry code as soon as the condo is ready. There is no need to visit our office. Drive straight to the condo and enter the code on the keypad by the door.
Directions and the property address are on the second page of your email confirmation.
There are about 20 steps down and 15 steps up to the entrance, so pack bags you can carry.
Early check-in can be requested before your arrival date. It depends on the housekeeping schedule, so please call us to confirm 1 day before you arrive.
Please check the condo when you arrive and report anything that needs fixing to our office straight away.

WI-FI: The condo has wireless internet. The network name and password are in your arrival information. Internet and cell service are generally good around the lake, though some spots can be patchy. There is no landline phone.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests. Because of fire codes this can't be exceeded.
The person renting must be at least 25 years old, and young adults under 25 must be with a parent or guardian for the whole stay.
Absolutely no smoking inside. If you smoke outside on the deck, keep the doors and windows closed and never throw cigarette butts off the balcony.
No pets. All our condos have a strict no-pet policy, and a $200 fee applies if evidence of a pet is found.
Fireworks, kegs and illegal substances are strictly prohibited.
The fireplace in this condo is electric and you're welcome to use it. Wood-burning and gas fireplaces in our properties are for owners only.
Locked closets and tubs belong to the owner and are not part of your rental.
Please respect your neighbors and follow the Knolls condominium rules posted on the refrigerator and in your digital welcome book.

THE CONDO: The master suite has a king bed, its own bathroom, a TV with DVD player and direct access to the lakefront deck.
The second bedroom has two twin beds and a TV with cable. A queen air mattress is available for extra sleeping.
Both bathrooms have a tub and shower combination.
The full kitchen has full-size appliances, a dishwasher, ice maker, coffee maker plus a single-serve coffee maker, a toaster and a blender.
Washer and dryer in the condo, so you can pack light.
The private deck has an electric grill and seating for four.
We provide towels (one set per guest), linens and starter supplies of toilet paper, paper towels, dish soap, laundry soap, dishwasher tabs, trash bags and toiletries. Bring beach towels for the pool and the lake.

RESORT AMENITIES: Indoor and outdoor pools, a hot tub, sauna and steam room.
Indoor and outdoor tennis courts, a basketball court and a playground.
A game room with pinball, foosball and a pool table, plus a clubhouse.
A swim platform for lake swimming, a boat launch and trailer parking.
Please follow the opening hours and rules posted at each facility.

LAKE: Boat slips at The Knolls are available to rent. Ask our office when you book.
Plan where you will launch your boat and park your trailer before you arrive. The nearest boat launch and trailer parking areas are on the directions with your confirmation and on the magnet on the refrigerator.
In Missouri, anyone born after January 1, 1984 must complete an approved boating education course before driving a motorized boat. Operators must be at least 14, and under-16s need a supervising adult aboard.
Everyone aged 15 to 65 needs a Missouri fishing permit. You can buy one at Walmart or Fitz Fishing Tackle & Supplies.

PARKING: This condo has parking for up to 2 vehicles.
If a parking pass is provided in the condo, display it in your vehicle for your whole stay, or you may be towed. Leave the pass in the condo when you leave.
If you bring more cars than the condo allows, you can park an extra vehicle at our office in Osage Beach (4571 Osage Beach Pkwy North).

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM on your departure date. Thank you for staying with us!
- Lock all doors and windows.
- Set the AC to Auto at 78 degrees in summer, or the heat to 55 degrees in winter.
- Take all trash out to the proper containers.
- Put used towels in one of the bathtubs.
- Leave any parking pass in the condo, and report any damage to our office before you leave.
Late checkout can be requested before your departure date. Please call to confirm 1 day before you leave, not on the morning of departure. Staying past 10:00 AM without an arrangement costs $200 per hour.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the condo during office hours, call Your Lake Vacation on 573-365-3367. The office is open 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM, seven days a week from Memorial Day to Labor Day and Monday to Friday the rest of the year.
For urgent problems after hours, call our emergency number 573-216-8715. If you reach voicemail, leave a message and we will call you back as soon as possible.
For heat, water or air problems, first check the troubleshooting guide on the refrigerator magnet.

LOCAL TIPS: The condo is in the heart of Osage Beach, minutes from restaurants, shopping, grocery stores, miniature golf, arcades and go-karts.
Some favorites from our lake area guide
- Big Surf Waterpark, with a wave pool, slides, a floating river and white-water rapids
- Miner Mike's and Buster's, a 50,000 sq ft indoor family fun center for rainy days
- Lemans Family Fun Park, the lake's original go-kart track
- Celebration Cruises, with sightseeing cruises from Osage Beach and Lake Ozark
- Lake Ozark Helicopters, scenic flights over Bagnell Dam and the lake
- Ozarks Amphitheater in Camdenton for summer concerts
Want to get out on the water without your own boat? Boat and jet ski rental companies around Osage Beach include LOTO Boat Rental, Surdyke Boat Rentals and The Getaway.
Golfers are well looked after here. Ask us about our Lake of the Ozarks golf packages.
For events during your stay, see the community calendar at funlake.com.
```

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples**: Your Lake Vacation doesn't publish fees for early check-in, late checkout or boat
slips (the only published figure is $200 per hour for an *unarranged* late departure), so tell them they
set their own prices.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Early check-in (from 1:00 PM) | $40 | Get to the lake sooner. Confirmed 1 day before arrival, if the housekeeping schedule allows. |
| Late checkout (until 12:00 PM) | $40 | One more slow morning on the deck. Confirmed 1 day before departure, subject to availability. |
| Boat slip at The Knolls | $50 per night | Keep your boat right at the resort during your stay. Subject to availability. |

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | Between 3:00 and 4:00 PM; code sent on arrival day when the condo is ready; early check-in on request, call 1 day before |
| How do we get the keys? | No keys: keyless 4-digit code sent on arrival day, go straight to the condo |
| Can we use the fireplace? | Yes, it's electric and fine to use (wood/gas fireplaces are owner-only) |
| Can I park my boat trailer at the condo? | Plan it before arriving; launch and trailer parking are on the confirmation directions and fridge magnet; slips can be rented |
| We have three cars, where do we park? | 2 spaces at the condo; extra car at the office in Osage Beach |
| What do we do before we leave? | By 10 AM: lock up, AC 78 / heat 55, trash out, towels in a tub, leave parking pass |
| The AC isn't working at 9 PM, who do I call? | Check fridge magnet guide, then after-hours number 573-216-8715 (911 for emergencies) |
| ¿Podemos traer a nuestro perro? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: no, condos are strictly no-pet ($200 fee) |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's sent on arrival day / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the checkout answer, the Spanish answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
