# Demo guide: Dry Heat Resorts, Haven House at Andreas Hills (Palm Springs, CA)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Dry Heat Resorts' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Haven House:** a 4-suite luxury home in Andreas Hills with a saltwater pool, spa, fire pit and a
paid winter pool-heat fee. It has a long, detailed listing and clear house rules (outdoor music ban,
day and night occupancy, car limit), which make a convincing demo.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- dryheatresorts.com/listings/561493 (description, house rules, check-in 3 PM / check-out 11 AM, amenities, reviews, Palm Springs City ID)
- dryheatresorts.com (home page: "Better Properties | Personal Service", "Since 2004, Dry Heat Resorts has represented private vacation home rentals in Palm Springs", kenny@dryheatresorts.com)

Every fact below comes from those pages. Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders.
**Check:** the pool-heat fee is "$60/night + tax" for **Nov 1–Feb 28** in the description but
**Nov 1–Mar 31** in the house rules, so the manual says "winter bookings from November 1" and you should
confirm the end date. The listing shows **Palm Springs City ID #2994** (the city's vacation rental
registration). No phone number is displayed on the site, so the kit uses Kenny's email.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Haven House at Andreas Hills — Palm Springs`
   - **City, country:** `Palm Springs, California`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the city).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Haven House at Andreas Hills, your "Santa Barbara in the Desert" getaway, represented by Dry Heat Resorts. This 4-bedroom, 3,400 sq ft solar-powered home sits on a large private lot in Andreas Hills, with sweeping mountain views, a saltwater pool and four en-suite bedrooms. Palm Springs City ID #2994.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is at 3:00 PM. Your entry details are in your arrival message from Dry Heat Resorts. Please don't share them.
All guests must sign the rental agreement before arrival.

WI-FI: The home has Wi-Fi. The network name and password are in your arrival message.
Cell reception can be patchy here, guests tell us, so the Wi-Fi is your friend. Free long-distance calls to the US and Canada are included.

HOUSE RULES: A maximum of 8 people may stay overnight. Maximum daytime occupancy is 12.
All music is prohibited outside, at all times of day, under Palm Springs ordinance.
No smoking inside.
No pets.
Maximum of 4 cars at the property.

THE HOME: Four bedroom suites, each with its own bathroom. The primary suite has a king bed, gas fireplace, desk, 55-inch TV, a private patio overlooking the pool and fire pit, a bathtub with a mountain view and a walk-in shower. Two more suites have king beds and walk-in closets. The fourth has a queen bed and a door to the pool.
Gourmet kitchen with a Wolf double oven, Wolf six-burner stove, Sub-Zero fridge, Bosch dishwasher, and both drip and Keurig coffee makers. Breakfast nook for four overlooking the pool.
Formal dining for eight and a formal living room with a gas fireplace, both opening to furnished patios with mountain views.
Family room with a 65-inch TV and a bar, next to the kitchen.
Laundry room, and a water purification and softening system throughout.

POOL: Lagoon-shaped saltwater pool and a bubbling spa with a waterfall, with four chaise lounges, a gas fire pit, a gas grill and a large covered furnished patio with ceiling fans.
Pool heat is charged at $60 per night plus tax on winter bookings, from November 1.

PARKING: Large driveway and a two-car garage with an electric car charger. Maximum of 4 cars at the property.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
Please lock up when you leave.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the home, email Kenny at Dry Heat Resorts at kenny@dryheatresorts.com, or reply to your booking messages.

LOCAL TIPS: Downtown Palm Springs is only five minutes away.
Indian Canyons Golf Resort is right next door, and the Indian Canyons palm tree oasis is around the corner.
You can walk from the house to the Garston Trail or the South Lykken Loop. The views are breathtaking.
Andreas Hills, known as the Bel Air of Palm Springs, is quiet and secluded, with views of the desert floor, rugged canyons and the surrounding mountains.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `3:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `11:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | leave empty (not shown on their site; ask Kenny) |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The pool
heat price is **their real published fee**; the other two are **demo examples**.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Pool heat (per night, winter) | $60 | Heated saltwater pool for winter stays from November 1. Plus tax. |
| Early check-in (from 12:00 PM) | $75 | More time by the pool on arrival day. Subject to availability. |
| Late check-out (until 1:00 PM) | $75 | One more desert morning. Subject to availability. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| Can we play music by the pool? | No, outdoor music is prohibited at all times (Palm Springs ordinance) |
| Can we invite 4 friends over for the afternoon? | Daytime maximum is 12; overnight maximum is 8 |
| Is the pool heated in December? | Yes, pool heat is $60/night + tax on winter bookings |
| Can I charge my Tesla? | Yes, there's an electric car charger in the garage |
| How many cars can we bring? | Maximum 4 |
| Is there a hiking trail nearby? | Walk to the Garston Trail or South Lykken Loop |
| ¿A qué hora es el check-in? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: 3 PM |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the music answer, the Spanish answer, the Extras tab) for a follow-up email.
