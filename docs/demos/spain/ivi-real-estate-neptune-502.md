# Demo guide: IVI Real Estate, Neptune 502 (Torremolinos / Benalmádena Costa, Spain)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of IVI Real Estate's holiday apartments *before*
contacting them, so the first message says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Neptune 502:** a beachfront 2-bedroom apartment with a sea-view terrace in a gated complex with a
communal pool. Its listing has everything a guest asks about: check-in and check-out times, self
check-in, luggage drop-off, tourist licence, free parking and house rules. IVI also sells an airport
transfer and a €60 welcome pack on their booking site, so the Extras tab tells a real story. Their
office is open 10:00–18:00, so the concierge covering evenings and nights is the hook.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
booking.ivirealestate.com/en/neptune-502-1-linea-playa-terraza-piscina-vistas-al-mar-h1160 (and the
Spanish /es/ version), booking.ivirealestate.com/en/107/servicio-de-transfer (airport transfer, no price
shown), booking.ivirealestate.com/en/109/te-hacemos-la-compra (welcome pack €60 and contents), and
ivirealestate.com/en/ (office hours daily 10:00–18:00; office at Calle Salvador Allende 41, Torremolinos,
in La Carihuela). Every fact below comes from those pages. **Unverified:** the town. The listing only
shows a map pin (36.6043, -4.5087) close to the Torremolinos / Benalmádena Costa border; the street
address is not published. The pool is described only as "seasonal" (no dates or hours), and parking is
only listed as "free car park" with no details. Door codes, key box details and the Wi-Fi password are
placeholders.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Neptune 502 — Beachfront, Terrace & Pool`
   - **City, country:** `Torremolinos, Spain` (confirm; the pin is near the Benalmádena border)
   - **Street address:** leave empty.
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 3:00 PM until midnight. This apartment has self check-in, and your access details are in your arrival message.
Arriving early? Luggage drop-off is allowed, so ask us and we will tell you how.
Need a ride? IVI Real Estate offers an airport transfer service. Ask us to book it when you send your flight details.

GUEST REGISTRATION: Spanish law requires every holiday rental to register the identity documents of all guests with the authorities. That is why we ask for passport or ID details for everyone in your group before arrival. Thank you for sending them early, it makes your arrival smoother.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

THE APARTMENT: About 90 square metres, for up to 6 guests.
- Bedroom 1 with a double bed, and bedroom 2 with bunk beds (two single beds). There is also a sofa bed.
- Two bathrooms, both with walk-in showers, complimentary shower gel and shampoo, and a hairdryer.
- Kitchen with ceramic hob, microwave, fridge and freezer, coffee maker, kettle, toaster, dishes and utensils.
- Living room with a comfortable sofa, a large flat-screen TV, a dining table and air conditioning with heating.
- Washing machine, iron and ironing board, clothes hangers, a clothesline and cleaning products.
- Bed linen and towels are included. Cots are available on request.
The terrace is the best place in the apartment, so enjoy the sea views with a drink in the sun.

POOL: The gated complex has a communal swimming pool and garden. The pool is seasonal, so please ask us whether it is open during your dates.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests.
No pets.
No parties, and no stag or hen parties.
The building is very quiet. Please keep noise down, especially at night and on the terrace.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Self check-out applies, so follow the steps in your arrival message for the keys.
Thank you for staying with IVI Real Estate. We hope to see you again on the Costa del Sol!

EMERGENCY: For any emergency in Spain, call 112 (police, ambulance, fire). Operators speak English.
For anything about the apartment, call IVI Real Estate on +34 952 381 627 or WhatsApp +34 640 181 485, or email info@ivirealestate.com. Our office is open every day from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Our office is in La Carihuela, Torremolinos' old fishermen's quarter, at Calle Salvador Allende 41. Come and say hello if you need anything during office hours.
Want a full fridge when you arrive? Our welcome pack (€60) includes water, milk, orange juice, olive oil, coffee, tea, bread, butter, jam, ham, cheese, yogurts, cereal, eggs, bacon, baked beans and 4 San Miguel beers.
```

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.
