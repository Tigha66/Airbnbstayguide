## Before you send

1. **Volume:** max 10 emails a day from hello@getstayguide.com. Mix markets, no weekends.
2. **Send times (UK clock):**

   | Market | Send time (UK) |
   |---|---|
   | UK | 09:00–11:00 |
   | Spain, Portugal, Greece | 09:00–11:00 (their morning) |
   | Dubai | 06:00–08:00 (their 10:00–12:00), Monday–Friday |
   | US East | 14:00–17:00 |
   | US Arizona / California / Hawaii | 17:00–21:00 |

3. **Rules differ by country (not legal advice):**
   - **UK (PECR):** cold emails to companies (Ltd/LLP) are fine with your name, address and an opt-out.
   - **Spain:** law LSSI is strict about unsolicited commercial email, even business-to-business. For Spain, a softer first contact is safer: the **contact form or WhatsApp**, asking whether they'd like the details.
   - **Portugal and Greece:** keep it to company addresses (info@), with a clear opt-out.
   - **Dubai and the US:** standard business-to-business outreach is fine.
   - Always honour "no" immediately.
4. **Price:** don't quote one in the first email. Offer the free pilot (3 properties, 3 months). If asked, quote in their currency (£, € or AED). Add the matching prices in Stripe before the first sale.
5. **Check every fact on the company's site** before using it in the first line.
