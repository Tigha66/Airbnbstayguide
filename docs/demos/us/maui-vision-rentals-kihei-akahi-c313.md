# Demo guide: Maui Vision Rentals, Kihei Akahi C313 (Kihei, Maui, HI)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Maui Vision Rentals' condos *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why C313:** Maui Vision Rentals began in 2002 when Kathy McCartney bought a condo at the Kihei Akahi
Resort, and most of their Maui listings are still in that complex. C313 has the most complete listing
(times, occupancy, parking, rules) and is right across from Kama'ole Beach Park II.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- mauivision.com/vrp/rentals/prop1002.htm (description, check-in/out, occupancy, amenities, smoking, GE/TA tax ID, reviews)
- mauivision.com/cancellation-and-rental-policies/ (quiet time, pets, pool, insects, checkout duties, keys, safety)
- mauivision.com/about-us/ (Kathy McCartney, "The dream started in 2002… Kihei Akahi Resort, unit C618")

Every fact below comes from those pages. Door codes, the key/lockbox details and the Wi-Fi password are
left as placeholders. **Check:** the listing says "Free Reserved Parking Spot" but also ticks "Parking
For A Fee"; the kit uses the free reserved spot. Beach gear details (snorkel gear, beach toys) come from
guest reviews, so the kit only names what the listing itself says (beach chairs, beach towels, ice chest).
Hawaii GE & TA tax ID for this unit: **006-076-2112-01** (shown on the listing).

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Kihei Akahi C313 — Kihei, Maui`
   - **City, country:** `Kihei, Hawaii`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Aloha and welcome to Unit C313 at the Kihei Akahi Resort, a beautifully renovated one-bedroom condo cared for by Maui Vision Rentals. You're on the third floor, just steps from Kama'ole Beach Park II, with a private lanai looking over lush gardens, Haleakala and a peek of the ocean.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is at 4:00 PM. Self check-in. Your entry details are in your arrival message from Maui Vision Rentals. Please don't share them.
Need an earlier check-in? Please get our approval in writing before you arrive on Maui. If we can accommodate you, we will. A guaranteed earlier time has a small charge.
Kahului Airport is a 25 to 30 minute drive away. The resort has elevators.

WI-FI: Free secured high-speed Wi-Fi (400 Mbps). The network name and password are in your arrival message.
You can also make free calls to the U.S. mainland and Canada from the condo phone.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 3 guests (an extra charge applies for a 3rd guest). Only registered guests may stay overnight.
No smoking or vaping in the condo, on the lanai or in the hallways. A $500 fine plus damage costs applies. There is a designated outdoor smoking area.
No pets. Pre-approved service and emotional support animals only.
Quiet time is 9 PM to 8 AM. Please keep music low and follow the rules posted around the resort.
No parties, receptions or other group events without our written consent.
Keep the front door and lanai screen doors closed behind you. Hawaii is tropical and insects love to come inside.
Please lock all windows, the lanai sliding door and the front door whenever you go out.

THE CONDO: King bed in the bedroom with a TV, and a queen-size sofa bed in the living room with a 40-inch TV.
Window air conditioner in the living room, plus ceiling fans throughout.
Fully equipped kitchen with full-size appliances, dishwasher, coffee maker, filtered cold water, cookware and utensils.
Walk-in shower, and a washer and dryer in the condo.
Beach chairs, beach towels and an ice chest are here for you. Please bring beach items back for the next guests.
There's no daily housekeeping. We leave a starter supply of soap and paper products.

POOL: The resort has two swimming pools with lounge chairs and a bathroom with showers. Both pools have a BBQ area with a sink, refrigerator, tables and chairs. There's also a tennis court and a pickleball court.
Children must be supervised by an adult at the pool at all times.

PARKING: One free reserved parking spot for the condo.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Mahalo for staying with us!
Wash the dishes or put them in the dishwasher, and bag all garbage so it can't leak.
Leave the pool key (and condo key, if you have one) in the departure spot described in your arrival message. Lost keys cost $200, as locks must be changed.
Need a late check-out? Ask for written approval in advance. An unauthorized late check-out may be charged an extra night.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the condo, call Maui Vision Rentals on 808-989-6369.
Ocean safety matters. Surf, currents and weather can change fast, and strong waves can be dangerous. Never turn your back on the ocean.

LOCAL TIPS: Kama'ole Beach Park II is right across the street.
Wailea and Keawakapu Beach are just minutes away.
Restaurants and shops are close by, so you can do a lot on foot.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `4:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `11:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `808-989-6369` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples**: Maui Vision only says a guaranteed early/late window has "a small charge", so tell
Kathy she sets her own prices.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|

| Guaranteed late check-out (until 2:00 PM) | $40 | One more swim before your evening flight. Approved in writing in advance. |

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |

| How far is the beach? | Kama'ole Beach Park II is right across the street |
| Is there air conditioning in the bedroom? | Window AC in the living room only; ceiling fans throughout |
| Can I smoke on the lanai? | No; $500 fine; designated outdoor smoking area |
| Are there beach chairs? | Yes, beach chairs, beach towels and an ice chest |
| Where do we leave the keys? | In the departure spot from the arrival message; $200 if lost |
| What time is quiet time? | 9 PM to 8 AM |
| 何時にチェックアウトですか？ (Japanese) | Answers in Japanese: 11 AM |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (Japanese is a good one for Maui) and the **Extras** tab.
