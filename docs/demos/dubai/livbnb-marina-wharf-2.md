# Demo guide: Livbnb, Marina Wharf High Floor with Open View (Dubai Marina)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Livbnb's homes *before* contacting them, so the first
email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Marina Wharf 2:** a 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom high-floor apartment for 6 guests in Marina Wharf
Tower 2, next to the Marina Promenade. The listing publishes almost every Dubai guest question in one
place: check-in window, passport copies for building registration, the refundable deposit, the
Tourism Dirham, the smoking fine and the access/parking card fee. Livbnb uses the same guideline text
across its listings, so one guide shows how it would work for their whole 180+ portfolio.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): livbnb.com/listing/livbnb-3b-w-marina-views-near-beach-marina-mall
(title, description, guidelines, amenities, default check-in 15:00 / check-out 11:00; the page loads its
text from Livbnb's own listing feed), livbnb.com/faq (guest FAQs: mid-stay cleaning and maintenance on
request), livbnb.com/marina (metro, tram, Marina Walk, JBR Beach), experiences.livbnb.com (their own
tours and tickets with AED prices) and the livbnb.com footer (Marina Plaza office, +971 4 835 7309,
hello@livbnb.com, WhatsApp +971 55 740 1954). Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not published:**
the unit number, pool and gym hours, and where the access card is handed over. Door codes, access
cards and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders, and you should say so in the email. The
listing page slug says "3B w Marina Views near Beach Marina Mall" but the title is "Marina Wharf High
Floor with Open View"; use the title.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Marina Wharf 2 — Dubai Marina`
   - **City, country:** `Dubai Marina, Dubai, United Arab Emirates`
   - **Street address:** `Marina Wharf 2, Dubai Marina` (as published on the listing)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to your high-floor 3-bedroom apartment in Marina Wharf Tower 2, Dubai Marina, managed by Livbnb. It has three balconies with panoramic views, 3.5 bathrooms and sleeps up to 6 guests. The beach, Marina Walk, restaurants and nightlife are all within walking distance. This is a registered holiday home apartment.

ARRIVAL: Standard check-in is between 3:00 PM and 12:00 AM (midnight). If you need to check in outside these hours, please tell us in advance and we'll do our best to arrange it, depending on availability. Additional fees may apply.
Early check-in is subject to availability and an additional cost.
Your access card, parking card and arrival instructions are sent in your arrival message from Livbnb. Please don't share them.
Livbnb's field operations team assists with check-ins in person.

GUEST REGISTRATION: For building registration, we need a copy of the passport of every guest staying in the apartment before you arrive.
The primary guest signs a rental agreement and pays a refundable security deposit after booking and before check-in. The amount depends on the length and cost of the stay. Deposits are refunded within 7 business days of check-out, after the apartment has been inspected.
Dubai's Tourism Dirham is 10 AED per night per bedroom. It is not included in your booking price and can be paid separately in advance or on arrival.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

THE APARTMENT: Three bedrooms with four beds, 3.5 bathrooms and three balconies.
The kitchen has an oven, stove, microwave, toaster, dishwasher, refrigerator, dishes and utensils.
Also included are air conditioning, a washing machine, iron and board, hair dryer, TV with satellite channels, linens and towels.
We provide a starter pack of toiletries. Cooking condiments, cleaning products and extra toiletries are not stocked, so plan to pick these up during your stay.
Need a mid-stay clean or maintenance? Contact the Livbnb team and we'll schedule a visit.

POOL AND GYM: Marina Wharf Tower 2 has outdoor swimming pools, a gym, recreational facilities, high-speed elevators and retail outlets. Ask the Livbnb team for current opening hours.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests. Minimum stay is 2 nights.
Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the apartment. A fine of 2,000 AED is charged for smoking indoors. Smoking is allowed only on the balconies.
A replacement fee of 500 AED is charged for a lost access card or parking card.
Dubai is always growing, so there may be construction or renovation work in the community or the building during your stay.

PARKING: Free parking on the premises. Your parking card comes with your access card. A lost parking card costs 500 AED to replace.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Thank you for staying with Livbnb!
Late checkout is subject to availability and an additional fee. Please contact your agent at least 24 hours in advance to ask.
Please return your access card and parking card as described in your arrival message. Your deposit is refunded within 7 business days after the inspection.

EMERGENCY: For police call 999. For an ambulance call 998.
For anything about the apartment, call Livbnb on +971 4 835 7309, WhatsApp +971 55 740 1954, or email hello@livbnb.com. Livbnb's office is in Marina Plaza, Dubai Marina.

LOCAL TIPS: Marina Walk is perfect for an evening stroll, and JBR Beach is close by.
Dubai Marina has its own metro and tram stations, so getting around the city is easy, and supermarkets are nearby.
Livbnb also books Dubai experiences, from Burj Khalifa At the Top tickets to Black Boats tours of Atlantis, jet skis around the Palm and the Traditional Dubai City Tour. Browse them at experiences.livbnb.com.
```

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. Early
check-in and late checkout prices are **demo examples** (Livbnb only says "additional cost"). The tour
price is Livbnb's own published price on experiences.livbnb.com.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Early check-in (from 12:00 PM) | AED 150 | Land early? Get into the apartment sooner. Subject to availability. |
| Late checkout (until 2:00 PM) | AED 150 | A slower last morning in the Marina. Request at least 24 hours ahead; subject to availability. |
| Black Boats: 60-minute Atlantis tour | AED 145 | See the Palm and Atlantis from the water, booked through Livbnb Experiences. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | Between 3:00 PM and midnight; outside those hours tell Livbnb in advance (fees may apply) |
| Do you need our passports? | Yes, a copy for every guest before arrival, for building registration |
| How much is the Tourism Dirham? | 10 AED per night per bedroom, not included, paid in advance or on arrival |
| Can I smoke on the balcony? | Yes, balconies only; 2,000 AED fine for smoking inside |
| I lost my parking card | 500 AED replacement fee; contact Livbnb |
| Is there a pool? | Yes, the tower has outdoor pools and a gym; ask the team for hours |
| ما هو وقت تسجيل المغادرة؟ (Arabic: what time is checkout?) | Answers in Arabic: 11:00 AM; late checkout on request 24h ahead |
| Когда вернут депозит? (Russian: when is the deposit refunded?) | Answers in Russian: within 7 business days after check-out and inspection |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; access details are in the arrival message / hands over to Livbnb |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Arabic) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the Tourism Dirham answer, the Arabic answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
