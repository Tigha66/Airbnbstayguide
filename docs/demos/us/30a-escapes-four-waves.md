# Demo guide: 30A Escapes, Four Waves (Seacrest Beach, FL)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of 30A Escapes' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Four Waves:** a brand-new 5-bedroom home for 14 in Seacrest Beach, one of their featured homes.
Big groups plus a community full of rules (pool passes, tram, bikes with waivers, no LSVs) means lots of
repeat questions, and it's the kind of home they're proud of.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- 30aescapes.com/rentals/four-waves (description, beds, bikes, Seacrest amenities, cameras, LSV rule, pets, parking)
- 30aescapes.com/faqs (check-in 4 PM / check-out 10 AM, door codes, checkout list, pool passes, supplies, bikes, contacts)
- 30aescapes.com (home page line "For 15 years, 30A Escapes has brought the feeling of 30A to life…")
- 30aescapes.com/30a-services (local partners: groceries, chefs, beach chairs, bonfires)

Every fact below comes from those pages. Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders.
**Note:** their FAQ asks guests **not** to request early check-in, so the kit doesn't sell one. No
specific prices for extras are published on the pages checked.

---

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Four Waves — Seacrest Beach, 30A`
   - **City, country:** `Seacrest Beach, Florida`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the community).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Four Waves, a brand-new 5-bedroom, 5.5-bath beach home in the heart of Seacrest Beach, cared for by 30A Escapes. Start the morning with coffee on the front porch, spend the afternoon at the 12,000 sq ft Lagoon Pool or on the beach, then grill fresh seafood and settle into the screened porch for the evening.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is at 4:00 PM. There's no office to visit. The home has keyless entry, and your door code is sent on the day of check-in. Please don't share it.
Please don't request an early check-in. We use every minute between stays to make the home perfect for you.

WI-FI: The home has Wi-Fi. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 14 guests.
Pets are not allowed in this home.
LSVs (low-speed vehicles) are not allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest or Alys Beach.
For your security, there are active cameras over the garage, on the side of the home overlooking the driveway, overlooking the front porch and at the front door.

THE HOME: Five en-suite bedrooms across three floors.
- 1st floor. King guest suite with a walk-in shower, plus a half bath off the living area.
- 2nd floor. Queen guest suite, primary king suite with dual vanities, and a bunk suite with two twin-over-twin bunks.
- 3rd floor. A guest suite with two queen beds that sleeps 4.
Chef's kitchen with stainless steel appliances, a standalone ice maker, breakfast bar for 4 and dining for 8.
Three places to hang out. The main living room, a second-floor lounge with a kitchenette and wine cooler, and a separate hangout for kids and teens.
Screened porch with outdoor sofas, a TV and dining for 4. The gas grill is just outside it.
We provide starter shampoo, conditioner, lotion and body wash, toilet paper, paper towels, dish soap, dishwasher and laundry packets, and trash bags. Bath towels and bed linens are provided, but beach towels are not.

POOL: Seacrest Beach's 12,000 sq ft Lagoon Pool is yours to enjoy. The home has 2 pool passes from the Homeowners Association, and one pass gets your whole group in.
The pool pass gives access to the fitness and tennis centers, but there's a daily, hourly or weekly fee to use them.

BIKES: Seven bikes are included. A signed waiver is required before using them. Need more bikes, other sizes, a trailer or a tagalong? We rent them. Helmets and baskets are free on request.

PARKING: Free parking at the home, including the driveway and garage.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is at 10:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
- Leave all linens on the beds and towels in the bathrooms.
- Take all trash out to the outside bins, including anything in the fridge.
- Load the dishwasher and start it.
- Set thermostats to Cool at 74 (March to October) or Heat at 65 (November to February). Leave the AC fan on AUTO.
- Close and lock all doors and windows.
- Leave keys, amenity bracelets or fobs in their lockboxes or in the home.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
30A Escapes offers 24/7 guest support. Call or text Guest Relations on 850-534-6832 or email connect@30aescapes.com.

LOCAL TIPS: Take the seasonal tram to the beach and the Lagoon Pool, or use Seacrest's private community beach access.
Seacrest has more than 1.3 miles of walking trails, plus easy access to the Scenic Highway 30A bike path.
Peddler's Pavilion is nearby for casual dining, shopping, live entertainment and seasonal events.
Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach are close by for restaurants, coffee and boutiques.
Want groceries waiting, a private chef, a beach chair setup or a beach bonfire? Ask us and we'll point you to our trusted local partners.
```

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| Can we check in at 1 PM? | Check-in is 4 PM; please don't request early check-in |
| How many pool passes do we get? | 2, and one pass gets the whole group in |
| Can we bring our golf cart? | LSVs aren't allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest or Alys Beach |
| Are beach towels provided? | No, only bath towels and linens |
| How many bikes are there? | 7, waiver required; more can be rented |
| What do we do before leaving? | The checkout list (linens on beds, trash out, dishwasher, thermostats, keys) |
| Can we bring our dog? | No pets in this home |
| ¿A qué hora es la salida? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: 10 AM |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's sent on the day of check-in / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the pool-pass answer, the Spanish answer, the Extras tab) for a follow-up email.
