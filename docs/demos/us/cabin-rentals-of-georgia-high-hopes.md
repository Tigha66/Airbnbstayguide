# Demo guide: Cabin Rentals of Georgia, High Hopes (Blue Ridge, GA)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Cabin Rentals of Georgia's homes *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why High Hopes:** a typical CRG luxury cabin (4 suites, sleeps 8, hot tub, game room, mountain views)
with a very detailed listing: distances, layout, pet rules, and the Toccoa River access just down the road.
It shows off the questions Blue Ridge guests always ask: check-in window, dogs, firewood, the river, bears.
CRG's FAQ and Rental Policies pages cover the company-wide rules for all 30+ cabins.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- cabin-rentals-of-georgia.com/cabin/blue-ridge/high-hopes (description, layout, features, distances)
- cabin-rentals-of-georgia.com/rental-policies (check-in 3:30–5:30 PM, check-out 10:00 AM, early/late fee,
  pet rules and fees, age, smoking, firearms and firecrackers)
- cabin-rentals-of-georgia.com/faq (door code and Wi-Fi texted on arrival afternoon, directions two weeks
  before, no 4WD needed, firewood delivery prices, lighter fluid ban, bears and trash, hospital, groceries)
- cabin-rentals-of-georgia.com/about-us (the Knight family; built and decorated 10 cabins; massage,
  personal chef, grocery stocking)

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not verified / left out:** door code, Wi-Fi network and
password, the exact address, and an after-hours phone number. Note that the early check-in/late check-out
fee is worded two ways on their site ("$50.00 charge up to 2 hours" on Policies, "$50/hour up to 2 hours"
in the FAQ); the manual avoids quoting a price.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `High Hopes — Blue Ridge`
   - **City, country:** `Blue Ridge, Georgia`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to High Hopes, a luxury four-suite mountain cabin in a quiet gated community with beautiful views of the Blue Ridge Mountains. It's looked after by Cabin Rentals of Georgia, the Knight family's Blue Ridge business since 2003. Downtown Blue Ridge is about 10 minutes away, McCaysville and Copperhill about 5, and the Toccoa River is just down the road.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is between 3:30 PM and 5:30 PM. Head straight to the cabin using the directions we send you two weeks before arrival. Your door code and Wi-Fi password are texted to you on the afternoon of your arrival. Please don't share the code.
If you arrive after 5:30 PM, our office will be closed, so follow our turn-by-turn directions. GPS can be unreliable in the mountains.
You don't need 4WD. The gated community has paved roads.
Early check-in or late check-out of up to 2 hours may be possible for a fee, if available. It must be approved by us in advance.

WI-FI: The cabin has high-speed internet. The network name and password are texted to you on your arrival afternoon.
Mountain internet can occasionally drop, especially in cloudy or rainy weather. If it does, try resetting the modem or router, or use your phone's hotspot. Verizon and AT&T usually work in the area.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 8 guests. At least one person aged 25 or older must stay overnight every night, and the person who booked must stay with the group.
No smoking in the cabin or on the porches.
No dirt bikes, ATVs, firearms or firecrackers on the property.
Dogs are allowed here only if approved by us before arrival. Up to 2 dogs, 35 lbs or less, non-shedding and crate trained. No cats. Keep dogs off the furniture, beds and hot tub, and never leave them alone in the cabin; take them with you or crate them.
Please bag your trash and put it in the bear-proof garbage cans. Never leave trash outside, because bears and raccoons will find it.

INSIDE HIGH HOPES: The main floor has an open living area with a gas log fireplace, dining for 8, a fully equipped kitchen (K-cup and regular coffee maker, crock pot, blender, griddle, toaster), a half bath, laundry with detergent, and the king master suite with a clawfoot tub and stone shower.
Upstairs are two queen suites, each with its own bathroom and big mountain-view windows.
The terrace level has a game room with a gas log fireplace, shuffleboard, a game table and a mini bar with sink and fridge, plus a fourth bedroom with a king bed.
There are 6 smart TVs. Linens, towels, paper products, soaps and starter toiletries are provided; just bring your personal items. Hot tub towels aren't provided in every cabin, so pack a few.

HOT TUB AND DECK: The hot tub is on the covered terrace-level porch. Please keep dogs out of it.
The main-level deck has an outdoor wood-burning fireplace with seating and a gas grill nearby. The old fire pit has been removed.
For wood fires, bring a starter log like Duraflame. Lighter fluid or any liquid fire starter is strictly forbidden; if it's found at check-out, the security deposit is forfeited.
Want firewood waiting for you? Ask us to add a delivery to your reservation.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is promptly at 10:00 AM, unless we've agreed a late check-out in advance. Thank you for staying with us!
Make sure all trash is bagged and in the bear-proof cans. If you leave something behind, call our office as soon as possible; shipping and handling costs apply.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911. The local hospital is Fannin County Regional, on Hwy 5 off Hwy 515 in Blue Ridge. Riverstone Medical Clinic is on Hwy 515 near Ingles.
For anything about the cabin, call Cabin Rentals of Georgia on 706-432-2140 or email info@cabin-rentals-of-georgia.com. The office closes at 5:30 PM.
You're in the mountains, so you may see deer, bears, raccoons, turkeys, snakes and the odd small scorpion. Give wildlife space. In fall, harmless ladybugs sometimes come indoors; just vacuum them up.

LOCAL TIPS: Distances from High Hopes:
- 0.3 miles: community pavilion with Toccoa River access for fishing and tubing
- 2.9 miles: downtown McCaysville, GA and Copperhill, TN
- 4.8 miles: Horseshoe Bend Park, about a 2-hour tube float from the cabin. Leave a car there, or have someone in your group pick you up.
- 6.7 miles: Mercier Orchards
- 9 miles: downtown Blue Ridge, with boutiques, galleries, coffee shops, restaurants and breweries
- 12.8 miles: Ocoee Whitewater Center (whitewater rafting on the Ocoee River)
- 21 miles: Fields of the Wood
- 31.6 miles: Harrah's Cherokee Valley Casino
Our partner North GA Trout Fishing offers guided fly-fishing in the Trout Capital of Georgia.
Groceries are at Walmart, Food Lion and Ingles in Blue Ridge. You can buy beer and wine in Blue Ridge; bottled liquor is sold in Ellijay.
Fall festivals are usually early to mid-October, and the leaves peak in late October and early November.
Our local team can also arrange massage, a personal chef or grocery stocking. Just ask.
```
