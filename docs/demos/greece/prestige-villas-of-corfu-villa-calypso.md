# Demo guide: Prestige Villas of Corfu, Villa Calypso (Kassiopi, Corfu)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Prestige Villas of Corfu's villas *before*
contacting them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Villa Calypso:** a stone-built 4-bedroom villa with a private pool, two minutes from Soukia
Beach and an eight-minute stroll to Kassiopi harbour. It's "Exclusive to Prestige Villas", a
"Car Free Holiday", and one of their highest rated (54 reviews on the listing). Its listing shows
check-in and check-out times, the property licence number, the pool size and local boat hire, so the
guide is rich, and Kassiopi is their heartland.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- prestigevillas.co.uk/villa-holidays/villa-calypso-kassiopi/ (description, three levels, 4 en-suite bedrooms, sleeps 2–8, pool 10.5 x 5.5 m, free A/C and Wi-Fi, check-in 16:00, check-out 10:00, regular housekeeping visits, welcome pack, pool towels, private parking, car not essential, not suitable for limited mobility, Kassiopi notes incl. Filippos Boats, licence 00000354752)
- prestigevillas.co.uk/gdpr-terms-and-conditions-prestigevillas/ (no events, no extra overnight guests, no security deposit but Accidental Damage Insurance, water tanks and outages, pool at own risk, extras such as chefs, boat hire and transfers booked direct with suppliers, phone numbers)
- prestigevillas.co.uk/about-us/ (Jane and Chris Sharpe; founded 2008 "in response to a handful of owners seeking guidance"; "almost one hundred" properties; emergency calls 24/7)
- prestigevillas.co.uk/chefs-cooks/ (recommended local private chefs)

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Conflicts and gaps:** the listing says check-out 10:00,
while the general terms say exclusive properties must be vacated by 10:30; the kit uses the
listing's 10:00 (flag it to them). The listing's "Extra Info" shows "Smoking Allowed / Pets Allowed /
Party Allowed" labels without clear yes/no values, so smoking and pets are left out (the terms ban
events). No prices are published for chefs, boats, transfers or late checkout, and the climate
resilience fee amount isn't stated. Key details and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Villa Calypso — Kassiopi`
   - **City, country:** `Kassiopi, Corfu, Greece`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (the listing only says "Nr Soukia Beach, 49081").
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Villa Calypso, a beautiful stone-built villa on a quiet lane in Kassiopi, on Corfu's north-east coast, exclusive to Prestige Villas of Corfu. Soukia Beach is two minutes away and the harbour, tavernas and shops of Kassiopi village are less than an eight-minute stroll, so you won't need a car. Property licence number 00000354752.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 4:00 PM. Your key and arrival details are in your arrival message from Prestige Villas of Corfu. Please don't share them.
A welcome pack is waiting for you in the villa.
Please note the villa has steps throughout, so it isn't suitable for guests with limited mobility.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi throughout the villa. The network name and password are in your arrival message.
There's a TV in the villa.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 8 guests. Only the guests on your guest list may stay overnight.
Functions and events of any kind are strictly not allowed.
Please look after the villa and its furnishings. Your booking includes Accidental Damage Insurance for accidental damage, but not negligence. Please report any accident, injury or damage to us during your stay.
Use appliances only for their intended purpose.
There's no lifeguard at the pool. Use it at your own risk, supervise children, and don't swim after drinking alcohol.

THE VILLA: The villa is on three levels.
- Top floor. The master suite takes the whole floor, with a king-size bed, views over Kassiopi to the Ionian Sea, and an en-suite with bath and separate shower.
- Main floor. Open-plan living and dining area, kitchen with granite worktops, utility room, a king-size double bedroom (en-suite with bath and separate shower) and a twin bedroom with en-suite shower room. Patio doors in every room open onto the terrace above the pool.
- Pool level. A further bedroom with en-suite shower room and its own outside access, opening straight onto the pool terrace.
There's also a guest WC.
Free air conditioning throughout, and mosquito nets on the windows.
Dishwasher and washing machine.
Our housekeepers visit regularly throughout your stay.
Island life note. Greece sometimes has short power or water cuts. The villa has its own water storage tank, and if you lose water we'll arrange a delivery as soon as possible.

POOL: The private pool is 10.5 m by 5.5 m, set in stone and old-brick patios with olive trees for shade. Pool towels are provided.
There are sun loungers with cushions and parasols, wicker lounge furniture, a dining table for 8 and a brick-built BBQ.

PARKING: The villa has private parking. You don't need a car here, because the beach, harbour, restaurants and shops are all walkable. If you'd like a car for exploring, we can recommend car hire.

GREEK TAXES: Greece charges a climate resilience fee per night for holiday rentals. We'll tell you if anything is due for your stay.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM. We try to be flexible if availability allows. Some owners charge to guarantee a late checkout, so please ask us in advance.
Please turn off the air conditioning, close the windows and doors, and leave the keys as described in your arrival message. Thank you for staying with us!

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 112 (the European emergency number).
For anything about the villa, call our resort office in Corfu on +30 693 894 3258 (also on WhatsApp). We take emergency calls 24/7.
You can also reach the UK office on +44 7984 751023.

LOCAL TIPS: Kassiopi is one of Corfu's most famous north-east coast resorts. The beaches here are pebble and shingle, so pack water shoes.
The long pedestrian high street has beach boutiques, souvenir shops, tavernas and cocktail bars, and there are plenty of small supermarkets and mini-markets.
The picture-postcard harbour is the highlight. Take one of the organised boat trips down the coast, or hire your own boat with Filippos Boats.
Want a night in? We recommend local private chefs who'll send you a personalised menu and cook at the villa. We can also recommend a shopping delivery service, Corfu walking tours, bicycle hire, nanny services, car rental and taxi or minibus transfers. These are booked and paid directly with the supplier.
```
