# Demo guide: Elite LUX Holiday Homes, Chic Studio in Manchester Tower (Dubai Marina)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Elite LUX Holiday Homes' apartments *before*
contacting them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why this studio:** a 370 sq ft studio for up to 3 guests on the 4th floor of Manchester Tower,
featured on their homepage, with an unusually detailed listing (transport, parking caveat, gym,
online check-in 24 hours before arrival). Their published House Rules add the questions every Dubai
guest asks: check-in times, visitor hours, ID at check-in, shisha, BBQs and quiet hours. The same rules
apply to every Elite LUX home, so one guide shows how it works across their portfolio.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): eliteluxhomes.ae/properties/chic-studio-in-the-heart-of-dubai-marina-rtp00001
(description, guest access, neighbourhood, getting around, things to know, house rules),
eliteluxhomes.ae/house-rules (check-in/out times, visitors, noise, smoking, BBQ, parking, emergency
contacts), eliteluxhomes.ae/policies (ID and card at check-in, security deposit released within 7 days,
5% VAT, Tourism Dirham for the first 30 nights), eliteluxhomes.ae/about ("transparency, trust, and
experience", 24/7 support) and the site footer (hotline +971 4 818 2300, WhatsApp +971 52 691 7660,
info@ and reservations@eliteluxhomes.ae, licence 1040155). **Conflict:** the listing says "check-in
and check-out times will be confirmed upon booking", while the House Rules page gives 3:00 PM
(4:00 PM for villas) and 11:00 AM; the kit uses the House Rules times, so double-check with them.
**Not published:** Tourism Dirham amount, deposit amount, gym hours, whether parking is included,
Wi-Fi password, access details.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Chic Studio, Manchester Tower — Dubai Marina`
   - **City, country:** `Dubai Marina, Dubai, United Arab Emirates`
   - **Street address:** `Manchester Tower, Dubai Marina, Dubai` (as published on the listing)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```

ARRIVAL: Standard check-in is at 3:00 PM.

Your access details are sent in your arrival message from Elite LUX. There's lift access to the 4th floor.
Early check-in is subject to availability and needs our written approval first. An unapproved early check-in is charged at the cost of a full night.
When you arrive, please check the studio and tell us within 24 hours about anything missing or damaged.

RTA taxis are easy to hail, or book with the Careem or Uber apps.
JBR beach is easy to reach from the Marina.
```

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | 3:00 PM; online check-in and documents 24h before arrival |
| Can my friend visit at 11pm? | No: visitors 8:00 AM–10:00 PM only, registered 24h ahead with ID |
| Can we smoke shisha on the balcony? | No, shisha isn't allowed indoors or outdoors |
| Is there parking? | Confirm with Elite LUX at booking; use only the designated space |
| What if we stay past 11? | Late checkout needs written approval; otherwise a full night is charged |
| How do I get to Dubai Mall? | Metro Red Line from DMCC or JLT, or a taxi/Careem/Uber |
| هل يجب أن أحضر جواز السفر؟ (Arabic: do I need my passport?) | Answers in Arabic: yes, original ID for every guest at check-in |
| Есть ли в здании спортзал? (Russian: is there a gym?) | Answers in Russian: yes, run by building management |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; access details are in the arrival message / hands over |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Russian) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the visitor-hours answer, the Arabic answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
