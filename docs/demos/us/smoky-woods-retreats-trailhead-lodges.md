# Demo guide: Smoky Woods Retreats, Trailhead Lodges (Cosby, TN)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Smoky Woods Retreats' cabins *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Trailhead Lodges:** 14 near-identical treehouse-style studio cabins on one site in Cosby, each
with a king bed, a private hot tub and room for 4, all managed by Smoky Woods Retreats. One guide
fits 14 of their ~30 listings, so it's the most convincing demo. The example unit is Trailhead Lodge #1.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): smokywoodsretreats.com/trailhead-venues (14 lodges, sleeps 4,
about 23 minutes to Gatlinburg), smokywoodsretreats.com/trailhead-lodge-1 (amenities, 7 minutes to the
Cosby entrance, directions to 4744 Indian Camp Creek Road, Cosby), and their direct booking page
smokywoodsretreats.holidayfuture.com/listings/557529 (check-in 4 pm, check-out 10 am, pets allowed,
no smoking inside, communal fire pit, 500+ Mbps Wi-Fi). Contact details from the site header
(360-306-1410, smokywoodsretreats@gmail.com). Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not
published:** pet fee, quiet hours, parking/driveway notes, trash or bear rules, firewood. Door codes
and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders, and you should say so in the email.

---

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Trailhead Lodges — Cosby`
   - **City, country:** `Cosby, Tennessee`
   - **Street address:** `4744 Indian Camp Creek Road, Cosby, TN` (from the "Get directions" link on their site)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Trailhead Lodging, 14 cozy treehouse-style cabins tucked in the woods of Cosby, Tennessee, managed by Smoky Woods Retreats. You're on the quiet side of the Smokies, 7 minutes from the Cosby entrance of Great Smoky Mountains National Park and about 24 minutes from downtown Gatlinburg.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 4:00 PM. Your door code is sent in your arrival message from Smoky Woods Retreats. Please don't share it.
Each lodge has a private entrance, so you can let yourself in at any time after 4:00 PM.
Your lodge is a studio with a king bed and one bathroom, and sleeps up to 4 guests.

WI-FI: Free fast Wi-Fi (500+ Mbps), with a laptop-friendly workspace. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 4 guests.
No smoking inside the cabin.
Pets are allowed on request. Please ask us before you arrive.
Please treat the lodge like your own and be considerate of guests in the neighboring lodges.

HOT TUB: Your private hot tub is on your own balcony, surrounded by the forest. It's just for your group.
If the hot tub isn't warm or something doesn't look right, message us and we'll help.

THE LODGE: Cozy living room with a fireplace and a Smart TV.
Fully equipped kitchen with oven, stove, cooking basics, coffee and a dining area.
Premium linens, towels, shampoo, body soap and shower gel are provided.
Air conditioning, heating and a ceiling fan.
Outside, there's a charcoal grill, and a communal fire pit to gather around.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
Please turn off the fireplace and the lights, close the windows and lock the door.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the cabin, call Smoky Woods Retreats on 360-306-1410 or email smokywoodsretreats@gmail.com.

LOCAL TIPS: Driving times from Trailhead Lodging:
- 7 minutes to the Cosby entrance of Great Smoky Mountains National Park, the quiet side of the park
- About 23 to 24 minutes to downtown Gatlinburg
Hiking trailheads are close by, which is how the lodges got their name.
Great Smoky Mountains National Park requires a parking tag for vehicles parked more than 15 minutes in the park. Check nps.gov/grsm before you go.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `4:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `10:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `360-306-1410` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples** (Smoky Woods doesn't publish extras or a pet fee), so tell them they set their
own prices.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Early check-in (from 1:00 PM) | $35 | Get to the hot tub sooner. Subject to the cleaning schedule. |
| Late checkout (until 12:00 PM) | $35 | One more slow morning in the woods. Subject to availability. |
| Firewood bundle | $20 | A bundle of firewood for the fire pit, waiting when you arrive. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | From 4:00 PM |
| What time is checkout? | By 10:00 AM |
| Can we bring our dog? | Pets allowed on request; ask before arrival (doesn't invent a fee) |
| Is the hot tub private? | Yes, on your own balcony, just for your group |
| Can we have a campfire? | There's a communal fire pit, plus a charcoal grill |
| How far is the national park? | 7 minutes to the Cosby entrance; parking tag needed |
| How far is Gatlinburg? | About 23–24 minutes |
| ¿Cuántas personas caben? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: up to 4 guests, king bed studio |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the hot tub answer, the Spanish answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
