# Demo guide: Your Devon Escape, Hawkins (Dartmouth, Devon)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Your Devon Escape's homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Hawkins:** a refurbished 5-bedroom Georgian townhouse in Dartmouth, their home town, sleeping 11
with an enclosed garden and garage parking for two cars. Large family groups ask the most questions,
and this listing already answers the big Dartmouth ones: where to park (garage size, town car park,
Park and Ride), the nearest beach, the nearest station and the ferry.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): yourdevonescape.co.uk/property/hawkins-dartmouth/
(description, bedrooms, kitchen, bathrooms, garden, "no pets", garage dimensions, car park and Park
and Ride, pre-arrival form, neighbourhood, Totnes station), yourdevonescape.co.uk/faqs/ (check-in
generally 3/4pm, check-out 10am, keys and key safe, late arrival, arrival details seven days before,
linen and towels, travel cot and high chair hire, no smoking or vaping, EV charging rule, no stag or
hen parties), yourdevonescape.co.uk/meet-the-team/ and /let-your-property/ (Brooke and Paul,
founders; Claire, Property Services Manager) and the homepage ("quality holiday properties, not
quantity"). The owner testimonial praising Paul's communication with guests is on /let-your-property/; the "always there to help instantly" quote in INTERNATIONAL-PROSPECTS.md wasn't found on their reviews page, so it isn't used. **Not published:** the exact arrival time for Hawkins (FAQ says "3/4pm in most of our
properties"), the street address (sent seven days before arrival), the key-safe code, the Wi-Fi
password and any guest phone number (only enquiries@yourdevonescape.co.uk and a contact form).

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Hawkins — Dartmouth`
   - **City, country:** `Dartmouth, Devon, United Kingdom`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Hawkins, a beautifully refurbished Georgian family home in Dartmouth, looked after by Your Devon Escape, the local team based right here in town. It sleeps up to eleven guests in five bedrooms, has an enclosed garden for eating outside, and is a short, level stroll from the harbour, independent shops and waterside restaurants.

ARRIVAL: Your arrival time is in your booking confirmation. Check-in is generally from 3:00 PM or 4:00 PM at most of our homes.
Your arrival details, including the full address, directions for the last part of your journey and how to collect the keys, are sent once your balance is paid, normally seven days before you arrive.
Before your stay, please complete our pre-arrival form and sign and agree to the rental conditions.
Arriving late is fine. The keys are in a secure key location. If you're unsure about anything, contact us a few days before your holiday.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your arrival details and the property guide.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 11 guests.
No pets, sorry.
No smoking of any kind, including vaping, inside the house or in the garden.
No stag or hen parties unless agreed in advance with the owner.
Electric vehicles can't be charged at the property, because there is no dedicated charging point. Please use a public charger.
If you have any problem during your stay, get in touch with us straight away so we can put it right.

THE HOUSE: On the first floor, a bright sitting room with large sash windows, two king-size bedrooms and a family bathroom with a freestanding slipper bath and separate shower.
On the top floor, two twin bedrooms, a family room with bunk beds and a single bed, and a second bathroom with a bath and separate shower.
Separate WCs on the ground and first floors.
The dining room has a large table, with French doors to the garden.
The kitchen has an electric oven, gas hob, microwave, dishwasher, washing machine and tumble dryer. There's also a smart TV and a coffee maker.
The enclosed garden has a lawn, patio, garden furniture and a BBQ.
Bed linen and towels are provided. Please bring your own beach towels.
A travel cot and high chair can be hired for a small charge if you ask us in advance. Please bring your own cot bedding.

PARKING: Hawkins has a garage for two standard-sized cars. The garage opening is 7 ft high and 13 ft 7 in wide.
If you have more cars, the main central car park is a ten-minute walk away. Pay with the PayByPhone app, or try the JustPark app for local alternatives.
From May to September, the Park and Ride is the easiest option, with regular buses through the day to and from the town centre.
Once you're parked, the town, waterfront cafés and restaurants are all within easy walking distance.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM on your day of departure.
Please leave all keys in the key safe, or as instructed in your arrival details. Thank you for staying with Your Devon Escape!

EMERGENCY: In an emergency, call 999 for fire, police, ambulance or coastguard. For urgent medical advice that isn't an emergency, call NHS 111.
For anything about the house, contact your property manager using the details in the property guide, or email enquiries@yourdevonescape.co.uk. Our office is at 1 The Sail Loft, 12 Mayors Avenue, Dartmouth.

LOCAL TIPS: Stroll along the embankment by the River Dart, then explore the independent shops, restaurants and cafés.
Take a boat trip on the River Dart, or catch the ferry across and hop on the steam train.
Take a boat up the river to Greenway, the holiday home of Agatha Christie.
Blackpool Sands is the closest beach, a five-minute drive or about an hour and a half on foot along the South West Coast Path. Dartmouth Castle is also close by.
For families, Woodlands is about five miles away, with rides, indoor and outdoor play areas and a toboggan run, whatever the weather.
Dartmouth's big events are the Dart Music Festival in May, the Port of Dartmouth Royal Regatta in August and the Dartmouth Food Festival in October.
The nearest train station is Totnes, about twelve miles away.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `Confirm` (FAQ: "generally 3/4pm"; not stated for Hawkins) |
| Check-out | `10:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival details` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | leave empty (no guest phone published; use enquiries@yourdevonescape.co.uk) |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| Will our camper van fit in the garage? | Garage is for two standard cars, 7 ft high x 13 ft 7 in wide |
| We have three cars, where can we park? | Central car park 10 min walk (PayByPhone / JustPark); Park and Ride May–September |
| Can we bring our dog? | No pets at Hawkins |
| What's the nearest beach? | Blackpool Sands, 5 minutes' drive or ~1.5 hours on the coast path |
| Can I charge my electric car? | No, there's no dedicated charger; use a public one |
| Can we hire a travel cot? | Yes, for a small charge if requested in advance; bring cot bedding |
| What time do we leave? | 10:00 AM; keys back in the key safe |
| Wie weit ist der nächste Bahnhof? (German) | Answers in German: Totnes, about twelve miles |
| What's the key-safe code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival details / hands over to the team |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to German or French) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the garage answer, the German answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
