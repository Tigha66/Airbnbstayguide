# Demo guide: Roadrunner Escapes, 5-Bedroom Villa with Private Casita (Scottsdale, AZ)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Roadrunner Escapes' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why this villa:** it's the most detailed listing on their own site: heated pool, separate casita,
dog policy with a published fee, and their 24/7 team promise written into the description. It's
exactly the kind of big-group home where guests message the most.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
roadrunnerescapes.com/villa/5-bedroom-villa-with-private-casita-heated-pool-mountain-views-scottsdale-az/,
roadrunnerescapes.com/contact/ (phone 602-345-1379, "Mon-Sun: 24 Hours") and roadrunnerescapes.com/our-team/
(Nyles Edwards and Mark Whipple, co-founders; 16 local team members; 45+ homeowners). Every fact below
comes from those pages. **Not published on their site:** check-in and check-out times, street address,
house rules beyond pets, and whether pool heating costs extra. Those are left as "Confirm" or sent to
the host, and you should say so in the email. (The booking widget on the page shows template text such
as "$39 / ticket" and a +84 phone number. Ignore it; it's not theirs.)

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `5-Bedroom Villa with Private Casita — Scottsdale`
   - **City, country:** `Scottsdale, Arizona`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.


## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `Confirm` (not published on their site; leave empty if the field needs a time) |
| Check-out | `Confirm` (not published) |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `602-345-1379` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The dog fee
is **their real published fee**; the other two prices are **demo examples**, so tell them they set
their own prices.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Bring your dog (up to 2, prior approval) | $150 per dog | Dogs are welcome with approval. Send us a photo before you book. |
| Early check-in | $50 | More pool time on arrival day. Subject to the cleaning schedule. |
| Late checkout | $50 | One more slow morning by the pool. Subject to availability. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | It's in your booking confirmation; offers to check with the team (no invented time) |
| Can we bring our dog? | Yes, up to 2 dogs with prior approval, $150 each; send a photo before booking |
| Is the pool heated? | Yes, a private heated pool (doesn't invent a heating fee) |
| Where can grandma sleep away from the noise? | The private casita, with its own entrance and bathroom |
| Is there somewhere to work? | High-speed Wi-Fi and a laptop-friendly workspace |
| How far is Old Town Scottsdale? | Minutes away |
| ¿Hay lavadora? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: yes, washer and dryer |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the team |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the dog answer, the Spanish answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
