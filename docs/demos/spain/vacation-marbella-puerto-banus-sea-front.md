# Demo guide: Vacation Marbella, Puerto Banús Sea Front (Marbella, Spain)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Vacation Marbella's homes *before* contacting them,
so the first message says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Puerto Banús Sea Front:** a 130 m², 3-bedroom, 5th-floor apartment for 6 by the beach in Puerto
Banús, with 86+ reviews (4.83). Its listing publishes every rule and fee a guest asks about: check-in
4–9 PM, early check-in €100, late checkout €40, pets up to 10 kg, parking €10/day, the pool closing on
1 October, damage fees and the no-groups-under-30 rule. One review even says "we were not made aware of
the pool being closed", exactly the kind of question the concierge answers before it becomes a
complaint. Their guest service runs 11:00–21:00, so nights are uncovered.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): vacationmarbella.com/vacation-rentals/puerto-banus-sea-front/
(description, floor plan, amenities, house rules, extra-cost services, distances, damage costs) and
vacationmarbella.com/faq/ (check-in at the office or €25 meet-at-property, smoking on terraces, Wi-Fi,
lost and found, office address, hours Mon–Sun 11:00–21:00). Every fact below comes from those pages.
**Conflicts / unverified:** the late check-in fee is €25 cash "from 9pm" on the listing, but €40
(9 PM–midnight) / €55 (midnight–5 AM) in the FAQ, so the manual gives no amount; the FAQ says parking is
"included in the quotation" while the listing says €10/day (manual uses the listing); amenities list both
"Pets Accepted" and "Pets Not Allowed", while the house rules say small pets up to 10 kg (€10/day), used
in the manual; the FAQ allows smoking on terraces, the listing says "No smoking" and fines €300 for
smoking "in the property". No tourist licence and no street address are published (the address comes
in their Property Guide after booking). Key handover details and the Wi-Fi password are placeholders.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Puerto Banús Sea Front — Marbella`
   - **City, country:** `Puerto Banús, Marbella, Spain`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (sent after booking).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```

Forgot something? Tell us within 48 hours of leaving and we'll do our best to find it and ship it to you (shipping is charged).

We can also arrange a private chef, body and face treatments at the apartment, babysitters, boat tours and fishing charters, golf green fees, car rental, and private tours to Seville, Granada, Córdoba, Ronda and Mijas, including wine and olive oil tours.
```
