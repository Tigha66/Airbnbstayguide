# Demo guide: Lucky Savannah, General Lachlan McIntosh House (Savannah, GA)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Lucky Savannah's homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why the McIntosh House:** it's Lucky Savannah's showpiece. Escapia (their software vendor) reports it
was named a **2026 Vrbo Vacation Rental of the Year**. It's also full of guest questions waiting to happen:
a 1770 house, a separate garden apartment, an elevator, and a big private driveway in the Historic District.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- luckysavannah.com/vacation-rentals/general-lachlan-mcintosh-house (description, parking, beds, amenities, certificate numbers)
- luckysavannah.com/guest-info/faq (check-in/out, noise, smoking, age, contact and after-hours numbers)
- luckysavannah.com/pages/rental-policies (early/late fees, pets, no smoking, age, Wi-Fi caveat)
- escapia.com/resources/articles/lucky-savannah-vrbo-vacation-rental-of-the-year-escapia/ (award, Corey Jones as owner, 400+ homes)

Every fact below comes from those pages. Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders.
**Check:** the page gives two parking figures ("7 private spaces, tandem" in a photo caption and "up to 9
small to mid-size or 6 large vehicles" in the description); the kit uses the description. It also says
both "built in 1770" and "1764"; the kit uses 1770. The page shows two Savannah certificate numbers,
**SVR-00980** and **SVR-01008** (probably main house and garden apartment).

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `General Lachlan McIntosh House — Savannah`
   - **City, country:** `Savannah, Georgia`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (the page only says East Oglethorpe Avenue).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to the General Lachlan McIntosh House, cared for by Lucky Savannah. Built in 1770, it is considered the oldest brick residence in Georgia. The first constitutional session of the Georgia Legislature was held here in 1783, and George Washington himself visited in 1791. Today it's a beautifully renovated 5-bedroom home on East Oglethorpe Avenue, in the heart of the Historic District.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is at 4:00 PM. Your entry instructions and door code are in your arrival message from Lucky Savannah. Please don't share them.
Need an early check-in or a late check-out? Message us the day before. Early check-ins and late check-outs can be purchased for a small fee from 24 hours before, if the schedule allows. We can't accommodate same-day requests.
When you arrive, please let us know straight away if anything is amiss.

WI-FI: Every Lucky Savannah home has free Wi-Fi. The network name and password are in your arrival message.
Internet and cable in Savannah can occasionally be unreliable. If it drops, tell us and we'll do our best to get it back.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 10 guests. Having more guests than the home sleeps isn't permitted by city regulation, licensing and insurance.
Unfortunately we can't host bachelor or bachelorette parties or traveling singles in this home.
No events without our prior permission.
No smoking of any kind, inside or outside, including courtyards, decks and balconies. A $500 fee applies.
Guests must be at least 25 (28 during the St. Patrick's Day season), except children with their parents.
Dogs are only allowed in homes listed as pet friendly. Please ask us before bringing one.
Please be kind to the neighbors and don't use the patio, porches or other outdoor areas after 10 PM.
Report any accidental damage to us before you check out.

THE HOUSE: Four suites upstairs, each with a king bed, its own en-suite bathroom with a walk-in shower, and its own temperature control. George Washington's room is the guest favorite.
An elevator (or the stairs) takes you to the third floor, where a private patio overlooks some of Savannah's oldest buildings.
The historic winding staircase is roped off for your safety.
The main kitchen has high-end appliances. The dining table seats six, and there's a wet bar just off the dining room for mixing drinks.
A half-bath is just off the living room. There's also a washer and dryer.
The garden-level apartment is outside and a few short steps down. It has its own full kitchen, a queen bed, an en-suite bathroom and the original fireplace from the 1700s, where the household's meals were once cooked.
We provide towels and bed linens, coffee for one pot, filters, paper towels, toilet paper and dishwasher detergent to get you started. Please don't take the towels to the beach.

PARKING: There's a large private driveway behind the home with parking for up to 9 small to mid-size cars or 6 large vehicles. You may need to park tandem. This is a rarity in the downtown Historic District!

CHECKOUT: Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
Please take out the trash and start the dishwasher before you go.
Let us know about any loss or damage before you leave.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
Our Guest Experience Team is based in the downtown Historic District, from 8 AM to midnight daily. Call (912) 495-8582, text (912) 616-5696 or email frontdesk@luckysavannah.com.
After midnight, for a maintenance emergency (power, water, heating or air conditioning), call (912) 495-8582. For anything else after midnight, email or text and we'll reply next morning.

LOCAL TIPS: Monterey Square is just two blocks away.
Leopold's, Savannah's most famous ice cream parlor, is a few short blocks away.
For a fine dinner, try Husk on Oglethorpe Avenue. For a unique brunch, The Collins Quarter is also off Oglethorpe.
The Historic District is best explored on foot, so you can leave the car in the driveway.
```

## Step 3: Property details

| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |

| Host phone | `(912) 495-8582` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |

| How many cars can we park? | Private driveway: up to 9 small/mid-size or 6 large, may be tandem |
| Can we do a bachelorette weekend here? | No, not in this home |
| Did George Washington stay here? | Yes, he visited in 1791 (his room is the guest favorite) |
| Can we sit in the courtyard at 11 PM? | No outdoor areas after 10 PM |
| The AC stopped working at 1 AM, who do I call? | (912) 495-8582 (after-midnight maintenance emergency) |
| Can I smoke on the balcony? | No smoking anywhere, $500 fee |
| Quelle est l'heure de départ ? (French) | Answers in French: 11 AM, take out trash and start the dishwasher |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** and the **Extras** tab.
