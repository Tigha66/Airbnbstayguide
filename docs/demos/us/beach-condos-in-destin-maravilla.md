# Demo guide: Beach Condos in Destin, Maravilla 1207 (Miramar Beach, FL)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Beach Condos in Destin's condos *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Maravilla 1207:** Beach Condos in Destin say they have "the largest number of rentals at Maravilla"
(their Maravilla page lists about 30 units), and Maravilla is a gated resort with shared pools, hot tub,
private beach and the same gate, parking and pet rules for every unit. One guide, with the bed layout
swapped, fits dozens of their listings. Unit 1207 has the most complete public property page.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- https://www.beachcondosindestin.com/property/maravilla-1207/ (unit layout, sleeps 8, amenities, beach service)
- https://www.beachcondosindestin.com/neighborhood/maravilla/ (resort amenities, nearby restaurants, unit list)
- https://www.beachcondosindestin.com/rental-policy/ (check-in/out, early check-in, smoking, parking, gate codes, pets, trash, AC, fridge, Wi-Fi, beach rules, fees)
- https://www.beachcondosindestin.com/about-beach-condos-in-destin/ (owners Renee and Laura Clayton, team, beach chair dates, ~72% repeat guests)
- https://www.beachcondosindestin.com/contact-us/ (phone, email)
- https://www.beachcondosindestin.com/state-parks-near-destin-miramar-beach/ (local tips)

Every fact below comes from those pages. Things only the host knows (door code, gate code, Wi-Fi
network and password, exact street address) are left as "in your arrival message", and you should say
so in the email. Not verified: the beach chair season is "March 1 – October 31" on the 1207 page but
"March 15 – Oct 31" on the About page, so the manual just says "in season, through October 31".
The site doesn't publish a pet fee, the condo's exact address, or a nearest hospital, so none are used.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Maravilla 1207 — Miramar Beach`
   - **City, country:** `Miramar Beach, Florida`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Maravilla 1207, a newly updated 3-bedroom, 2-bath condo in Building 1 of the gated Maravilla Resort in Miramar Beach. The private Maravilla beach is less than a football field away. Beach Condos in Destin is a small, family-owned company run by Renee and Laura Clayton, and we're only five minutes away if you need us.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is any time after 3:00 PM (Central time). Maravilla has self check-in, so there's no front desk to visit.
Your door code and the gate code for Maravilla's secured parking are sent in your arrival message once your rental payment is complete. Please don't share them.
In rare cases a problem can delay check-in until 5:00 PM. If that happens we'll tell you as soon as possible.
From March through October it is nearly impossible to offer early check-in, so please plan to arrive at or after 3:00 PM.
One tip for your first evening. The fridge starts empty and takes a while to cool a big grocery load. Bring a cooler for the trip, put things that spoil easily in the freezer, and keep the fridge door closed. If it still isn't cold the next day, call us.

WI-FI: Wireless internet is included and the condo is remote-work ready. The network name and password are in your arrival message.
Internet and TV come through the cable company. If the internet stops working, call us first. Sometimes we'll need you to call the cable company, because they need someone in the condo to fix it.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 8 guests. Occupancy limits are strict. If they are exceeded, guests will be asked to check out with no refund.
We don't rent to vacationing students or young adults under 25. The person who booked is responsible for the whole group following these rules.
No smoking inside the condo. Evidence of smoking inside means a fine of $250 to $1,000. If you smoke on the balcony, use an ashtray, keep the balcony door closed, and never drop or throw cigarette butts off the balcony (that's a $500 county fine).
Please be respectful of other guests at the resort, especially in the evenings.
Don't leave the balcony door open. It creates a wind tunnel that can slam the front door, and it makes the air conditioning freeze up.
Shake sand out of clothes and towels before washing them, because sand damages the washer and dryer. Don't wash colored beach towels or clothing with the condo's white linens.
Trash goes in the dumpsters or trash chutes. Never leave bags outside the door or in stairwells, as there's a $100 fine each time.

PETS: This is a pet-friendly condo, with prior approval. No more than two pets.
Dogs must be up to date on shots and flea prevention, bathed and groomed before arrival, and on a leash at all times outside the condo.
Walk dogs only in Maravilla's pet area, and always carry a bag. Bag the waste and put it in an outside dumpster, because the condo association fines $100 if it isn't picked up.
Pets are not allowed on the beach or around the pools. Don't leave a dog alone on the balcony, and please keep pets off the furniture and beds (or bring covers). A vacuum is provided for pet hair.

THE CONDO: Sleeps 8. The master bedroom has a king bed and its own bathroom with a walk-in shower. The guest bedroom has a king bed and its own bathroom with a shower and tub. The bunk room has twin-over-twin bunks, great for kids, and the living room has a queen sleeper sofa.
Second-floor balcony with a side view of the Gulf.
Fully stocked kitchen, washer and dryer in the condo, and three flat-screen TVs with cable.
Bed linens and bath towels are provided. Please bring your own beach towels and a hair dryer, as hair dryers aren't supplied.
We leave a starter supply of toilet paper, soap and paper towels. Please bring laundry detergent and any extra paper products you'll need.

THE RESORT: Maravilla has 2.5 acres of private, gated beach access, the largest in the area, with a ramp for easy access.
In season, through October 31, your stay includes beach service with four chairs and two umbrellas set up on the beach. It depends on the beach contractor's schedule and the weather.
Tents and canopies are not allowed on the beach.
The resort has two community pools (the clubhouse pool is heated in cooler months) and a hot tub. Pool rules are posted at each pool, and you swim at your own risk.
Also at Maravilla: tennis and pickleball courts, shuffleboard, a basketball goal, cornhole, BBQ grills, a shaded picnic area, a fitness center, and a clubhouse with a kitchen and Wi-Fi for gatherings. Bikes, kayaks and paddleboards can be rented onsite, and evening beach bonfires are available.

PARKING: Maravilla is a gated, secured parking complex. The gate code is in your arrival message.
A parking pass comes with the condo, and every vehicle must display it. Park only in single-car spaces.
RVs, trailers, boats, campers and similar vehicles can't be parked at Maravilla for any reason. Golf carts are no longer allowed at Maravilla except for owners.
Please leave the parking pass in the condo when you leave.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM (Central time). This matters for our cleaners and the next guests.
Before you go, please run the dishwasher and the garbage disposal, don't leave dirty dishes or pans, and take all trash to the dumpster or trash chute.
Leave the parking pass and any keys in the condo. There's a $50 fee for keys that aren't returned.
Check drawers and under the beds for your belongings. If we have to ship something back, there's a $25 fee plus packing and shipping.
Let us know about any damage before you leave. Thank you for staying with us!

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the condo, call Renee or Laura on (850) 269-3342 (toll free (888) 251-5214), or email beachcondosindestin@gmail.com. There's no front desk at the resort, but we're five minutes away.
If something isn't working, please call as soon as you notice it, ideally at the start of your stay. Repair visits happen between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM, or anytime for an emergency repair.

LOCAL TIPS: You can walk to Pompano Joe's, Captain Dave's and Kenny D's, and there are miles of paths for walking and biking along the coast.
Regatta Bay golf course is about 2.5 miles away.
Silver Sands outlet mall and Destin Commons are close by for shopping.
Nearby you'll also find deep sea fishing, boat and wave runner rentals, dolphin trips, water parks, go-karts and outdoor concerts.
For a day trip, Henderson Beach State Park has white sand and big dunes, and Grayton Beach State Park, about 35 to 40 minutes east, has rare coastal dune lakes that are great for kayaking.
```

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

are **demo examples**: Beach Condos in Destin doesn't publish early check-in or late checkout fees, so
tell them they set their own prices. Their policy says early check-in is nearly impossible March to
October, so the early check-in extra is limited to November to February.

|---|---|---|
