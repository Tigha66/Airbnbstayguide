# Demo guide: North Wales Holiday Cottages, Sandbanks (Deganwy, North Wales)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of North Wales Holiday Cottages' homes *before*
contacting them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Sandbanks:** a ground-floor seafront apartment in Deganwy, their home patch, looking out over
the Conwy Estuary to Anglesey and the Carneddau. The listing is unusually detailed (room-by-room
inventory, distances to every restaurant, EV and pet rules), so the guide answers a lot, and the
location makes great local tips: Conwy Castle, Llandudno, the Wales Coast Path on the doorstep.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
northwalesholidaycottages.co.uk/holiday-cottages/sandbanks/4991 (property code NW567: description,
max 3 including infants, rooms and inventory, garden and courtyard, off-street parking for 1, no pets,
no EV charging and nearest fast charger at Lidl Llandudno Junction 1.4 miles, linen and towels
included, Wi-Fi, check in 15:00, check out 10:00, local facilities with distances, Deganwy area guide,
Conwy and Llandudno recommendations), northwalesholidaycottages.co.uk/booking-conditions (key
collection arrangements and owner/key holder contact sent after final payment, max occupancy, no
smoking, vaping or candles, leave clean and tidy with rubbish recycled, keys, report damage, contact
the owner/key holder first, then the agency), northwalesholidaycottages.co.uk/contact (office hours;
out of hours, calls go to an answering machine) and /about-us (established 1966, "the oldest
independent holiday letting agency in all of Wales"). **Not published:** the street address,
key-safe or key collection details, the Wi-Fi password, and the owner/key holder's name and number.
**Note:** the prospect list quotes "oldest independent holiday letting agency specialising in North
Wales"; their About page now says "in all of Wales".

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Sandbanks — Deganwy`
   - **City, country:** `Deganwy, Conwy, United Kingdom`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Sandbanks, a ground-floor apartment right on the seafront in Deganwy, at the entrance to the Conwy Estuary, looked after by North Wales Holiday Cottages. From the front you can see the Irish Sea, Anglesey, Conwy Mountain and the Carneddau mountains, and the Wales Coast Path is just a few metres away.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 3:00 PM, unless your travel directions say otherwise.
Your key collection arrangements, travel directions and the name and phone number of the owner or key holder are sent once your final payment is made. Please keep them on your phone.
There's a step halfway along the entrance porch, and a few steps inside the apartment, so take care with luggage.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your arrival details.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 3 people, including infants. There isn't room for a cot in either bedroom.
No pets, sorry.
Smoking, vaping and candles are not allowed in the apartment.
Please report any damage or breakages to the owner or key holder as soon as possible.
Keys are your responsibility. Please return them to where you collected them.

THE APARTMENT: Bedroom 1 has a double bed, a dressing table and a hair dryer. Bedroom 2 has a large single bed, 4 ft wide, and a built-in wardrobe.
The bathroom has a bath with a shower over it and a heated towel rail.
The lounge has a sofa, an armchair, an electric fire and a TV, with a dining table for three.
The kitchen has an electric hob and oven, microwave, kettle, toaster, fridge, freezer, dishwasher and washing machine.
At the back there's an enclosed lawned garden with a table and chairs, and a courtyard with more seating.
Bed linen and towels are included.

PARKING: Off-street parking for one car.
The apartment can't be used to charge electric cars. The nearest public fast charger is at the Lidl supermarket in Llandudno Junction, 1.4 miles away.
Deganwy has a railway station, and you can walk, drive, take the bus or the train to both Llandudno and Conwy.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM.
Please leave the apartment clean and tidy, put your rubbish and recycling in the right bins, and return the keys to where you collected them.
Diolch, and thank you for staying with North Wales Holiday Cottages!

EMERGENCY: In an emergency, call 999 for fire, police, ambulance or coastguard. For urgent medical advice that isn't an emergency, call NHS 111.
If there's a problem with the apartment, first contact the owner or key holder (their number is in your arrival details). If they can't help, call North Wales Holiday Cottages on 01492 582492 or email info@northwalesholidaycottages.co.uk.
Our office is open Monday to Friday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM and Saturday 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM, and closed on Sundays and bank holidays. Out of hours, calls go to an answering machine, so leave a message and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

LOCAL TIPS: Restaurants within a short walk include Nikki Ip's Chinese (200 m), Jai Ho Indian (215 m), the dog-friendly Olive Grove Café (240 m) and Paysanne French restaurant (370 m). Harbour View Fish and Chips is 0.8 miles away, the Castle View pub 0.9 miles and the Co-op 1 mile.
The Wales Coast Path runs along the shore, a quiet, traffic-free walk or cycle to Llandudno or Conwy.
Deganwy beach is shingle at the southern end and sandy further north, and dogs are allowed all year. A small seafront café opens in summer, with public toilets near the railway station.
For a harder walk, climb the Vardre behind Deganwy, a double-summit hill and the site of an ancient castle older than Conwy's, with views over Llandudno, Conwy, Anglesey and Puffin Island.
In Conwy, visit Conwy Castle, a UNESCO World Heritage site begun in 1283, take a guided walking tour of the town, and try Parisella's ice cream or Dylan's waterfront restaurant. RSPB Conwy is great for birdwatching.
In Llandudno, ride the cable car to the top of the Great Orme, or visit the Penderyn whisky distillery.
Snowdonia National Park and Anglesey are a short drive away.
```

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | From 3:00 PM unless the travel directions say otherwise |
| Can we bring a travel cot for our baby? | No room for a cot; maximum 3 people including infants |
| Can I charge my electric car? | No; nearest fast charger at Lidl, Llandudno Junction, 1.4 miles |
| Can we bring the dog? | No pets |
| Where's the nearest fish and chips? | Harbour View, 0.8 miles |
| Can we walk to Conwy? | Yes, along the Wales Coast Path (or bus/train) |
| The boiler's broken on a Sunday, who do I call? | The owner/key holder first; agency answering machine out of hours |
| Wie weit ist es zur Burg Conwy? (German) | Answers in German: Conwy is reachable on foot along the coast path, or by bus or train |
| What's the key-safe code? | Doesn't make one up; key collection details are in the arrival details / hands over |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to German or Welsh if available) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the Sunday answer, the German answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
