# Demo guide: Cactus Vacation Rentals, Heated Pool 1-Bedroom Condo in Old Town (Scottsdale, AZ)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Cactus Vacation Rentals' homes *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why this condo:** it's in a complex where Cactus manages several apartments (the listing says "We
have other apartments in this complex"), so one guide can be reused across those units. It also has
the questions Old Town guests always ask: the year-round heated resort pool and hot tub, coin
laundry, the assigned parking spot and the free trolley.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): their direct booking site
book.cactusvacationrentals.com/property/old-town-scottsdale-condo-w-spa-pool ("Heated Pool! 1 bedroom
in Old Town S'dale": description, guest access, "Other Details to Note", getting around) and the
booking site's About section. Every fact below comes from that page. **Caveats:** their main website
www.cactusvacationrentals.com returned **502 Bad Gateway** on 9 October 2026, so the phone number
480-666-1337 comes from an archived copy of their homepage (January 2026); confirm it before you
send. **Not published:** check-in and check-out times (only "24 hr check-in with smart lock"), house
rules (pets, smoking, quiet hours) and the address. Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as
placeholders, and you should say so in the email.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Old Town Heated Pool Condo — Scottsdale`
   - **City, country:** `Scottsdale, Arizona`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to your Old Town Scottsdale condo, managed by Cactus Vacation Rentals. It's a 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom apartment with a queen bed for up to 2 guests, in a complex with a large resort-style pool that's heated all year round. Think of us as your personal concierge, whether you need directions or just our favorite restaurant around the corner.

ARRIVAL: Your check-in time is in your booking confirmation. Check-in is 24-hour self check-in with a smart lock, so you can arrive at nearly any time of day or night. Your door code is sent in your arrival message from Cactus Vacation Rentals. Please don't share it.
Fresh sheets, towels and linens are ready for you.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi in the apartment, plus a laptop-friendly workspace and Cox cable TV. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 2 guests.
Please keep all communication on the booking platform you booked through. For an emergency, or if you need us right away, call or text the phone number we sent you.
For anything not covered here, message us and we'll answer quickly.

POOL: The complex has a huge, luxurious resort-style swimming pool, heated all year round, and a hot tub.
There's also a gym and exercise room on the property.

THE APARTMENT: One bedroom with a queen bed and one bathroom.
Toiletries are provided, including shampoo, soap and body wash, plus bath towels.
The apartment has heating in the winter months and air conditioning in the summer months. It runs one or the other depending on the time of year.
Laundry is shared, in the building. The machines take coins or a credit card.

PARKING: You have 1 assigned, covered parking spot. Extra parking is available on the street for free.
You don't need a rental car. Most things are within walking distance or a quick Uber or Lyft ride away.

CHECKOUT: Your check-out time is in your booking confirmation. Thank you for staying with us!
Please lock the door behind you when you leave.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything urgent about the apartment, call or text the phone number Cactus Vacation Rentals sent you with your booking.

LOCAL TIPS: You're really close to the entertainment district for nightlife, Scottsdale Fashion Square for shopping, golf, and Scottsdale Stadium for baseball Spring Training.
The City of Scottsdale runs free trolley rides that take you all around town. Visit scottsdaleaz.gov/trolley for routes and times.
Want to go bike riding? There's a bike rental shop right across the street.
Travelling with a group? We have other apartments in this complex. Message us and we'll send you the links.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `Confirm` (not published; "24 hr check-in with smart lock") |
| Check-out | `Confirm` (not published) |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `480-666-1337` (from the archived homepage; confirm) |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples** (Cactus doesn't publish extras for this condo; the pool is heated at no extra
cost), so tell them they set their own prices.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Early check-in | $40 | Start your Old Town getaway sooner. Subject to the cleaning schedule. |
| Late checkout | $40 | One more slow morning by the pool. Subject to availability. |
| Welcome grocery stock-up | $60 | Arrive to coffee, breakfast basics and snacks already in the kitchen. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| Is the pool heated in January? | Yes, heated all year round; hot tub too |
| Where do I park? | 1 assigned covered spot; extra free street parking |
| Do we need a rental car? | No; walk, Uber/Lyft, or the free Scottsdale trolley |
| Is there a washing machine? | Shared coin/credit card laundry in the building |
| We arrive at 1 AM, is that OK? | 24-hour self check-in with smart lock; code in arrival message |
| Can I rent a bike? | Bike rental shop right across the street |
| How far is Spring Training? | Really close to Scottsdale Stadium |
| ¿Hay gimnasio? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: yes, a gym/exercise room on the property |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.
