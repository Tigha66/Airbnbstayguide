# Demo guide: Andaluz Apartments, Apartment MDN06 (Nerja, Spain)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Andaluz Apartments' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first message says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why MDN06:** their 3-bedroom top-floor apartment for 6, with a private roof terrace and fixed
barbecue, sea and mountain views, and a seasonal communal pool. Andaluz Apartments publishes unusually
detailed rental conditions (check-in/out, late-checkout fee, electricity allowance in winter, linen
charges, rubbish, deposit, licences), which are exactly the questions their guests ask (the site is in English, Dutch and Spanish, and they
sell long winter stays). The owner, Monique, runs it personally from Nerja.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): andaluzapartments.com/apartment/apartment-mdn06/
(description, amenities, 2026 prices, check-in/out), andaluzapartments.com/lease-terms/ (rental
conditions, parking, pool, check-out routine, licences A/MA/01388, A/MA/01504 and VFT/MA/57062),
andaluzapartments.com/extra-services/ (airport taxi €85, minibus €125, intermediate cleaning €80),
andaluzapartments.com/enjoy-nerja/ (local tips), andaluzapartments.com/about-us/ (Monique; apartments open since 2014,
run from Nerja) and andaluzapartments.com/contact/ (WhatsApp +34 650 853 146). Every fact below
comes from those pages. **Conflicts / unverified:** the MDN06 page says "parking free in the street",
but the lease terms list MDN06 among apartments with free covered parking in the complex, so the manual
says "ask us" for the covered space; the pool dates on the page are for 2024 ("June until the end of
September"); the company licences are listed collectively, not per apartment, so none is put in the
manual; the exact address/area in Nerja isn't published. Door codes, key handover and Wi-Fi password are
placeholders.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Apartment MDN06 — Roof Terrace & Pool, Nerja`
   - **City, country:** `Nerja, Spain`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (not published).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Apartment MDN06, a spacious top-floor apartment with two terraces, including a private roof terrace with a fixed barbecue and spectacular views over the sea, the mountains, the town and the pool. It is run personally by Monique and the Andaluz Apartments team, who live in Nerja and are happy to help with advice, things to see, or translating.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 3:00 PM, any day of the week. Your arrival details and access information are in your arrival message.
Arriving earlier? Whenever possible we are flexible with early arrivals, so just ask.
Need a ride from Málaga Airport? We can arrange a taxi for €85 per single ride (standard taxi, up to 4 people and 4 suitcases) or a minibus for €125 per single ride. Mention it under "requirements" when you book, or ask us.

GUEST REGISTRATION: Spanish law requires holiday rentals to register the identity documents of all guests with the authorities. That is why we ask for passport or ID details for everyone in your group. Thank you for sending them before you arrive.

WI-FI: Good, free fibre Wi-Fi. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

THE APARTMENT: Three bedrooms with 6 single beds (90 x 200 cm) and air conditioning in every room, and two bathrooms with bath and shower, bidet and a hairdryer. For up to 6 guests.
- Kitchen with fridge-freezer, oven, microwave, coffee machine, kettle, toaster, citrus press and kitchen linen, plus a washing machine.
- Living room with TV with international channels and air conditioning with heating.
- Terrace next to the living room with a garden set for 6 and a parasol.
- Private roof terrace with a lounge set, pergola, 4 sunloungers and a fixed barbecue.
- Iron and ironing board, a small safe, a shopping trolley and cleaning supplies.
- A cot and a high chair are available on request.
Bed, bath and kitchen linen are provided. The apartment is not cleaned during your stay, but for stays of 8 nights or more your bed linen is changed. An intermediate cleaning, with fresh kitchen, bed and bath linen, can be booked for €80 at any time.

POOL: The complex has a large seasonal communal pool with a children's pool and a sunbathing area with sunbeds. The opening dates are set by the community each year (it has been open from June until the end of September). Use of the pool is free and at your own risk.

ELECTRICITY: From 1 April to 31 October, normal water and electricity are included, with 105 kWh of air conditioning per week free. Above that, it is €0.45 per kWh.
From 1 November to 1 April, electricity is not included. You get 225 kWh per 30 days free (7.5 kWh per day), and extra use is €0.45 per kWh, deducted from your deposit. There is a meter in the apartment so you can check your use. Gas bottles are not included.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests. The apartment is licensed by the Junta de Andalucía for this number, so extra people cannot be allowed in.
Smoking is not allowed. If it is, a €100 extra cleaning fee is charged.
No pets.
Please take care of the linen and don't take it outside. Stubborn stains (make-up, hair dye, tanning cream, suntan oil, nail polish and similar) are charged, for example €7.50 for a big towel or €15 for a duvet cover.

PARKING: Parking is free in the street. The complex also has free covered parking (one car per apartment), so ask us whether a space is available for MDN06.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is before 10:00 AM. Late checkout is possible whenever we can; leaving after 12:00 noon costs €35.
You don't need to strip the beds, wash towels or clean. Please just empty the apartment completely, with no food, drinks or rubbish bags left behind. The rubbish containers are right near the apartment. If our staff have to remove your rubbish, it costs €15 per bag.
If you like, we will say goodbye personally on the day. If you leave before 10:00 AM, we'll say goodbye the day before.
Your €150 deposit is refunded within 7 days after check-out, once the apartment has been checked.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency in Spain, call 112 (police, ambulance, fire). Operators speak English.
For anything about the apartment, call or WhatsApp Andaluz Apartments on +34 650 853 146, or email info@andaluzapartments.com. We live in Nerja, so we're close by.

LOCAL TIPS: Nerja is the easternmost town on the Costa del Sol, in the Axarquía region, with around 320 sunny days a year and mild winters.
- Balcón de Europa, the famous viewpoint in the centre, with a pedestrian promenade and a magnificent sea view.
- Burriana beach, a lively wide beach with a nice boulevard and many beach bars.
- Cuevas de Nerja (the Nerja Caves), in Maro.
- Río Chillar, a spectacular walk through a riverbed, with a waterfall at the start.
- Los Acantilados natural park, for horse riding, mountain biking and Nordic walking.
- Nerja market on Tuesday mornings, 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM, for souvenirs, clothes and food.
Ask us for our free e-book with 10 fun day trips near Nerja, doable 365 days a year.
```
