# Demo guide: Orlando Regional Property Management, 8887 Geneve Ct (Kissimmee, FL)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Orlando Regional Property Management's homes
*before* contacting them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why this property:** Orlando Regional Property Management (ORPM) manages 47 vacation homes around
Kissimmee, and Windsor at Westside Resort is their biggest single community — this 5-bedroom townhome
is one of more than a dozen ORPM listings there. One guide fits most of those homes, so it's the most
convincing demo.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): orpminc.com (ORPM's own site) and its property page
orpminc.com/listing/396917 ("Cozy 5BR Resort Retreat Near Attractions!", 8887 Geneve Ct), plus ORPM's
partner profile on experiencekissimmee.com/places-to-stay/vacation-home-rentals/orlando-regional-vacation-homes
(47 homes, phone number, concierge desk). Every fact below comes from those pages. ORPM doesn't publish
a public email address, so Step 7 uses their listing enquiry form or their phone number. Things only the
host knows (the private door code, Wi-Fi password) are left as placeholders, and you should say so in
the email.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `8887 Geneve Ct — Windsor at Westside Resort`
   - **City, country:** `Kissimmee, Florida`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```

The home sleeps up to 10 guests and has its own private pool, with access to the resort's clubhouse, pools and fitness center. If this home isn't available for your dates, ask the ORPM team about their other homes nearby. They manage more than 30 in the area.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 4:00 PM. Your private door code is sent in your arrival message from Orlando Regional Property Management. Please don't share it with anyone outside your group.
Parking is right at your door, with space for one car in the driveway and additional parking nearby.

WI-FI: Free high-speed Wi-Fi and cable TV are included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 10 guests.
No smoking anywhere on the property.
No pets.
No events or parties.
Children are welcome, and the private pool has a child safety fence, but please supervise children around the pool at all times.

HOME AND KITCHEN: This townhome is about 2,265 square feet, with 5 bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms. There is one king bed, two queen beds and four twin beds.
Central air conditioning keeps the home comfortable in the Florida heat.
The kitchen is fully equipped for cooking, with a dishwasher, microwave and coffee maker.
A washer and dryer are in the unit, so you can pack light.
Relax in the cozy family room with a big-screen TV, or unwind outside on the furnished patio with sun loungers.
An iron, ironing board and hair dryers are provided in the suites.

Once turned on, the heater needs about 24 hours to fully warm up. The spa heats 10 to 20 degrees warmer than the pool when the jets are running.
When the outside temperature drops below 60 degrees Fahrenheit, the heater automatically shuts off to protect the equipment, so the pool and spa may not reach their usual temperature in colder weather.
If the pool heat you paid for isn't working and the pool isn't warm by checkout, you'll be refunded the pool heat fee.
There is no separate hot tub. The pool and spa share one heating system.
A BBQ grill is also available to rent for a small fee. Ask the ORPM team to add it.

PARKING: There is space for one car in the driveway, with additional parking available nearby in the resort. Windsor at Westside Resort is a gated community.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us.
Please lock the door behind you when you leave.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the home, call Orlando Regional Property Management at (321) 284-8569.

LOCAL TIPS: Windsor at Westside Resort has its own clubhouse, pools and a fitness center for guests to use during their stay.
Nearby stores and essentials:

- ESPN Wide World of Sports, about 12 minutes away.
- Orlando Vineland Premium Outlets, about 20 minutes away.
- SeaWorld Orlando, about 25 minutes away.
- Universal Studios and The Mall at Millenia, both about 30 minutes away.
```

| Name | Price | Description |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.
