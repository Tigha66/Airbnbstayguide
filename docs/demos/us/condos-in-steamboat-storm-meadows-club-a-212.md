# Demo guide: Condos-In-Steamboat, Storm Meadows Club A 212 (Steamboat Springs, CO)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Condos-In-Steamboat's units *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Storm Meadows Club A 212:** Storm Meadows is the complex where they manage the most units
(Club A 212, Club C 316, Christie Base 350, East Slopeside, plus the Trails at Storm Meadows
townhomes), so the shared parts of this guide (ski-in/ski-out trail, Club Spa pool and hot tub, ski
shuttle, coin laundry) fit several listings. Its page is one of the few with a full "Additional
information" list, and their Policies page gives the company-wide rules.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- condosinsteamboat.com/vacation-rentals/ski-in-ski-out/storm-meadows-club-a/ (unit details, Club Spa, shuttle hours, no pets, no smoking, max 4, Wi-Fi, ski locker, grill)
- condosinsteamboat.com/about-us/policies/ (check-in 4pm, check-out 10am, $1,500 smoking and pet charges, common pool/hot tub seasonal closures, parking, occupancy, no housekeeping during stay)
- condosinsteamboat.com/area-guide/ (elevations, free city bus, Fish Creek Falls, Strawberry Park Hot Springs, Yampa River Core Trail, dining guide in each unit)
- condosinsteamboat.com/contact-us/ and /about-us/ (Kim and Dan Filler, info@condosinsteamboat.com, hours 8am–8pm Mountain Time, home office)

Not verified: early check-in or late checkout policy (not published, so the extras below are
examples), the Club Spa opening hours, and the number of parking spaces for this unit (their policy
says to ask Kim). Door code and Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Storm Meadows Club A 212 — Steamboat Springs`
   - **City, country:** `Steamboat Springs, Colorado`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Storm Meadows Club A 212, a newly renovated 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom ski-in/ski-out condo perched high on the hill at Storm Meadows, cared for by Kim and Dan at Condos in Steamboat. You're steps from the Club Spa, and a short trail takes you straight to the slopes.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is at 4:00 PM. Your door code and arrival directions are sent in your arrival message from Condos in Steamboat. Please don't share the code.
The condo is on the ground floor, so there are no stairs with your bags.
Steamboat sits at nearly 7,000 feet. Drink plenty of water on your first day and take it easy.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Sleeps a maximum of 4 people. This is an association rule and a City of Steamboat Springs fire code rule, so please don't exceed it.
Absolutely no smoking of any kind. All Condos in Steamboat units are 100% tobacco and cannabis free, and smoking costs a $1,500 charge to the card on file.
No pets of any kind. This is also a complex policy, and bringing a pet costs a $1,500 charge.
There is no housekeeping during your stay.

THE CONDO: King bed in the bedroom with a 32-inch TV. The living room has a queen sofa sleeper with a Tempur-Pedic mattress, so up to 4 can sleep here.
Cozy gas log fireplace, 50-inch smart TV where you can log in to Netflix, Amazon and more, a DVD player and a movie library.
Renovated kitchen with oven, cooktop, microwave, dishwasher, full-size fridge, coffee maker, crock pot, blender and toaster. Basic spices are provided.
Private patio with chairs and a Weber gas grill.
Two humidifiers, a hair dryer and two bath robes.
The unit is fully stocked with sheets, towels, pool and hot tub towels, toilet paper, soaps, shampoo, dishwasher tabs and cleaning supplies.

SKIING: Storm Meadows Club Condominiums has a trail system for ski-in, ski-out access to the slopes.
You have a private ski locker on the lower ski-in level, and a drying rack for ski gear in the condo.
In ski season, a ski shuttle runs from the condo to the base area from 8:00 AM until 5:20 PM.

CLUB SPA: The Club Spa Facility is just steps from the condo. It has an outdoor heated pool, a hot tub, a sauna, an exercise room with cardio and weights, and a tennis court in summer.
The Club Spa is run by the complex association, not by Condos in Steamboat. Most common-area pools and hot tubs in Steamboat close from Labor Day to Thanksgiving, and from the end of ski season to Memorial Day.
There is a coin laundry on the lower level.

PARKING: We highly recommend a car. Uber and Lyft also operate in the area.
Parking rules depend on the complex, including the number and size of vehicles. Please check with Kim before you arrive. Any towing is the guest's responsibility.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is at 10:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
Our cleaners clean after you leave, but please leave the condo in an acceptable condition, or extra cleaning charges may apply.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the condo, call Kim at Condos in Steamboat on 970-879-5351. Kim answers from 8 AM to 8 PM Mountain Time, 7 days a week. It's a home office, so please keep to those hours unless it's urgent.

LOCAL TIPS: Steamboat is known as Ski Town, USA, and has produced more winter Olympians than any other town in North America.
The free Steamboat Springs Transit city bus connects the mountain area, downtown, restaurants and grocery stores. The ski area base and downtown are about 3 miles apart.
There is a dining guide on the coffee table with local restaurants.
Fish Creek Falls is 3 miles from town, and Strawberry Park Hot Springs is 7 miles from town.
Walk or bike the Yampa River Core Trail, which runs along the river through downtown.
In summer, Steamboat has three championship golf courses, hiking, biking, kayaking, fly fishing and horseback riding, plus a summer rodeo series.
Yampa Valley Regional Airport is about 22 miles from town. Denver is a 160-mile drive over Rabbit Ears Pass, so check road conditions in winter.
```
