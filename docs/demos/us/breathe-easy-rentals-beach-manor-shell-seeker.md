# Demo guide: Breathe Easy Rentals, Beach Manor 702 "Shell Seeker" at Tops'l (Miramar Beach, FL)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Breathe Easy Rentals' condos *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Beach Manor:** Breathe Easy manages about 16 condos in Beach Manor at Tops'l Resort (more than
any other building on their site). Resort amenities, check-in rules and the checkout checklist are the
same across those units, so one guide fits all of them. The example unit is Beach Manor 702 "Shell
Seeker" (2 bedrooms, sleeps 4, 7th floor).

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
breatheeasyrentals.com/vacation-rentals/rental/beach-manor-0702---shell-seeker/ (description, resort
amenities, important notes, house rules, amenity list), breatheeasyrentals.com/booking-faq/ (check-in
and check-out times, early entry fee, Wi-Fi, emergencies, checkout checklist, checkout text number) and
breatheeasyrentals.com/vacation-rentals/results/ (unit count). Every fact below comes from those pages.
**Not published:** the free activities list (only "details provided upon booking") and prices for
their beach gear rentals. Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders, and you should
say so in the email.

**Heads-up:** their FAQ says guests already get "links directing you to our digital guidebooks", so
they use a guidebook tool today. Pitch the concierge (instant answers in any language, extras) as the
difference, not the guide itself.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Beach Manor 702 Shell Seeker — Miramar Beach`
   - **City, country:** `Miramar Beach, Florida`
   - **Street address:** `9001 Tops'l Beach Blvd, Miramar Beach, FL 32550` (published on their site)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```

ARRIVAL: Check-in is at 4:00 PM Central Time. There's no front desk. You let yourself in with a smart lock keypad, and your door code is sent in your arrival message from Breathe Easy Rentals. Please don't share it.
Your door code is only activated once you've signed the rental agreement (within 48 hours of booking) and completed the security verification.
Early entry. If the condo is ready before check-in time, our team will contact you. Early entry is $50. Please don't pressure our teams for early access. Entering early without written approval (text or email) from the Breathe Easy team means you'll be asked to leave, and a $200 plus tax fee is charged to the card on file.

WI-FI: High-speed Wi-Fi throughout the condo. Your Wi-Fi login is in your arrival message and digital guidebook. We suggest taking a screenshot of it before you arrive.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 4 guests at all times. Babies, infants and children count in the limit.
The primary guest must be 25 or older and stay for the whole reservation. Children are welcome with their parents.
No smoking, vaping, candles, incense or open flames inside the condo or on the balcony.
No pets.
Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM. Please be respectful of the neighbors.
No parties, events or unauthorized gatherings.
Guests and visitors must follow all property, HOA and community rules.
The condo is not handicap accessible.

THE CONDO: Main bedroom with a king bed, walk-in shower and ocean views. Guest bedroom with a queen bed and its own tiled walk-in shower.
Open-concept living area with smart cable TVs, and a private balcony for sunrise coffee.
Kitchen with pots and pans, a Keurig coffee maker and a blender. Washer and dryer in the condo.

RESORT AMENITIES: Pool (heated November to March), a hot and dry sauna, an indoor hot tub, a fitness center and grills by the pool.
The beach boardwalk is at your doorstep, with direct beach access.
You also have access to The Tides, with a basketball court, shuffleboard and putt-putt.

- Move furniture back to where it was.
- Take all your travel companions with you!

Last step, and it's important. Text us at (850) 749-5221 to say you've headed out.

You can rent beach chairs, beach umbrellas, a 60-quart rolling cooler, a pack n play and pickleball equipment. Ask us for details.
Every time you stay with Breathe Easy Rentals, you get access to more free tickets for local activities.
```
