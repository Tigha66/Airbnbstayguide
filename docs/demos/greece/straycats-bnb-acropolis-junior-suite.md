# Demo guide: Straycats BnB, Acropolis Junior Suite (Koukaki, Athens)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Straycats' apartments *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why the Acropolis Junior Suite:** their "Premium suite just across the Parthenon" (code KOOKSUITE on
their site): a 45 m² 7th-floor loft 350 m from the Parthenon, with a bathtub facing it, two patios
and less than 100 m to Acropolis metro. With 293 reviews at 4.94 it's clearly a flagship, and its
listing has the times, rules and registration number we need.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- straycats-bnb.com/athens-center (card "Luxstays under Parthenon's shadow", code KOOKSUITE: 3 guests, 1 bedroom, 2 beds, 1 bath, 350 m from the Parthenon, 45 m², 7th floor; links to Airbnb)
- airbnb.com/rooms/651374958985277215 ("Acropolis Junior Suite", hosted by Romanos: check-in 2:00 PM to 2:00 AM, checkout before 11:00 AM, 3 guests maximum, no pets, no parties, no smoking, quiet hours 11:00 PM to 8:00 AM, checkout steps, amenities, two elevators, host greets you, registration 00003137679)
- straycats-bnb.com/property-management ("24/7 support from a dedicated local property manager", "personalized welcome pack of local produce")
- straycats-bnb.com/about-us ("16 hours per day communication shifts", welcome person waiting for you)

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not published:** the street address, the climate resilience
fee amount, and any early check-in, late checkout or transfer prices (a review on their homepage
says Romanos "booked us the taxi"). Their site says "24/7 support" on the management page but
"16 hours per day communication shifts" on the About page, so the manual avoids promising 24/7.
Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders, and you should say so in the email.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Acropolis Junior Suite — Athens`
   - **City, country:** `Athens, Greece`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the city).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```

You can see the Parthenon from the bathtub and from both patios, along with the top floor of the Acropolis Museum, Lycabettus and Philopappou hill. It's ideal for couples who want the centre of Athens without the city noise. Registration number 00003137679.

If your flight changes, just tell us and we'll do our best to adjust your check-in.
Your entry details are in your arrival message from Straycats. Please don't share them.
The building has two elevators.

WI-FI: Wi-Fi is included, and there's an ethernet connection and a dedicated workspace. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

No parties or events.
No commercial photography.

Central air conditioning and central heating.

Ask us for tailor-made tips. We love sharing the secret side of our city, and we partner with local concierge and leisure companies to help plan your days in Athens and trips around Greece.
```

## Step 3: Property details

| Check-out | `11:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your phone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| Our ferry gets to Piraeus at 11pm, can we still check in? | Yes, check-in runs until 2:00 AM; send arrival time; Acropolis metro under 100 m |
| How far is the Acropolis? | About 350 m; metro under 100 m |
| Is there a washing machine? | No, but there's a laundromat nearby |
| Can we have friends over for a party? | No parties; quiet hours 11 PM–8 AM; max 3 guests |
| Is there a lift? | Yes, two elevators |
| What do we do at checkout? | Before 11 AM: gather towels, trash out, turn things off, return keys |
| Τι ώρα είναι το check-out; (Greek) | Answers in Greek: before 11:00 AM |
| Y a-t-il la climatisation ? (French) | Answers in French: yes, central air conditioning |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Greek) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the late-ferry answer, the Greek answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
