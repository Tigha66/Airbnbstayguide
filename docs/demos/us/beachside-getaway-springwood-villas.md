# Demo guide: Beachside Getaway, Springwood Villas (Hilton Head Island, SC)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Beachside Getaway's homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Springwood Villas:** Beachside Getaway manages five villas in this one South Forest Beach
community (22, 53, 70, 71 and 74 Springwood). They share the same rules, community pool, parking and
distances, so one guide fits several of their listings, which makes it the most convincing demo.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): beachsidegetaway.com/hilton-head-vacation-rentals/22-springwood-villa,
/53-springwood-villas and /70-springwood-villas (rules, pool season, parking, distances, amenities);
beachsidegetaway.com/rental-policies (check-in 4:00 PM, check-out 10:00 AM, age 25, occupancy, pets,
keys returned to the office); beachsidegetaway.com/pre-arrival-checklist (grocery delivery, bikes, airport
cabs); beachsidegetaway.com/beach-gear-credit; beachsidegetaway.com/meet-your-beachside-team;
beachsidegetaway.com/hilton-head-island-property-management ("over 100 owners", Airbnb, Vrbo, TripAdvisor,
FlipKey). Every fact below comes from those pages. Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are not public, so
they are left as "in your arrival message"; say so in the email. Not verified: the exact number of
Springwood units overall (only the 5 managed by Beachside are counted), and whether Springwood guests
still return physical keys (the policy page says so; many units may now be keyless). Note: listing
amenity tags show "Outdoor Grill", but the written Springwood rules say no grills, so the guide follows the rules.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Springwood Villas — Hilton Head Island`
   - **City, country:** `Hilton Head Island, South Carolina`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Springwood Villas in South Forest Beach, Hilton Head Island, cared for by the local, family-owned team at Beachside Getaway. You're about half a mile from the beach and Coligny, with a lagoon, a fountain and a bridge to the community pool right outside. We're so glad you're here.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is at 4:00 PM. The villa and its amenities are not available before then. We do our very best to have everything ready by 4:00 PM.
Your door code and entry details are sent in your arrival message from Beachside Getaway. Please don't share them.
Arriving late? After-hours check-in is possible with prior notice, so please call the office before you travel.
The guest who made the reservation must be at least 25 and stay in the villa for the whole reservation.

WI-FI: High-speed internet is included, good for streaming. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Please stay within the number of guests on your rental agreement.
No pets of any kind at Springwood.
No smoking anywhere on the property.
No grills are allowed at Springwood.
No trailers, campers, boats or golf carts on the property.
A complete list of Springwood rules is posted inside the villa.
Events, weddings or large gatherings need written approval from Beachside Getaway first.
If something gets accidentally damaged, please tell us before you leave. Reported accidental damage is covered up to $3,000.

POOL: The community pool is a short walk across the courtyard and over the bridge.
The pool is open seasonally from April 1 to October 31. The pool deck is open all year for sunbathing.
There is a 1 ft kiddie pool for little ones. The pool is not heated.

PARKING: Each villa has 2 parking spaces. One is reserved for you, and the second is first come, first served in the shared spaces.
No trailers, campers, boats or golf carts, please.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
Please take all garbage out, lock up and make sure the villa is secure.
If you were given keys, return them to our office at 1 Corpus Christi Place, Suite 115, Hilton Head Island.
Refunds aren't issued for early departures.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the villa, call Beachside Getaway on (843) 686-6044 or email info@beachsidegetaway.com. We fix maintenance issues quickly.

SAND AND SURF: The beach is about half a mile away, around a 12 minute walk.
Your stay includes a free Beach Gear Credit with Vayk Gear. Look for the email with your link after booking and use your reservation number. Chairs, umbrellas, coolers and beach games are delivered the day you arrive and picked up when you leave, and you don't need to be there. Just rinse the sand off before pickup. Gear damage protection is 8.9% of the gear order.

LOCAL TIPS: Distances from Springwood:
- 0.5 mi: the beach
- 0.7 mi: Coligny Plaza, for restaurants and shopping
- 0.9 mi: Lowcountry Celebration Park, with the Pirate Ship Playground and the Children's Play Museum
- 4.1 mi: Shelter Cove
- 4.2 mi: Harbour Town
- 5 mi: South Beach Marina
Hilton Head has miles of bike paths and firm-packed sand that's perfect for beach cruisers. Forest Beach Surf and Cycle rents bikes, surfboards and paddleboards and gives lessons. Book early.
Coastal Concierge delivers groceries and will put them away in the villa before you arrive.
Flying into Hilton Head (HHH) or Savannah/Hilton Head (SAV) without a car? Book a cab or shuttle ahead, because cabs aren't always waiting and Uber can be unreliable off-hours. Yellow Transportation of Hilton Head and Diamond Transportation are our favorites.
Book tee times, tennis and pickleball courts, dinner reservations, and dolphin or fishing cruises before you arrive. They fill up fast.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `4:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `10:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `(843) 686-6044` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples**: Beachside Getaway doesn't publish early check-in or late checkout fees, so tell
them they set their own prices. (Don't sell beach gear: they already include a free Beach Gear Credit.)

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Early check-in (from 1:00 PM) | $50 | Get to the beach sooner. Confirmed the day before, if the cleaning schedule allows. |
| Late checkout (until 12:00 PM) | $50 | One more slow island morning. Subject to availability. |
| Welcome grocery stock-up | $60 | Arrive to coffee, breakfast basics and snacks already in the kitchen. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | 4:00 PM; after-hours check-in with prior notice |
| Is the pool open in November? | No: pool open April 1 to October 31; deck open all year |
| Can we bring our dog? | No pets at Springwood |
| Can we use a grill? | No, grills aren't allowed at Springwood |
| How many cars can we park? | 2: one reserved, one first come, first served |
| How far is the beach? | About half a mile, around 12 minutes on foot |
| Do we need to bring beach chairs? | No: free Beach Gear Credit with Vayk Gear, delivered on arrival |
| ¿A qué hora es la salida? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: 10:00 AM, take garbage out, lock up |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message, hands over to the team |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the pool answer, the Spanish answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
