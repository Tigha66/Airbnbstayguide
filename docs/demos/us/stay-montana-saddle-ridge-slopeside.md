# Demo guide: Stay Montana, Saddle Ridge Slopeside Retreat (Big Sky, MT)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Stay Montana's homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Saddle Ridge Slopeside Retreat:** a featured ski-in/ski-out townhouse at Moonlight Basin with a
private hot tub, no air conditioning and a "no private lodge access" note. These are the questions Big Sky
guests ask most, and the listing answers all of them in detail.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- staymontana.com/vacation-rentals/saddle-ridge-slopeside-retreat/ (description, beds, policies, supplies, AC note, guest resources)
- staymontana.com/faq/ (check-in 4 PM / check-out 10 AM, lock code emailed 7 days before, digital guide texted 2 days before, 4WD advice, RV parking, contacts, info@staymontana.com)
- staymontana.com/ella-cookies/ (Ella Cookies at check-in)
- staymontana.com/awards-accolades/ (Best of Montana 2025 and other awards)
- Owners' names (Chris and Jen Torsleff, founders in 2016) come from distinctlymontana.com and explorebigsky.com, not Stay Montana's own site.

Every fact below comes from those pages. Door codes, the Wi-Fi password and the 24-hour emergency
number are left as placeholders. **Note:** Stay Montana already texts guests "a link to our digital
property guide", so pitch StayGuide as the **concierge that answers back** in any language, not just
another guide. The listing says "60 miles to West Yellowstone" and also "45 minutes" to the park's west
entrance; the kit uses the mileage.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Saddle Ridge Slopeside Retreat — Big Sky`
   - **City, country:** `Big Sky, Montana`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to the Saddle Ridge Slopeside Retreat, a beautifully remodeled 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse in the heart of Saddle Ridge at Moonlight Basin, cared for by Stay Montana. Ski-in/ski-out access, sweeping views of the Spanish Peaks and a private hot tub waiting at the end of the day. Look out for your fresh Ella Cookies, our family recipe, waiting for you at check-in!

ARRIVAL: Check-in is at 4:00 PM. Your check-in instructions, with directions and the lock code, are emailed 7 days before arrival. Contactless self check-in. Please don't share your code.
Would you like to arrive earlier? Our concierge can help with an early check-in request.
In winter we highly recommend a 4WD vehicle.

WI-FI: Wireless internet throughout. The network name and password are in your check-in instructions.
Speeds can't be guaranteed, depending on weather and usage in the area.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 8 guests.
No smoking.
No pets.
Minimum age to book is 18.
No open fires during fire season.
A vacation rental agreement must be signed before arrival.
Please note this property does not have access to the private lodge at Moonlight.

THE TOWNHOUSE: Open-plan living, dining and kitchen on the main level, with a cozy stone fireplace and leather furniture. The dining table seats six, plus bar seating for three.
Upstairs, the primary suite has a king bed plus a twin bed, a reading nook, mountain views and an en-suite bathroom.
Downstairs, one bedroom has a full bed, a twin bed and a bunk bed. The other has a queen bed. The bunk room opens onto the back patio and hot tub.
Kitchen with gas range, dishwasher, microwave, coffee machine, toaster, blender, crockpot, tea kettle, bakeware and basic spices. There are coffee filters, but please bring your own coffee.
Washer and dryer, Smart TV and live or cable/satellite TV.
There's no air conditioning, which is typical for Montana mountain homes. Evenings cool down beautifully at elevation. Open windows at night (only while you're at home) and keep windows and curtains closed during the day. Fans are in the bedroom closets.
We provide a starter kit of sheets, towels, hot tub towels, Bee Kind toiletries, toilet paper, paper towels, dish soap, dishwasher tablets and laundry pods.

HOT TUB: Your private hot tub is on the back patio, with hot tub towels provided. Soak under the stars!

PARKING: Parking is available at the townhouse. Sorry, we can't accommodate RVs, campers or trailers. Ask us if you need help finding parking for a large vehicle.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is at 10:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
Want a late check-out? Our concierge can help if you ask at least 48 hours before departure.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
Stay Montana has a 24-hour emergency line and on-call maintenance. The number is in your check-in instructions.
For questions, text us at (406) 604-1235 or call our Big Sky office at (406) 995-2775.
The home has a carbon monoxide detector, smoke alarms and a fire extinguisher.

LOCAL TIPS: Ski right from the front door. Big Sky Resort has 5,800 skiable acres, 4,350 feet of vertical and 400+ inches of snow a year. Buy lift tickets ahead of time.
Lone Mountain Ranch has 85+ km of groomed Nordic trails.
In summer you're minutes from fly fishing, rafting, golf, hiking, mountain biking, horseback riding and zip-lining.
West Yellowstone is about 60 miles away along the scenic Gallatin River and Highway 191. Bozeman Yellowstone International Airport is about 55 miles.
The Skyline Shuttle serves Big Sky. Check its website for current schedules.
Our concierge can book restaurants, fly fishing, snowmobiling, Yellowstone tours, lift tickets, ski rentals, dog sledding and more.
```

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples**: Stay Montana handles early/late and grocery shopping through its concierge but
doesn't publish prices.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Early check-in (from 1:00 PM) | $75 | Get on the mountain sooner. Subject to availability. |
| Late check-out (until 12:00 PM) | $75 | One more slow mountain morning. Request 48 hours before departure. |
| Grocery stock-up | $75 | Arrive to coffee, breakfast basics and snacks in the kitchen (plus the cost of groceries). |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| Is there air conditioning? | No; open windows at night, fans in the bedroom closets |
| Can we use the Moonlight lodge? | No, this property doesn't have access to the private lodge |
| Do we need 4WD in January? | 4WD highly recommended in winter |
| Can we have a campfire in August? | No open fires during fire season |
| Is there coffee? | Filters yes, coffee no, so bring your own |
| How far is Yellowstone? | About 60 miles to West Yellowstone |
| Can we park our camper there? | No RVs, campers or trailers |
| ¿Hay jacuzzi? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: yes, private hot tub, towels provided |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the check-in instructions / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the AC answer, the Spanish answer, the Extras tab) for a follow-up email.
