# Demo guide: Oceanfront Cottage Rentals, Brass Rail 208 (Tybee Island, GA)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Oceanfront Cottage Rentals' homes *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Brass Rail 208:** it's a 3-bedroom condo in the gated Brass Rail Villas community on Tybee's South
End, which OCR promotes as one of its two "Tybee Island Resorts" (they list Brass Rail 105 and 208).
Same pools, hot tub, garage and walk to the pier for every unit, and the company-wide policies (3:00 PM
check-in at the office, 10:00 AM checkout, quiet hours, no dogs on the beach) apply to all 100+ homes.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- oceanfrontcottage.com/rentals/brass-rail-208 (description, parking Q&A, amenities, "pets: No" icon)
- oceanfrontcottage.com/explore-tybee/brass-rail-villas (2-minute walk to Tybee Pier and Pavilion)
- oceanfrontcottage.com/tybeeguestguide/our-policies (check-in after 3:00 PM, keys only to the person on
  the agreement, checkout by 10:00 AM, smoking, after-hours emergencies, housekeeping, office hours)
- oceanfrontcottage.com/faqs (office location, key drop box, early check-in, coffee maker, dogs on beach)
- oceanfrontcottage.com/tybeeguestguide/tybeeislandgoodneighborpolicy (quiet time, charcoal, trash, turtles, beach rules)
- oceanfrontcottage.com/parkingontybee (pay-to-park rules and rates)
- oceanfrontcottage.com/ (24/7 support promise) and /about-us/history

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not verified / left out:** the Wi-Fi network and password,
any keyless code, the after-hours emergency number (the site says one exists but doesn't publish it), pool
hours (only a guest review mentions 9 PM), and the primary bed size (the page says king in one place and
queen in another, so the manual doesn't state it).

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Brass Rail 208 — Tybee Island`
   - **City, country:** `Tybee Island, Georgia`
   - **Street address:** `3 15th Street, Unit 208` (published on the listing page; or leave empty).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Brass Rail 208, a spacious three-bedroom condo in the gated Brass Rail Villas community on Tybee Island's South End, looked after by Oceanfront Cottage Rentals, the island's original vacation rental company since 1997. The beach, the Tybee Pier and Pavilion, restaurants and shops are all a short walk away, so you can park once and leave the car.

ARRIVAL: Check-in begins after 3:00 PM on your arrival date. Please collect your keys from our office at 717 1st Street (Hwy 80), just past the first stop light on the left, next door to the Chevron and across from the post office. It's a yellow house with a turquoise roof, and the office is upstairs. If you can't climb stairs, call us when you arrive and we'll come down to you. The office is open every day, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
Keys or keyless entry codes can only be given to the person on the rental agreement, who must show a valid ID (renters must be 25 or older). Any keyless code is sent in your check-in instructions. Please don't share it.
We can't give keys before 3:00 PM because housekeeping needs the time between guests. You can call the day before and we may be able to get you in an hour or so early, but only if the condo wasn't rented the night before. In summer this is rare. Arriving early? Spend a few hours in historic Savannah or at a Tybee restaurant.

WI-FI: Internet is included. The network name and password are in your check-in instructions. Please note that TV and internet problems are not treated as after-hours emergencies, so report them during office hours.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 8 guests. Only registered guests may stay overnight.
No parties or events of any kind (graduation, prom, birthdays, receptions). Breaking this rule can mean immediate eviction with no refund.
No smoking inside. Smoking is only allowed on outside balconies. Evidence of smoking inside carries a $150 cleaning fee.
This condo is not pet-friendly.
Tybee's island-wide quiet time is 9:00 PM to 7:00 AM. No loud music outside, please.
Charcoal grilling on decks is prohibited; use the community grilling areas.
Please don't leave doors or windows open while the A/C or heat is running.

CONDO AND AMENITIES: Three bedrooms and two and a half bathrooms. The primary suite has balcony access and a bathroom with a double vanity, walk-in shower and jetted tub. The second bedroom has a queen and a twin; the third has two twins. There's a half bath off the living area.
Fully equipped kitchen with stainless steel appliances, dishwasher, microwave, toaster, blender and a drip coffee maker. We leave a few filters, small packs of coffee and sugar and creamer; bring your favourite coffee.
Full-size washer and dryer, iron and ironing board. Bed linens and towels are provided.
Private wraparound balcony overlooking the pool courtyard. Elevator access (elevators can occasionally be out of service).
The community has two swimming pools, a hot tub and shared grilling areas, just steps from the condo.

PARKING: Two standard-size vehicles may park in the garage (7 foot clearance), in the spaces marked for Unit 208, one in front of the other. Golf carts are not allowed in the complex. With prior approval, extra parking is available at our office.
Everywhere else on Tybee is pay to park, enforced 8 AM to 8 PM every day of the year. Pay-and-Display is $2.00 per hour island-wide; display the receipt on your dashboard. Never park in yards, neighbours' driveways or blocking streets, as cars can be towed.

SAND AND SURF: South Beach is a short walk, and the Tybee Pier and Pavilion is about 2 minutes on foot.
Dogs are not allowed on any Tybee beach at any time, and the city fines start around $280.
No glass, no fires or grilling, and no littering (including cigarette butts) on the beach. Stay off the dunes and dune plants.
Swim within 50 yards of the shoreline and obey the lifeguards.
From May to November, please support the sea turtles by turning beachfront lights off by 9:00 PM.

TRASH: Bag your garbage securely and put it in the trash and recycling carts provided. Tybee has weekly "side door" pickup, so don't roll the carts to the curb.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
- Leave linens on the beds. Gathering towels in one spot helps housekeeping but isn't required.
- Load and start the dishwasher if you have dirty dishes. No need to unload it.
- Take all garbage out to the trash carts.
- Return your keys to the office by 10:00 AM. If you leave before we open at 9:00 AM, use the black drop box beside our front door.

EMERGENCY: For police, fire or medical emergencies, call 911. The Tybee Island Fire Department's number is 912-786-4573.
For anything about the condo, call Oceanfront Cottage Rentals on (800) 786-5889. Our local team offers 24/7 support, and there is an after-hours emergency line for maintenance. After hours, please call only for air conditioning, heat, or anything that could be harmful to you; everything else can wait until the office opens at 9:00 AM.

LOCAL TIPS: Tybee has three miles of sandy beaches and is about 20 minutes from historic Savannah.
- Walk to Tybrisa Street and the South End shops and restaurants, including Sting Ray's, Fannie's on the Beach and The Breakfast Club.
- The Tybee Pier and Pavilion is great for fishing and people-watching and hosts festivals through the year.
- Waves Surf Shop and Tim's Bike and Beach Gear are nearby on the South End.
- North Beach is quieter, with the Tybee Island Lighthouse and Fort Screven.
- Back River has calm water, kayaking and sunset views.
- Travelling with a dog? Tybee has two dog parks, and dogs must be on a leash whenever they're outside a fenced area.
Ask us for restaurant, tour and event tips any time.
```
