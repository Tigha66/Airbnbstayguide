# Demo guide: Bespoke Residences & Holiday Homes, North Residence (Palm Jumeirah)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Bespoke Residences' apartments *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why North Residence:** a 1,444 sq ft, 1-bedroom apartment on Palm Jumeirah, on the Palm where
Bespoke's own head office is (Fairmont North Residences, ground floor). The listing publishes check-in
and check-out times, guest registration 48 hours ahead, the deposit, VAT and service charge, free use
of the residential pool and paid access to the Fairmont's beach, gym and kids' club, plus a
Fairmont breakfast package. Those are exactly the questions guests send.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): bespokeresidences.com/en/properties/north-palm-residence
("North Residence - Palm Jumeirah": check-in/out timings, terms and policies, guest registration,
description, features, additional information, guest access, out and about, promotions; page shows
"Last updated: 09 Oct, 2026") and the site footer (head office Fairmont North Residences, Ground Floor,
The Palm; +971 55 938 4795; Reservations@bespokepropertymanagement.com). Every fact below comes from
that page. **Conflict:** the page lists both "15 AED per Tourism Assessment Fee per night" and
"DTCM fee: AED 10 per night for the first 30 consecutive nights"; the manual doesn't quote an
amount, so ask them. **Not published:** maximum guests (the page shows "0 Guests"), smoking/pet/party
rules, Wi-Fi details, pool hours, building access, cleaning fee amount, early/late fees. The "lists on
9 platforms" hook in INTERNATIONAL-PROSPECTS.md could not be re-confirmed on their site, so the email
doesn't use it.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `North Residence — Palm Jumeirah`
   - **City, country:** `Palm Jumeirah, Dubai, United Arab Emirates`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (no building address is published for the unit).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```

The spacious living area blends modern comfort with subtle Arabic influences, and the elegant bedroom has a plush bed for a restful night's sleep. Nakheel Mall is a 5-minute walk.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 3:00 PM.
Early check-in is subject to availability, and additional charges may apply.
Your access details and arrival instructions are in your arrival message from Bespoke Residences. Please don't share them.
The building has 24/7 security.

GUEST REGISTRATION: We need passport (or Emirates ID) copies of all guests and visitors at least 48 hours before arrival, to register your stay with the community and building security. We can't guarantee your check-in if ID copies aren't provided on time.
A refundable security deposit of AED 1,000 is required.
Rates exclude 5% VAT and a 10% service charge, and a one-time cleaning fee applies.

Please treat the apartment as your own. The refundable deposit covers any damage.
```

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples**: Bespoke says early check-in and late checkout "may" cost extra and offers a
Fairmont breakfast package without a published price, so tell them they set their own.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast at the Fairmont | AED 150 | Start the day with breakfast at the Fairmont Hotel, part of our Bed and Breakfast package. |
| Early check-in (from 12:00 PM) | AED 150 | Get settled sooner after your flight. Subject to availability. |
| Late checkout (until 3:00 PM) | AED 150 | One more slow morning on the Palm. Subject to availability. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | From 3:00 PM; early check-in subject to availability, may cost extra |
| When do you need our passports? | Copies for all guests and visitors at least 48 hours before arrival |
| Can we use the Fairmont beach? | Yes, at the hotel's prevailing prices; the residential pool is free |
| How much is the deposit? | AED 1,000, refundable |
| Is VAT included? | No: rates exclude 5% VAT and a 10% service charge |
| How far is Dubai Mall? | About 25 minutes by taxi |
| ما هو وقت تسجيل المغادرة؟ (Arabic: what time is checkout?) | Answers in Arabic: 12:00 PM; late checkout subject to availability |
| Можно ли позавтракать в отеле Fairmont? (Russian: can we have breakfast at the Fairmont?) | Answers in Russian: yes, ask about the Bed and Breakfast package |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; access details are in the arrival message / hands over |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Russian) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the beach answer, the Arabic answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
