# Demo guide: Naxos Vacation Rentals, Villa Elaia (Kastraki, Naxos)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Naxos Vacation Rentals' villas *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Villa Elaia:** one of the two "P square" Cycladic luxury villas in Kastraki, with a private pool,
under 500 m from Kastraki beach and 25 minutes from Chora and the port. It's a classic Naxos villa
stay: guests arrive by ferry, need a car, and ask about beaches, boat trips and food. Naxos Vacation
Rentals also sells day cruises and tastings on the same site, so the guide can point to real,
priced experiences.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- naxosvacationrentals.com/en/property/p-square-villa-elaia-luxury-villas (description, two levels, 2 double bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, sofa bed, baby cot, private pool, amenities marked available, guest selector up to 6, 3-night minimum, licence number 00001345239, free concierge, luggage assistance, housekeeping included, phone +30 694 945 3468 Monday to Friday 9am to 7pm)
- naxosvacationrentals.com/en/activity/daily-cruise-koufonissia (from €70 per person, from Agia Anna port at 9:45)
- naxosvacationrentals.com/en/activity/cheese-wine-trails (minimum 2 people, €570 total, pick-up from your accommodation)
- naxosvacationrentals.com/en/naxos-property-management (Proto Tourism "established in the early 80s", office at the port, "Platinum" 24/7 package, transfers and car with driver)

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not published:** check-in and check-out times (the
terms page is website terms only), house rules beyond "non-smoking", the climate resilience fee
amount and transfer prices. "Max 6 guests" comes from the booking form's guest selector and the
beds (2 doubles + sofa bed), so confirm it. The listing describes "panoramic sea views" but the
"Sea view" amenity is greyed out. Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Villa Elaia — Kastraki, Naxos`
   - **City, country:** `Kastraki, Naxos, Greece`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the village).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```

Traditional island architecture, a landscaped Cycladic garden and a private pool facing the Aegean and the sunset. Property licence number 00001345239.

ARRIVAL: Your check-in time is in your booking confirmation. Your door and key details are in your arrival message from Naxos Vacation Rentals. Please don't share them.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi. The network name and password are in your arrival message.
There's a flat-screen TV.

HOUSE RULES: The villa is non-smoking.
Please stay within the number of guests on your booking (the villa sleeps up to 6).

Air conditioning, a coffee machine, cookware and wine glasses.
A baby cot is available. Please ask before you arrive.
Housekeeping is included.

POOL: The villa has its own private pool with a spacious outdoor seating and dining area. It's the best place to watch the sun go down over the Aegean.

PARKING: The villa has parking. A car is the easiest way to explore, because the organised beaches and Chora are a drive away. We can arrange car hire, or ATVs, buggies, quads and bikes.

We also organise sailing and catamaran trips, scuba diving, fishing trips, day trips to Santorini, Mykonos and Delos, and massages at the villa. Just ask.
```
