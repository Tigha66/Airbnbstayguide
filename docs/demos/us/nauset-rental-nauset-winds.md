# Demo guide: Nauset Rental, Nauset Winds (Orleans, MA)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Nauset Rental's homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Nauset Winds:** it's in Nauset Heights, Orleans (their home town), a quarter mile from Nauset
Beach, the beach the company is named after. It's dog-friendly, sleeps 8, and its listing has a full
description plus local picks. Nauset Rental's check-in, checkout, smoking, noise, parking and pet rules
are company-wide (FAQ and Terms), so this guide is a template for their whole portfolio.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- https://www.nausetrental.com/rentals/nauset-winds-orleans-vacation-rental-walk-to-nauset (layout, amenities, local picks)
- https://www.nausetrental.com/faqs (check-in/out, self check-in, 24/7 on-call team, peak-season Saturday stays, linens, A/C, Wi-Fi, National Seashore pet rules, inquiry@ email)
- https://www.nausetrental.com/nauset-rental-booking-policies (smoking, noise, occupancy, parking, dogs, checkout)
- https://www.nausetrental.com/cape-cod-beaches (Nauset Beach, Skaket Beach)
- https://www.nausetrental.com/meet-the-nauset-rental-team and https://www.nausetrental.com/ ("70% Direct Bookings")

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not verified:** whether this home uses a lockbox or a keypad
(the FAQ says one or the other), the number of parking spaces, the pet fee for this home, and Nauset
Beach parking/sticker rules (their site doesn't publish them, so the manual doesn't mention them).
Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as "in your arrival instructions".

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Nauset Winds — Orleans`
   - **City, country:** `Orleans, Massachusetts`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Nauset Winds, a charming Cape Cod beach house on a sleepy dirt road in Nauset Heights, Orleans, cared for by the local team at Nauset Rental. Nauset Beach, with miles of white sand and Atlantic views, is just over a quarter mile from your front door.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is after 4:00 PM. This time is firm, so our cleaning and maintenance teams can fully prepare the home for you.
Check-in is self-service, using a lockbox with keys or an electronic keypad. Your detailed check-in instructions are emailed two weeks and again two days before you arrive.
Arriving late? No problem. If you have any trouble getting in, our 24/7 on-call team is available to help.
Pull up the driveway and around to the back of the house, where you'll find the new back deck.
Please report any cleaning problems within 12 hours of check-in so we can fix them quickly.

WI-FI: Wi-Fi is included, and there is a laptop-friendly workspace. The network name and password are in your arrival instructions.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 8 guests, including children and infants.
All Nauset Rental homes are non-smoking. This includes e-cigarettes and vapes.
Please be a good neighbor. Excessive noise or disturbances can mean being asked to leave.
Dogs are welcome at Nauset Winds. Please tell us in advance if you're bringing one, and we'll send a few quick questions for our records.
Keep your dog on a leash outside and clean up after it. Dogs are not allowed on furniture and shouldn't be left alone in the house. No cats or other pets.

THE HOUSE: Three bedrooms and two full bathrooms, each with a tub and shower.
The main level has the primary bedroom with a queen bed and a second bedroom with two twin beds. The whole lower level is the third bedroom, with a queen bed, a twin bed and a twin trundle.
The kitchen is fully stocked, lobster pot included, with a dishwasher, oven, microwave, coffee maker, blender and toaster, plus a dining table.
The living room has cathedral ceilings, a TV, and lots of books and games.
Washer and dryer, iron, extra pillows and blankets. There is air conditioning, plus ceiling fans and portable fans.
Outside you'll find the deck with outdoor furniture, a BBQ grill and an outdoor shower for rinsing off the sand.

SEA AND SAND: Nauset Beach is just over a quarter mile away. Beach essentials are provided, but please bring your own beach towels. Bed linens and bath towels are included.
For calm, warm water with little ones, try Skaket Beach on the bay side, which is perfect for sandcastles.
Pools, beaches and decks have no lifeguards from us, so please supervise children at all times.

PARKING: Park in the driveway and designated areas only. The maximum number of cars is in your arrival instructions, and cars outside designated areas can be towed.
The house has a garage.
No RVs, boats or trailers unless approved by us in writing.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM, and this time is firm. Thank you for staying with us!
Please follow the Checkout Checklist we send you. Leave the dishes clean and take care of the trash, because dirty dishes or excessive trash mean an extra cleaning charge.
Lock the doors and close the windows.
Tell us before you leave about anything that was damaged or isn't working.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the house, call or text Nauset Rental on 774-316-6287, or toll free (877) 352-1151. Our 24/7 on-call team can help after hours.
The house has smoke and carbon monoxide detectors, a fire extinguisher and a first aid kit.

LOCAL TIPS: Our favourite picks near Nauset Winds
- The Rail and The Barley Neck Inn for fresh local seafood and Cape Cod fare
- Nauset Farms for meats, fresh produce and prepared foods to cook at home
- The Academy Playhouse for live theater and local arts
- Skaket Beach for calm bay water and sunsets with kids
Orleans has freshwater kettle ponds, miles of salt marsh and plenty of water adventures for swimmers, boogie boarders and surfers.
Bringing your dog to Cape Cod National Seashore? Dogs must be on a 6-foot leash. In summer they aren't allowed on lifeguard-protected beaches, and they're kept out of posted shorebird nesting areas and designated nature trails. Check the signs before you go.
```
