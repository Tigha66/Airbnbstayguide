## 2. Service and fee

The supplier will prepare one mobile-friendly digital guest guide for the property identified above using information and assets supplied by the customer. The service includes:

- one guide with the agreed property information and guest instructions;
- a preview for the customer to review;
- one consolidated revision request submitted within seven calendar days after the first preview;
- a public guide link and QR code; and
- a short setup walkthrough.

The customer must supply complete, accurate information and assets that they are entitled to use. The supplier aims to provide the first preview within five UK working days after cleared payment and receipt of all required information and assets. The time estimate pauses while information, permissions or customer decisions are outstanding. Publication takes place only after the customer approves the guide.

The total fee is **$49.00 USD**, paid once. The invoice or payment receipt should describe this as “Founding Five guest guide and 12 months of basic hosting — $49.00 USD (one-time; no subscription or automatic renewal).” Supplier is not VAT registered. No UK VAT charged. No VAT number is displayed, and this is not described as VAT-exempt. The fee does not include a monthly subscription, automatic renewal or later continuation charge. Any work outside the included scope requires a separate written quote and the customer's agreement before it starts.

## 3. Hosting period

The supplier will host the published guide for 12 months beginning on the date the approved guide is first published. The supplier will record and confirm the publication date and hosting end date to the customer.

There is no automatic renewal and no requirement to buy a monthly subscription to receive the agreed hosting period. After the 12-month period, the supplier may offer a continuation option and price. Hosting continues only if the customer positively accepts that offer; silence does not authorise a charge.

The customer must keep the account and guide active, follow the service terms, and avoid publishing unlawful or unsafe material. The customer can ask the supplier to unpublish the guide or close the account. Unpublishing or account deletion makes the public guide unavailable and may end this hosting service for that guide. The supplier will explain any effect on the hosting period before carrying out a requested deletion where practicable.

The hosting service covers publication of the guide on the supplier's service. It does not promise uninterrupted availability, a particular business result, or recovery of content that the customer has deleted. The supplier will provide reasonable notice of planned maintenance where practicable and will use a proportionate backup and recovery process. Support hours and response target: [SUPPORT HOURS AND RESPONSE TARGET].

## 4. Not included

This agreement does not include a paid StayGuide subscription, Pro AI allowance, automated AI replies, automated WhatsApp messaging, PMS integration, third-party integrations, accommodation management, custom software, or a guarantee of bookings, revenue, guest behaviour or property performance. Those services are not included in the $49 fee and will not be activated by this agreement.

## 5. Customer content and responsibilities

The customer confirms that they have authority to provide the text, photographs, logos and other materials they submit, and to permit the supplier to use them to create, host and support the guide during the service. The customer retains ownership of their supplied materials. The supplier retains ownership of its software, templates and general know-how.

The customer is responsible for checking the preview for accuracy, safety and privacy before approving publication. The customer must not include door codes, identity documents, private Wi-Fi credentials or other sensitive information in a publicly accessible guide unless a separate, suitable access control has been agreed.

The supplier may make the guide unavailable if it reasonably believes the guide creates a security risk, breaches law or infringes another person's rights. Where practicable, the supplier will explain the issue and give the customer a reasonable opportunity to correct it.

## 6. Consumer cancellation and digital content

This section applies where the customer is a consumer and the contract is made at a distance. A consumer may have a statutory 14-day cancellation period. The supplier will provide the required pre-contract information and cancellation instructions before checkout and send a durable copy of the contract after acceptance.

The supplier will not begin commissioned service work during the consumer's cancellation period unless the consumer makes a separate, clear request to start early. If the consumer then cancels during that period, a proportionate amount for service work already supplied may be payable only where the law permits and the required information and request were obtained.

If digital content is to be supplied before the cancellation period ends, the supplier will obtain any separate express consent and acknowledgement required by law before providing it. The customer's approval of the guide preview or request to publish is separate from any cancellation consent. The right to cancel is not treated as waived merely because the customer reviewed or approved a preview.

A consumer can cancel by emailing [SUPPLIER CONTACT EMAIL] or writing to [SUPPLIER SERVICE AND INVOICE ADDRESS]. The customer may use the cancellation form at the end of this agreement but does not have to.
