# Demo guide: The Coppermines Lakes Cottages, Beckside Cottage (Coniston, Lake District)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of The Coppermines' cottages *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Beckside Cottage:** a detached 4-bedroom village house for 8 in Coniston, with a log fire, an
enclosed garden, room for 3 dogs and fell walks from the front door. It's a big-group,
dog-friendly, wood-fire cottage, so it raises every typical question: arrival time, logs, dogs, EV
charging, the 9:30 vs 10am departure, and who to call after the office closes.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): coppermines.co.uk/accommodation/beckside-cottage-coniston/
(description, bedrooms, kitchen, log fire, underfloor heating, "earlier 4pm check in" and "later 10am
check out", up to 3 pets, dog field, driveway parking, Voltshare EV at the Bluebird car park,
Coniston area guide), coppermines.co.uk/faqs/ (arrival info PDF, key safe, early arrival, pets £5 per
pet per night and pet rules, what's provided, initial firewood, baby equipment £5/item, no smoking,
EV rule, extra guests, problems on arrival, departure tidy-up, lost property, surprises),
coppermines.co.uk/covid-19-update/ev-charging-for-guests/ (6 chargers, VoltShare app),
coppermines.co.uk/contact-us/ (office hours; out-of-hours line for genuine emergencies only) and
coppermines.co.uk/about-us/ (since 1982, Philip Johnston, "over 90" cottages, info@coppermines.co.uk).
**Note:** the prospect list says "over 80"; their About page now says "over 90". **Not published:**
the key-safe code, Wi-Fi password and street address (sent in the arrival PDF).

---

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Beckside Cottage — Coniston`
   - **City, country:** `Coniston, Cumbria, United Kingdom`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the village).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Beckside Cottage, a bright, spacious detached house for up to 8 guests in Coniston village, looked after by The Coppermines Lakes Cottages, a local independent agency here since 1982. The Coniston fells start at the front door, The Ship Inn is a few minutes' stroll along the old railway line, and the lake is close by.

ARRIVAL: Check-in at Beckside Cottage is from 4:00 PM, an hour earlier than most of our cottages.
Your arrival information, including the address and key-safe code, is in the PDF sent with your booking confirmation. Please print it or save it on your phone before you travel, and don't share the code.
If you'd like to arrive earlier, contact us and we'll tell you if the cottage is ready. We'll also contact you on your arrival day as soon as it is.
You can arrive as late as you like, because the keys are left for you in the key safe.
Mobile signal can be patchy in the Lake District. Open this guide once before you set off and it will still work without signal.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your arrival information.
Broadband is outside our control, but if it stops working, let us know and we'll do our best to help.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 8 guests, including children. Extra guests can't stay, including in tents or caravans outside, for insurance and safety reasons.
All our cottages are strictly no smoking. If there's evidence of smoking inside, we will charge for specialist cleaning.
Electric cars must not be charged from the cottage. It's a serious fire risk and can invalidate the insurance. Use the public chargers in the village instead.
If something gets broken or damaged, please tell us as soon as possible so we can replace it for the next guests.

DOGS: Up to 3 pets are welcome, at £5 per pet per night.
Please don't leave pets alone in the cottage, unless they're comfortable in a secure cage.
Pets aren't allowed on the soft furnishings or in the bedrooms. You're welcome to bring their own bed or blanket.
Please clean up after your pet outside.
We can offer free use of a 1-acre field in Coniston village for dogs, by arrangement. Contact the office to book it.

FIREPLACE: The open-plan living and dining room has a log fire, and we leave you some initial logs to get started.
The snug has a period feature fireplace, a TV and a DVD player. The whole cottage also has underfloor heating.

THE COTTAGE: You come in at the back, through the kitchen, which has a gas hob, electric oven, microwave, fridge freezer, dishwasher, washing machine and dryer.
Upstairs there are 4 bedrooms with lake or field views. Bedrooms 1 and 3 have king-size beds. Bedrooms 2 and 4 can be a king-size bed or two 2 ft 6 in singles.
Two bathrooms, each with a bath and shower over, WC and basin.
At the back there's a large lawned garden with garden furniture, sheltered by trees and shrubs.
Bed linen and towels are provided and the beds are made up for your arrival. You'll find an iron, ironing board, hair dryer, toilet rolls, dishwasher tablets, washing-up liquid, a sponge, tea towels and an oven glove. Please bring your own food.
A high chair or travel cot can be hired for £5 per item for your stay. Please ask before you arrive.

PARKING: Parking for 2 cars on the driveway, with more parking in the lay-by less than 50 yards away.
For electric cars, there are 6 Voltshare chargers at The Bluebird Car and Coach Park in Coniston village, with discounted rates for Coppermines guests. Download the VoltShare app before you come, or scan the QR code to pay on arrival. Call the office for details.

CHECKOUT: Check-out at Beckside Cottage is by 10:00 AM, a little later than our usual 9:30.
You don't need to clean, as our housekeepers service the cottage straight after you leave, but please leave it reasonably clean and tidy.
If you leave something behind, contact us. Valuables are kept safe at our office, and we can post items back if you cover the postage.
Thank you for staying with The Coppermines!

EMERGENCY: In an emergency, call 999 for fire, police or ambulance. On the fells, call 999 and ask for Police, then Mountain Rescue. For urgent medical advice that isn't an emergency, call NHS 111.
For any problem with the cottage, call The Coppermines on 015394 41765 or email info@coppermines.co.uk. The office in Coniston is open Monday 9am to 6pm, Tuesday to Thursday 9am to 5pm, Friday 9am to 6pm, Saturday 9am to 5pm and Sunday 10am to 4pm.
Outside office hours the same number goes to an emergency line, for genuine emergencies only.

LOCAL TIPS: Coniston village has friendly pubs, local shops and lively cafés, and walks straight from the village, including the Old Man of Coniston.
The old railway line footpath takes you to The Ship Inn in a few minutes and into the village. There's no lighting, so take a torch after dark.
Coniston Boating Centre offers paddleboarding, kayaking and sailing on Coniston Water.
Wild swimming spots include the lake, mountain tarns and Church Beck, with its waterfalls and deep, clear pools.
Mountain bikers can ride the trails in nearby Grizedale Forest.
The Ruskin Museum tells the story of the area, including Donald Campbell's water speed record attempts.
On the eastern shore, Brantwood was John Ruskin's home, with an arts centre and The Terrace café with lake and mountain views.
Ambleside, Bowness and Windermere are a short drive away.
Our gift shop and office in Coniston is open 7 days a week.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `4:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `10:00 AM` (their standard is 9:30 AM; Beckside is later) |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival information` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `015394 41765` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The first
two are **their real published prices**; the logs top-up is a **demo example** (they leave initial
logs only), so tell them they set their own price.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Bring your dog | £5 per night | Per pet, up to 3. Free use of a 1-acre dog field in the village by arrangement. |
| High chair or travel cot | £5 | Per item, for your whole stay. Ready when you arrive. |
| Extra basket of logs | £10 | Keep the log fire going after the starter logs run out. Delivered before you arrive. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we arrive? | From 4:00 PM; earlier if ready, they'll contact you |
| We'll arrive at midnight, is that OK? | Yes, keys are in the key safe; save the code beforehand |
| Can we bring 2 dogs? | Yes, up to 3 pets, £5 per pet per night; not on sofas or in bedrooms |
| Are there logs for the fire? | Yes, initial logs are provided |
| Can I plug my electric car in at the cottage? | No; 6 Voltshare chargers at the Bluebird car park, guest discount |
| Do we have to clean before we leave? | No, just leave it reasonably tidy; out by 10:00 AM |
| The heating's broken and the office is closed, who do I call? | 015394 41765; diverted to the emergency line out of hours |
| Où peut-on se baigner dans la nature ? (French) | Answers in French: the lake, tarns and Church Beck pools |
| What's the key-safe code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival PDF / hands over to the team |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to French or German), the **Extras** tab,
and **Airplane mode**: reload the guide to show it still opens without signal.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the dogs answer, the French answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
