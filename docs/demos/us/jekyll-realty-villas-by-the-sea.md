# Demo guide: Jekyll Realty, Villas by the Sea (Jekyll Island, GA)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Jekyll Realty's homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Villas by the Sea:** an oceanfront condominium resort on the north end of Jekyll Island where
Jekyll Realty lists 19 rental villas (studios to 3 bedrooms) sharing the same pool, boardwalk, fitness
center and laundry rooms. One guide fits all of them, so it's the most convincing demo. The manual is
written around Villa 384 "Be by the Sea" (oceanfront, 3 bed, 2 bath, sleeps 6).

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- https://jekyllrealty.com/vacation-rentals/jekyll-island-communities/villas-by-the-sea/ (community page: 19 properties, studios to 3 bedrooms, on-site restaurant and lounge, tennis courts)
- https://jekyllrealty.com/vacation-rentals/m/properties/villa-384-be-by-the-sea (unit details, pool, boardwalk, fitness center, 3 coin-operated laundry rooms, no pets, no smoking)
- https://jekyllrealty.com/vacation-rentals/m/properties/villa-152-seas-the-day (example of a dog-friendly villa in the same resort)
- https://jekyllrealty.com/about-jekyll-realty/rental-policies/ (check-in 4:00–5:00 pm at the office, checkout 10:00 am, age 23+, pets, smoking, linens, departure condition)
- https://jekyllrealty.com/about-jekyll-realty/directions/ (office location, drive times)
- https://jekyllrealty.com/about-jekyll-realty/contact-us/ (phone, contact form, "restaurant recommendations")
- https://jekyllrealty.com/rental-gear/ (VayKGear beach gear and bike delivery)
- https://jekyllrealty.com/things-to-do/jekyll-island-area-beaches/ and https://jekyllrealty.com/things-to-do/jekyll-island-area-dining/ (beach rules, sea turtle lighting, restaurants)
- https://www.jekyllisland.com/visiting/parking (official island parking fee)

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not verified:** on-site parking at the resort (not stated on
the listing), Wi-Fi network name, door/key arrangements, air conditioning. Jekyll Realty's own directions
page still shows 2019 toll prices ($8/day), so the manual uses the official Jekyll Island figures instead.
Things only the host knows (Wi-Fi password, after-hours access) are left as placeholders; say so in the email.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Villas by the Sea — Jekyll Island`
   - **City, country:** `Jekyll Island, Georgia`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Villas by the Sea, an oceanfront condominium resort on the north end of Jekyll Island, managed by the local team at Jekyll Realty. Your villa has a full kitchen with microwave and dishwasher, satellite TV and Wi-Fi, and the resort has a pool, a boardwalk to the beach, tennis courts, and an on-site restaurant and lounge.

ARRIVAL: Please check in at the Jekyll Realty office between 4:00 pm and 5:00 pm on your arrival day.
The office is at 21 Main Street, Suite 107, in the Beach Village, next to the Convention Center and across from Ameris Bank. Look for the teal and white hanging sign.
If you are arriving after 5:00 pm, please call Jekyll Realty on (912) 635-3301 before you arrive to arrange after-hours access.
Driving onto the island, you'll pass the parking fee gate on the causeway (see the Parking section).

WI-FI: Wi-Fi is included in your villa. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Smoking is not permitted in any Jekyll Realty vacation rental. Violators incur a minimum fee of $250.
No pets unless your villa is listed as pet-friendly and Jekyll Realty has given prior written permission, with a non-refundable $150 pet fee paid in advance.
The person renting must be at least 23 years old. Younger guests must be with a parent or guardian.
Please don't exceed the number of guests your villa sleeps.
One set of bed linens and towels is included. Missing or damaged linens are charged.
On the beach, from May through October, please don't use flashlights or phone lights after dark. Nesting sea turtles and their hatchlings need natural darkness. A red-filtered light is fine.
No bonfires or open fires are allowed on Jekyll Island beaches, and no vehicles on the sand.

RESORT AMENITIES: The community pool, kids' play area, volleyball court and fitness center are for guests to enjoy.
A boardwalk takes you from the resort straight to the beach.
There are 3 coin-operated laundry rooms at the resort.
The resort also has tennis courts and an on-site restaurant and lounge.

SEA AND SAND: There are no lifeguards on any Jekyll Island beach, so swim with care.
Driftwood Beach, on the island's north side, is famous for its sun-bleached fallen trees and was named one of the best beaches in the U.S. by Tripadvisor's Travelers' Choice Awards.
Dogs on a leash are welcome year-round at Great Dunes Beach. Pets are not allowed at Glory Beach Park, to protect nesting migratory birds.
Beach chairs, umbrellas, bikes, kayaks, paddle boards, carts and coolers can be delivered to your villa by VayKGear, Jekyll Realty's gear partner. Look for their email after booking, or ask us.

PARKING: Jekyll Island charges a parking fee to everyone staying on the island, for each night of your visit, wherever you stay.
The daily fee is $10 per standard vehicle (valid until midnight), $15 for vehicles over 8 feet, with $5 extra on some special event days. Pay at the gate or online at jekyllisland.com. The gate reads your license plate.
There is no overnight parking in public parking areas on the island.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 am. Staying later results in additional fees.
Please leave the villa broom clean, with the dishes washed, and the trash bagged and put in the outdoor garbage can.
Thank you for staying with Jekyll Realty!

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about your villa, call Jekyll Realty on (912) 635-3301.

LOCAL TIPS: Hungry? Some favourites from Jekyll Realty's dining list.
- Beachhouse, tap room and artisan dining, 715 North Beachview Drive
- Love Shack BBQ Shrimp & Yardbird, smoked BBQ and fresh Georgia shrimp, 11 Main Street
- EightyOne Kitchen and Bar, locally inspired lunch and dinner, 80 Ocean Way
- Tortuga Jacks, tiki bar and oceanfront venue, 201 Beachview Drive North
- Sunrise Grille, coastal casual dining, 201 North Beachview Drive
- Red Bug Motors Pizza, pizza and Italian dishes, 550 Beachview Drive North
- The Wharf, on the pier with live music, 370 Riverview Drive
- Zachary's Riverhouse, coastal restaurant and bar, 1 Harbor Road
- McGarvey's Wee Pub, Irish pub, 20 Main Street
- Tribuzio's Grille, local eatery with golf views, 322 Captain Wylly Road
Day trips by car take about 1.5 hours to Savannah or Jacksonville, and about 3.25 hours to Charleston.
```

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples**: Jekyll Realty publishes no early check-in or late checkout prices (late departure
only "will result in additional fees"), and VayKGear prices aren't on their site. Tell them they set
their own prices.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Late checkout (until 12:00 PM) | $40 | One more slow beach morning. Subject to availability. |
| Early check-in (from 2:00 PM) | $40 | Get to the beach sooner. Only if the villa is ready; confirmed the day before. |
| Beach day set (2 chairs + umbrella) | $45 | Delivered to your villa by VayKGear and picked up when you leave. |

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | At the Jekyll Realty office, 21 Main St Suite 107, between 4:00 and 5:00 pm; call if arriving after 5 |
| We land late, around 9 pm. What do we do? | Call (912) 635-3301 before arriving to arrange after-hours access |
| Do we have to pay to get onto the island? | Yes, $10 per vehicle per night of the stay, at the gate or online |
| Can we bring our dog? | Only to a pet-friendly villa, with prior written permission and a $150 non-refundable pet fee |
| Can we use a flashlight on the beach at night? | Not May to October (sea turtles); a red-filtered light is OK |
| Where should we eat tonight? | Suggests places from the list (e.g. Love Shack, Tortuga Jacks, The Wharf) |
| What time is checkout and what do we need to do? | 10:00 am; broom clean, dishes washed, trash bagged in the outdoor can |
| ¿Hay socorristas en la playa? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: no lifeguards on any Jekyll Island beach |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; points to the office check-in / hands over to Jekyll Realty |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.
