# Demo guide: The Cornish Way, Lillie's Lookout (St Just, West Cornwall)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of The Cornish Way's cottages *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Lillie's Lookout:** a dog-friendly studio cabin for two near Botallack, minutes from the cliffs
and ten minutes' walk from St Just. It's typical of their far-west cottages: rural lanes, limited
phone signal, dogs welcome, guests arriving after dark. That makes it the perfect place to show a
guide that still works offline once it's opened.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): thecornishway.co.uk/properties/6686 (Lillie's Lookout:
description, parking, Wi-Fi, heater, dog rules, changeover, "limited phone signal", directions advice),
thecornishway.co.uk/frequently-asked-questions (arrival 4–6pm by property, departure 10am, early and
late arrival, Wi-Fi in storms, linen, what to bring, dogs £35, dog sitting, shopping delivery, EV
charging), thecornishway.co.uk/booking-conditions (no smoking or vaping, no stag or hen parties,
rubbish) and thecornishway.co.uk/owners ("We are small by design") and thecornishway.co.uk/meet-the-team (Seb Blakemore, Director; Lowenna Torrie, first point of
contact). **Not published for this cabin:** the exact arrival time (FAQ says most places from 4pm,
some 5pm, large houses 6pm), how keys are collected, the Wi-Fi password and the postcode (the map
marker is approximate). Those are left as "in your arrival information".

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Lillie's Lookout — St Just`
   - **City, country:** `St Just, Cornwall, United Kingdom`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town; the exact location isn't published).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Lillie's Lookout, a cosy studio cabin for two at No Go By, just outside Botallack near St Just, looked after by The Cornish Way. It sits in a corner of the owners' large garden, with your own fenced garden, sea views as far as the Isles of Scilly on a clear day, and the cliffs a few minutes' walk away.

ARRIVAL: Arrival is from 4:00 PM at most of our cottages. Your exact arrival time and how to get in are in your booking confirmation and arrival information.
We're in touch with our housekeepers throughout the day and will always try to let you know if you can arrive earlier.
Arriving late is fine. If you'll arrive after 10:00 PM, please tell us so we can make arrangements.
Phone signal here is limited. If you're arriving in the dark, read the directions before you leave and download them to your phone. Open this guide once before you set off and it will still work without signal.
The lanes are narrow, winding Cornish country lanes with high hedges, so take them slowly.

WI-FI: Free unlimited Wi-Fi. The network name and password are in your arrival information.
Like many rural Cornish properties, the connection can occasionally be slow, and lines sometimes go down in stormy weather. If that happens, let us know and we'll arrange a repair.
Mobile signal is limited at the cabin.

HOUSE RULES: The cabin sleeps 2.
No smoking or vaping anywhere at the property.
No stag or hen parties.
Please put all rubbish in the bins provided.
Lillie's Lookout is on the owners' land, so please be considerate of them and the neighbours.

DOGS: One well-behaved dog is welcome, at £35 per week or short break.
Please keep your dog out of the bedroom area and off the furniture, and rub down muddy or sandy paws before coming in.
The garden is fenced, but we can't guarantee any garden is fully escape-proof, so keep an eye on adventurous dogs.
Please bring your own towels for your dog.
For a small charge we can often arrange dog sitting, dog walking or a drop-in visit while you take a day trip to the Isles of Scilly or the Eden Project. Just ask.

THE CABIN: One-room living with a full-size, well-equipped kitchen, a dining table and breakfast bar, and a big sofa.
The bed deck has a double bed.
The bathroom has a bath with a shower over it and constant hot water.
An independent portable oil-filled heater keeps you warm in the cooler months.
Outside you have your own garden with seating and a barbecue.
Bedding and towels are provided. Please bring your own beach towels.
There are basics like washing-up liquid, tea towels, a dish cloth and some loo roll. This is self-catering, so bring your own food and supplies.

PARKING: There's plenty of room to park at the cabin.
For an electric car, never charge from the household supply or a 3-pin plug. The nearest fast chargers are at Land's End Visitor Centre, the Minack Theatre, Sainsbury's in Penzance and Asda in Hayle. Check zap-map.com for live availability.

CHECKOUT: Departure is by 10:00 AM, so our housekeepers can get the cabin ready for the next guests.
Before you leave, please take all your belongings and rubbish, put the rubbish in the bins provided, and leave the cabin clean and tidy.
Thank you for staying with The Cornish Way!

EMERGENCY: In an emergency, call 999 for fire, police, ambulance or coastguard. For urgent medical advice that isn't an emergency, call NHS 111.
Mobile signal is limited at the cabin. If you can't get through, try the Wi-Fi or move to higher ground.
For anything about the cabin, call The Cornish Way on 01736 279 500 or email hello@thecornishway.co.uk.

LOCAL TIPS: St Just, with its pubs and cafés, is a ten-minute walk. The Queen's Arms in Botallack is even closer.
The cliffs and the South West Coast Path are a few minutes' walk away. Down the road is Wheal Owles, the mine filmed as Wheal Leisure in Poldark, and the Botallack mines are a favourite spot for sunsets.
The wild moors of Carnyorth Common are right behind the cabin.
For groceries, The Cornish Food Box delivers local produce to your door by web or phone order, and Tesco and Sainsbury's both deliver here.
Day trips include the Minack Theatre, Land's End, Sennen beach, Penzance, and further afield the Isles of Scilly and the Eden Project.
```

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| Can we bring our dog? | Yes, one well-behaved dog, £35 per week/short break; not in the bedroom or on furniture |
| We'll arrive around 11pm, is that OK? | Yes, but tell them if it's after 10pm; download directions beforehand |
| What time do we have to leave? | 10:00 AM |
| Can I charge my electric car at the cabin? | No household or 3-pin charging; nearest fast chargers Land's End, Minack, Penzance, Hayle |
| The Wi-Fi isn't working, what do we do? | Can drop in storms; contact The Cornish Way to arrange a repair |
| How far is the pub? | St Just 10 min walk; Queen's Arms in Botallack even closer |
| Can someone mind the dog while we go to Scilly? | Often yes, for a small charge; ask |
| Peut-on fumer sur la terrasse ? (French) | Answers in French: no smoking or vaping anywhere at the property |
| What's the key-safe code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival information / hands over to the team |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to French or German), the **Extras** tab,
and **Airplane mode**: reload the guide to show it still opens without signal.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the dog answer, the French answer, the guide open in Airplane mode) to use in a follow-up email.
