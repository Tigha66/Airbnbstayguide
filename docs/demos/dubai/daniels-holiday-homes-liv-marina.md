# Demo guide: Daniels Holiday Homes, Daniels 2BR Liv Marina (Dubai Marina)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Daniels Holiday Homes' apartments *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Liv Marina:** a 2-bedroom marina-view apartment for 4 guests, featured on their homepage, in
Dubai Marina (their joint-biggest area, with 12 homes). The listing publishes clear house rules (quiet
hours, lost-card fees, community rules), check-in and check-out times, the building's pool, gym and
kids' play area, and their in-person guest relations check-in. Their office is open 9:00am–6:00pm,
so a guide that answers at night is an easy sell.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): danielsholidayhomes.com/properties/454850 ("Daniels 2BR |
Liv Marina | Marina View": description, guest access, amenities, house rules, booking rules,
cancellation policy, AED 2,000 refundable damage deposit and 1–30 night stay limits in the page's
booking widget), danielsholidayhomes.com (homepage: "Every property is managed by our in-house team",
area counts) and danielsholidayhomes.com/contact-us (+971 52 822 6691, Monday–Sunday 9:00am–6:00pm,
contact form). Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not published:** Tourism Dirham amount,
pool/gym hours, unit number, Wi-Fi password, access instructions. The page metadata also lists
info@danielsholidayhomes.com and +971 4 565 3673, but the visible contact page only shows the
form and +971 52 822 6691, so the guide uses that number. The page shows a "propertyLicenseNumber"
(MAR-LIV-MVSJ2) that doesn't look like a DTCM permit, so it's left out.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Daniels 2BR Liv Marina — Dubai Marina`
   - **City, country:** `Dubai Marina, Dubai, United Arab Emirates`
   - **Street address:** `Liv Marina, Al Emreef Street, Dubai Marina` (as published on the listing)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```

Detailed bedding layouts are shared before arrival.

Dubai also charges a Tourism Dirham fee per room per night. Ask our team how it applies to your booking.
```

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples**: Daniels only says late checkout "may incur additional charges" and publishes no
prices, so tell them they set their own.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Early check-in (from 12:00 PM) | AED 150 | Arriving on a morning flight? Get into the apartment sooner. Subject to availability. |
| Late checkout (until 2:00 PM) | AED 150 | A slower last morning on the marina. Subject to availability. |
| Mid-stay housekeeping | AED 200 | A fresh clean and new towels and linen halfway through your stay. |

1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | After 3:00 PM; a guest relations officer meets you |
| Is there a pool and gym? | Yes, outdoor pool, gym and kids' play area at Liv Marina |
| Can we have friends over for a party? | No, events and parties are strictly prohibited |
| What are the quiet hours? | 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM, including the balcony |
| How fast is the Wi-Fi? | At least 250 Mbps; details in the arrival message |
| Do you have a baby crib? | Yes, a crib and a high chair |
| هل يوجد موقف سيارات؟ (Arabic: is there parking?) | Answers in Arabic: free parking; follow building rules |
| Можно ли курить в квартире? (Russian: can I smoke in the apartment?) | Answers in Russian: no smoking inside |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the team |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Arabic) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the quiet hours answer, the Arabic answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
