# Demo guide: Easy Rent Málaga, Magnífico ático frente al mar (Rincón de la Victoria, Spain)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Easy Rent Málaga's apartments *before* contacting
them, so the first message says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."
**This kit is in Spanish** (manual and message): Easy Rent Málaga's site and team work in Spanish.

**Why this penthouse:** a sea-view penthouse 30 m from the beach in Rincón de la Victoria, with two
terraces (the upper one up a spiral staircase, with four sunloungers). The listing publishes everything
the guide needs: check-in window 16:00–22:00, check-out before 12:00, key collection, no smoking / pets
/ parties, a cot for €10 a night, tourist licence, and distances to the bus, supermarket, beach bars and
health centre. Easy Rent also recommends a transfer company, rent-a-car delivered to the flat and
LUGGit luggage storage, so the Extras tab tells a real story.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
reserva.easyrentmalaga.com/alquiler/apartamento-rincon-de-la-victoria-magnifico-atico-vacacional-frente-al-mar-en-rincon-de-la-victoria-457466.html
(description, features, times, notes, distances, licence **VFT/MA/48149**; the Registro Único number ends
in VFT/MA/481496, consistent), reserva.easyrentmalaga.com/ (transfers, rent-a-car, LUGGit lockers, guest
phone +34 722 80 74 76) and easyrentmalaga.com/contacto/ (WhatsApp chat "Soy Jorge, de Easy Rent
Málaga"). Every fact below comes from those pages. **Unverified:** no parking information is published
(the manual says to ask); no transfer or rent-a-car prices are published; the street address isn't
published (postcode 29730 only). Key handover details and the Wi-Fi password are placeholders.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Ático frente al mar — Rincón de la Victoria`
   - **City, country:** `Rincón de la Victoria, Spain`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (only postcode 29730 is published).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.
