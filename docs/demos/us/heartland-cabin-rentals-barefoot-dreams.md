# Demo guide: Heartland Cabin Rentals, Barefoot Dreams (Pigeon Forge, TN)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Heartland Cabin Rentals' cabins *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Barefoot Dreams:** Heartland Cabin Rentals (Gatlinburg, TN) manages more than 40 named cabins
across Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge, Sevierville and Wears Valley, and their check-in, check-out, pet, hot
tub and cancellation policies are the same site-wide, so one guide's structure fits most of their
listings. Barefoot Dreams has the most detail published of any cabin we checked: a full description,
an amenities list, distances to nearby attractions, and 95 guest reviews averaging 4.9 stars. Their
office is only staffed 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, so a guide that answers guests overnight is an easy story to
tell.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): heartlandrentals.com/rentals/barefoot-dreams (the cabin's
own listing), heartlandrentals.com/faq/ (company-wide check-in/out, pets, hot tub and cancellation
policy), heartlandrentals.com/contact/ (email and office details) and the heartlandrentals.com
homepage (company history). Things only the host knows (door code, Wi-Fi password, exact street
address) are left as placeholders, and the email says so.

---

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Barefoot Dreams`
   - **City, country:** `Pigeon Forge, Tennessee`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town; Heartland does not publish exact
     cabin addresses).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Heartland Cabin Rentals has been welcoming guests to the Smokies since 1993. Barefoot Dreams is a 2-bedroom, 2-bath log cabin between Pigeon Forge and Gatlinburg, with mountain views, a private hot tub on the screened deck, and a jacuzzi tub in the king suite. It sleeps up to 6 guests.

ARRIVAL: Regular check-in begins at 4:00 PM and continues until the office closes at 7:00 PM; if you are arriving later, call the office and they will tell you where to find your rental package, which includes directions and your key code. Your package will not be ready if your paperwork is incomplete, your balance is not paid in full, or you have not provided photo ID, and no arrivals are allowed on Thanksgiving or Christmas Day. Your door code is in your arrival message from Heartland Cabin Rentals, please do not share it.

WI-FI: Barefoot Dreams includes complimentary fiber Wi-Fi, and the network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Barefoot Dreams sleeps up to 6 guests, and fire code regulations mean this cannot be exceeded; this cabin does not allow pets, and smoking is not permitted anywhere on the property, inside or outside. Parties are not allowed and this is strictly enforced, loud music, excessive noise, or having more guests than the cabin sleeps will end your stay with no refund, and there is 24-hour security patrolling the area, so please be considerate of neighbouring cabins, especially in the evenings.

CABIN AND APPLIANCES: The king suite upstairs has its own gas fireplace, jacuzzi tub, Smart TV and a porch swing on the deck; the main-floor queen bedroom has its own Smart TV and a full bath with a tub and shower. The open living area has a gas fireplace, a Smart TV and a sleeper sofa, the kitchen is fully equipped for cooking, and there is a washer and dryer in the cabin. Indoor fireplaces are for seasonal use only, from October 1 through April 1, and nothing but firewood should ever be burned in them, no roasting hot dogs or marshmallows. Heartland Cabin Rentals and the cabin owner do not supply firewood or charcoal, so bring your own if you would like a fire or want to use the charcoal grill on the deck.

HOT TUB: Your private hot tub is on the screened deck. Please do not stand on the hot tub cover, it is for insulation only and a damaged cover may be charged at $350; keep the cover on when the tub is not in use, and use it at your own risk.

PARKING: Free parking is included at the cabin. Four-wheel drive and tire chains are recommended, and may be required, during the winter months in case of snow or ice, since some nearby roads are gravel rather than paved.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is 10:00 AM. Staying later costs $50 per hour after 10:00 AM, and late checkouts are only allowed with advance approval from the office. When you leave, call the rental office with your cabin name and departure time, follow the instructions in your check-in package for locking up, and please put used towels in the bathtub and start the dishwasher if you used any dishes.

EMERGENCY: For any life-threatening emergency, call 911. The nearest hospital is LeConte Medical Center in Sevierville. For anything about the cabin during office hours, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM seven days a week, call Heartland Cabin Rentals on (865) 430-9093. Your arrival package also includes an after-hours number for true emergencies such as no heat or air, a burst pipe, or illness, which is only monitored after 9:00 PM; please report any other issue within 24 hours of arrival so the team can fix it during your stay.

LOCAL TIPS: Driving distances from Barefoot Dreams, from the property listing.
- 5.3 miles, about 13 minutes: The Island in Pigeon Forge
- 5.4 miles, about 13 minutes: The Ripken Experience
- 5.8 miles, about 14 minutes: Anakeesta
- 5.9 miles, about 12 minutes: Dollywood
- 6.7 miles, about 16 minutes: Titanic Museum Attraction
- 9 miles, about 18 minutes: Sugarlands Visitor Center, Great Smoky Mountains National Park
- 27.7 miles, about 54 minutes: Cades Cove
Every reservation includes a free Gateway to Fun Pass, with one adult admission per unit per night to attractions that can include Dollywood, Dollywood Splash Country and Anakeesta, ask the office which are included during your stay. The closest grocery stores are a Food City on the Parkway in Pigeon Forge and a Kroger on Wears Valley Road in Pigeon Forge.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `4:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `10:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `(865) 430-9093` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Late checkout (until 11:00 AM) | $50 | Heartland's published late fee is $50 per hour after 10:00 AM checkout, subject to office approval. One real extra hour to pack up slowly. |
| Firewood & charcoal starter bundle | $25 | Heartland doesn't supply firewood or charcoal, so arrive to a ready bundle for the fireplace and deck grill. Demo price. |
| Welcome grocery stock-up | $60 | Coffee, breakfast basics and snacks waiting in the fully equipped kitchen when you arrive. Demo price. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link

1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | 4:00 PM, continuing until the office closes at 7:00 PM; call if arriving later |
| What time do we need to check out? | 10:00 AM; $50/hour after that with office approval only |
| Can we bring our dog? | No, this cabin does not allow pets |
| Can we stand on the hot tub cover? | No, it's for insulation only; a damaged cover may be charged $350 |
| Do we need 4-wheel drive in winter? | Recommended, and may be required, for snow or ice |
| How far is Dollywood from the cabin? | About 5.9 miles, roughly 12 minutes |
| ¿A qué hora es el check-in? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: 4:00 PM |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; hands over to Heartland Cabin Rentals |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.
