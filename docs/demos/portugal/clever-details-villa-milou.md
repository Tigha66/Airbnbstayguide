# Demo guide: Clever Details, Villa Milou (Vilamoura, Algarve)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Clever Details' villas *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Villa Milou:** a 3-suite villa fully renovated in 2026, with a private saltwater pool, a
two-car garage and air conditioning in every room. It's one of their newest listings and has
everything a Vilamoura guest asks about. Clever Details also publishes unusually clear terms
(check-in window, late-arrival meet and greet fees, checkout tidy-up rules, towel changes and the
Loulé tourist tax), so the guide can answer the questions that usually go to their 24/7 line.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- cleverdetails.com/holiday-rentals/item/335 (Villa Milou: sleeps 8 (6 adults + 2 children), 3 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, licence 178394/AL, rooms, distances, €500 security deposit)
- cleverdetails.com/about/terms_conditions.html (check-in 4pm to 6pm, meet and greet fees after 6pm, check-out until 11am, no pets, no smoking inside, no EV charging, noise law, towels and cleaning, AIMA guest registration, tourist tax €2/€1 per person per night)
- cleverdetails.com/about/faqs.html (what's included, 24-hour support, Member Card with partner discounts on private chefs and boat trips)
- cleverdetails.com/about/about-us.html (Rui Justino: "We manage your property as if it was your own hotel")

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not published:** the street address, the pool heating
price, and early check-in or late checkout prices (the terms only say changes must be agreed).
Door codes, key handover details and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders, and you should
say so in the email.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Villa Milou — Vilamoura`
   - **City, country:** `Vilamoura, Portugal`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Villa Milou, an elegant villa in a peaceful area of Vilamoura, fully renovated in 2026 and managed by Clever Details. It has three en-suite bedrooms, a private saltwater pool and space for up to 8 guests (6 adults and 2 children). Our team is available 24 hours a day if you need anything. Rental licence (Alojamento Local) number 178394/AL.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is between 4:00 PM and 6:00 PM. We hand over the keys once the villa is ready and has been inspected by our housekeeper. Your key handover and access details are in your arrival message from Clever Details.
If your flight lands between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM and the villa is ready, we can check you in straight away. If it isn't ready yet, ask whether you can leave your luggage in the villa or in the luggage room at our office near the marina.
Arriving after 6:00 PM? Our meet and greet has an extra charge. It's €35 between 6:00 PM and midnight, €50 between midnight and 1:00 AM, and €100 between 1:00 AM and 9:00 AM (subject to availability). For arrivals between midnight and 9:00 AM, you need to book the previous night as well.
Any change to these times must be agreed with us in advance.

GUEST REGISTRATION: The person who made the booking must be at least 30 years old and stay in the villa for the whole stay. We'll ask for identification documents for every guest on arrival.
Portuguese law requires us to record the arrival, departure and identification details of all non-Portuguese guests and report them to AIMA, the immigration authority.

TOURIST TAX: The municipal tourist tax is paid at check-in. From 1 April to 31 October it's €2 per person per night, and from 1 November to 31 March it's €1 per person per night. It applies for a maximum of 5 nights per person, and guests under 16 don't pay.

SECURITY DEPOSIT: The security deposit for Villa Milou is €500. Two days before arrival we email you a link to authorise a hold on your credit card. Cash isn't accepted. The hold is released 3 to 7 business days after checkout if there are no damages.

WI-FI: Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.
The living room has a Smart TV with cable TV and Netflix, and the leisure room downstairs has a 55-inch TV. There's also a home office with a desk, chair and monitor.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 8 guests (6 adults and 2 children). No extra guests.
No pets.
No smoking inside the villa.
No inflatable pools or other equipment that isn't part of the villa.
Charging electric cars from the villa's electricity is not allowed. There are several charging points in Vilamoura.
Please respect the Portuguese noise law and keep noise down for the neighbours.
Please don't move furniture or decorations without agreeing it with us first.
There are outdoor CCTV cameras at the main entrance, the side kitchen door, the exterior stairs to the garage and inside the garage.

THE VILLA: On the ground floor there's the open-plan kitchen and dining room, a large living room with floor-to-ceiling windows, a bathroom with a walk-in shower and a home office.
The kitchen has a ceramic hob, oven, microwave, fridge-freezer, dishwasher, washing machine, Nespresso coffee machine, kettle and toaster.
Upstairs are three en-suite bedrooms, each with a walk-in wardrobe, walk-in shower, air conditioning and a balcony. The master suite and suite 3 have double beds, and suite 2 has two single beds. The master suite's balcony overlooks the pool and the countryside.
A sofa bed in the living room is suitable for two children.
The lower ground floor has a leisure room with sofas, a TV and a ping-pong table.
Air conditioning (heating and cooling) in every room. Hot water is solar powered.
The villa is self-catering. On arrival we provide two rolls of toilet paper per bathroom and a bin bag in each bin. After that, toilet paper, detergents and other consumables are up to you.

POOL: The private saltwater pool has Roman steps, an outdoor shower and 8 sun loungers, plus a covered lounge area, an outdoor dining table for 8 and a charcoal barbecue.
Pool heating isn't included in the rate. Ask us if you'd like it.
The pool is cleaned and treated by the owner's pool technician, with chemicals added weekly. Their visits are short, but please let them in when they come.

CLEANING AND TOWELS: For 7-night stays towels are changed once a week. For stays of 10 nights or more there's a surface clean and towels and bed linen are changed on day 7. Towels left on the floor won't be replaced.
Please take care with towels. Make-up or stains are charged from the deposit (for example €20 per bath towel).

PARKING: Private garage for two vehicles, reached from outside the villa through the pool area.
Faro Airport is about 25 km away.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is until 11:00 AM. Any change must be agreed with us in advance.
Before you leave, please make sure the villa is tidy for our inspection.
- Clean the barbecue and oven if you used them.
- Wash the dishes and pantry items.
- Take all rubbish out of the villa.
Each of these left undone is a €20 charge from the deposit.
If you forget something, you can collect it at our office (Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM) or arrange shipping with a carrier of your choice.
Thank you for staying with us!

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 112 (the European emergency number).
For anything about the villa, our team is available 24 hours a day on +351 289 314 500 or at info@cleverdetails.com. Our maintenance team responds quickly.

LOCAL TIPS: Distances from Villa Milou are roughly 280 m to the bus stop, 1 km to the supermarket, 2.5 km to the nearest restaurant, and 4 km to both Vilamoura Marina and the beach.
Vilamoura is great for beach days, water sports, golf, tennis, marina walks, nightlife and the casino.
Booking with us gives you the Clever Details Member Card, with discounts at local partners for activities, restaurants, wellness, private chefs, boat trips and home medical services. Ask us for the partner list.
```

## Step 3: Property details

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The meet
and greet fee is **their real published price**; the other two are **demo examples** (their terms only
say changes must be agreed), so tell them they set their own prices.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Late arrival meet and greet (6pm to midnight) | €35 | Landing late? We'll meet you at the villa and hand over the keys. |
| Late checkout (until 2:00 PM) | €50 | One more swim in your private pool before you go. Subject to availability. |
| Pool heating | €25 per day | Warm water in the cooler months. Ask us to switch it on before you arrive. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your phone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| Our flight lands at 11pm, can we still check in? | Yes, meet and greet 6pm–midnight costs €35 |
| How much is the tourist tax? | €2 per person per night April–October, €1 November–March, max 5 nights, under-16s free, paid at check-in |
| Can I charge my Tesla at the villa? | No; use one of the charging points in Vilamoura |
| How far is the beach? | About 4 km (marina also 4 km); bus stop 280 m |
| What do we need to do before checkout? | Clean BBQ/oven, wash dishes, take out rubbish (€20 each if not), out by 11am |
| Can we bring our dog? | No pets |
| Quando é que o depósito é devolvido? (Portuguese) | Answers in Portuguese: 3 to 7 business days after checkout, if no damage |
| Où peut-on garer la voiture ? (French) | Answers in French: private garage for two cars |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Portuguese) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the late-arrival answer, the Portuguese answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
