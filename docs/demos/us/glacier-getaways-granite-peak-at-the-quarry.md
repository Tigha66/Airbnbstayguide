# Demo guide: Glacier Getaways, Granite Peak at the Quarry (Whitefish, MT)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Glacier Getaways' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Granite Peak at the Quarry:** the Quarry is a townhome development where Glacier Getaways manages
more than one unit (their site also lists "Big Mountain Views at The Quarry"), so the shared amenities
(hot tub, clubhouse, gym, summer pool) and the walk to town carry over. The listing page is detailed,
and their published terms give clear company-wide check-in, checkout and quiet hours.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- glacier-getaways.com/property/73-granite-peak-at-the-quarry (description, amenities, rules, hot tub hours)
- glacier-getaways.com/terms/ (check-in after 4:00 PM, checkout before 10 AM, codes 7 days before arrival, quiet hours)
- glacier-getaways.com/our-services/ (concierge, grocery delivery, housekeeping, massage, chefs, ski delivery, baby gear)
- glacier-getaways.com/guides/grocery-delivery/ (published grocery delivery prices)
- glacier-getaways.com/guides/visiting-glacier-national-park/ (2026 park entry and Logan Pass rules)
- glacier-getaways.com/guides/whitefish-winter-activities-guide/ and /about-us/

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not verified / left out:** door code and Wi-Fi details
(placeholders), the exact street address (not published), and any fire-ban or wildlife rules for this
unit (not on the listing). Park rules change each year; the manual tells guests to check nps.gov.

---

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Granite Peak at the Quarry — Whitefish`
   - **City, country:** `Whitefish, Montana`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Granite Peak at the Quarry, a modern mountain townhome in one of Whitefish's finest vacation rental developments, looked after by Jamie, Andy and the local team at Glacier Getaways. Downtown Whitefish is a ten-minute walk, City Beach is half a mile away, and the S.N.O.W. bus stop to the ski hill is just across the street.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is after 4:00 PM on your arrival day. Check-in is contactless. Your check-in instructions and access codes are emailed to you 7 days before arrival. Please don't share your code.
If you need any help on the way, call or text Glacier Getaways on 406-200-8512.
For direct bookings and Vrbo, a $750 damage deposit is pre-authorised on your card at check-in and released 5 days after check-out if no charges apply (this doesn't apply to Airbnb bookings).

WI-FI: Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your check-in instructions.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests and 2 cars.
Everyone in your group must be over 25 or accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.
Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 9:00 AM.
No smoking anywhere at the property.
No pets at this property under any circumstances.
No events or parties at any time.
No RVs or Sprinter vans for more than 24 hours (HOA rule).
Please be a good neighbour: keep noise, traffic and parking respectful of the community.

TOWNHOME: Over 1,000 sq ft, with no shared walls between townhomes. Electric blinds on the vaulted windows.
Downstairs is a great room with a fully stocked kitchen (Jura espresso machine, bread maker, dishwasher, microwave, kettle, toaster, blender), a breakfast bar, a living room with a cozy fireplace, and a king bedroom with its own bathroom. The living room sectional pulls out into a queen sleeper.
Upstairs, the loft bedroom has two twin XL beds that can be clipped together into a king, its own bathroom, and a built-in desk with a wine bar.
The large second-floor patio has a pizza oven, patio heaters, a retractable awning and an outdoor lounge table with a fireplace, perfect for s'mores. Off the kitchen there's a Weber gas grill (propane provided).
Air conditioning, central heating, washer and dryer, three smart TVs, beach towels, linens and towels are all included. A baby cot is available on request for a fee.

HOT TUB AND CLUBHOUSE: The Quarry's hot tub, clubhouse and fitness centre are open year-round, 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM. The outdoor pool is open in the summer season only. There's also a lounge and game room.

PARKING: Two dedicated off-street parking spaces. The maximum is 2 cars.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is before 10:00 AM on your departure day. Thank you for staying with us!
Please check you have everything. Shipping forgotten items costs a $75 convenience fee plus postage.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the townhome, call or text Glacier Getaways on 406-200-8512, or email info@glacier-getaways.com.

LOCAL TIPS: From the Quarry:
- 1 minute walk: S.N.O.W. bus stop to Whitefish Mountain Resort
- 3 minutes walk: three bars and restaurants
- 10 minutes walk: downtown Whitefish
- Half a mile: Whitefish City Beach (summer)
- Less than 30 miles: Glacier National Park
For 2026, Glacier National Park has no vehicle reservation or timed entry, but you still need an entrance fee or National Parks Pass. From July 1 to September 7, private parking at Logan Pass is limited to 3 hours with a free permit from the kiosk. For long hikes from Logan Pass, like the Highline Trail, book an express shuttle ticket on Recreation.gov. Always check nps.gov/glac for current road and shuttle status.
In winter, ski Whitefish Mountain Resort (877-SKI-FISH), with night skiing on select dates. Black Tie Ski Rentals delivers skis and snowboards to your door. Try cross-country skiing at the Glacier Nordic Center, skating at Stumptown Ice Den, snowshoeing, dog sledding or a sleigh ride at Bar W Guest Ranch.
Our free concierge can arrange activities, transportation, private chefs, in-home massage, baby gear (BabyQuip) and outdoor adventures with BetaHouse. Just ask us.
```

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |

| What time can we check in? | After 4:00 PM; codes and instructions emailed 7 days before |
| Is the hot tub open at 10:30 PM? | Yes, the hot tub is open 5:00 AM–11:00 PM year-round |
| Can we swim in the pool in January? | No, the outdoor pool is summer only |
| How do we get to the ski hill? | S.N.O.W. bus stop is a 1-minute walk across the street |
| Do we need a reservation for Glacier National Park? | Not in 2026, but a park fee or pass is needed; Logan Pass parking 3h limit Jul 1–Sep 7 |
| Can you stock the fridge before we arrive? | Yes, grocery delivery, $100 up to 25 items, 48 hours' notice |
| Can we bring our dog? | No pets at this property |
| Wie lautet das WLAN-Passwort? (German) | Answers in German: it's in your check-in instructions |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to German) and the **Extras** tab.
