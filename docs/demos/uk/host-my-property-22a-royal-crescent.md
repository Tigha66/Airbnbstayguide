# Demo guide: Host My Property, 22A The Royal Crescent (Bath)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Host My Property's homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why 22A The Royal Crescent:** a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom lower-ground apartment with its own entrance
and front courtyard on Bath's most famous street. It's their most recognisable listing, and it has the
classic Bath guest questions in one place: where to park (no parking at the door), when the key-safe
code arrives, whether bags can be dropped early, and how far everything is on foot.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): their guest booking site (Lodgify), linked from the Host My
Property Properties page and sharing the same phone number and Instagram:
holidayhomesbath.com/en/22a-the-royal-crescent---stunning-3-bedroom-apartment (description, sleeps 6,
amenities, check-in 04:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM, "Lower Ground Apartment 22 Royal Crescent"),
holidayhomesbath.com/en/faq (key safe, early check-in / late checkout, bag drop, parking prices),
holidayhomesbath.com/en/discover (museums and spas), and hostmyproperty.co.uk/about/ and
/services/24-7-guest-support/ (Daniel Daly, founded 2015, "24/7 availability").
**Conflict to confirm:** the FAQ says check-out is 10am, the property page says 11:00 AM. The kit uses
the property page. **Not published:** pets and smoking rules, Wi-Fi details, key-safe code. The
£17.10/day car park price is from their FAQ and may be out of date. Note also that
hostmyproperty.co.uk currently carries dozens of Dutch casino spam posts in its footer links (the site
looks compromised); don't mention it in the first email, but it may be a kind heads-up later.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `22A The Royal Crescent — Bath`
   - **City, country:** `Bath, United Kingdom`
   - **Street address:** `Lower Ground Apartment, 22 Royal Crescent, Bath` (published on their listing)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to 22A The Royal Crescent, a luxury 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment with its own private entrance and courtyard on Bath's famous Royal Crescent. It sleeps up to 6 guests and is looked after by Host My Property, a Bath-based family business. Bath's shops, bars and restaurants are a short walk away through the park.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 4:00 PM. This is self check-in. The keys are in a key safe, and the full address and key-safe code are sent to you a few days before your stay. Please don't share the code.
The apartment is in the lower ground floor of 22 Royal Crescent, with its own entrance from the courtyard at the front.
Early check-in can't be booked in advance. If the apartment is ready early we will always let you know on the day of arrival.
This is not a serviced apartment, so there are no staff or keys at the property until it has been cleaned. If you arrive early, there is a luggage store directly opposite Bath Spa train station that you can book in advance at bathluggagestorage.com.

WI-FI: Wireless broadband internet is included. The network name and password are in your arrival details.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests.
The Royal Crescent is a quiet residential street of historic homes. Please keep noise down, especially in the courtyard and in the evenings.
The apartment has smoke and carbon monoxide detectors. Please don't cover or disconnect them.
If anything is broken or not working, tell us straight away so we can fix it for you.

THE APARTMENT: Three large bedrooms with four beds, including a king-size master bedroom with a dressing area, and two bathrooms.
Comfortable modern lounge with TV.
Fully equipped kitchen with oven and hob, microwave, dishwasher, fridge, coffee machine and cooking utensils.
Washing machine, clothes dryer, iron and board, hair dryer, bed linen and essentials are provided. Heating is available.

PARKING: There is no parking at the apartment, and the city is compact and best explored on foot.
The nearest long-stay car park is Charlotte Street, a 5 to 10 minute walk through Royal Victoria Park. Avon Street and Manvers Street are also long-stay car parks. Long-stay parking was £17.10 per day on our last update, paid with the MiPermit app or cash.
By train, Bath Spa station is in the city centre, with a taxi rank right outside the main exit. The bus and coach station is a minute's walk away on Dorchester Street.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 11:00 AM.
A late checkout is only possible if no guests are arriving after you. We'll let you know if it's available.
Please leave the keys as instructed in your arrival details and make sure the door is locked behind you. Thank you for staying with us!

EMERGENCY: In an emergency, call 999 for fire, police or ambulance. For urgent medical advice that isn't an emergency, call NHS 111.
For anything about the apartment, call Host My Property on 01225 587071 or email hello@holidayhomesbath.com. We give you space to enjoy your stay, but we're on hand locally if you need us.

LOCAL TIPS: Bath is about 90 minutes from London by train, 19 miles from Bristol Airport, and has over 40 museums.
- Museums. The Roman Baths, Bath Abbey, the Holburne Museum, the Jane Austen Centre, No 1 Royal Crescent (right on your doorstep), Sally Lunn's historic eating house, the Museum of Bath Architecture and the American Museum and Gardens.
- Galleries. Victoria Art Gallery, the Museum of East Asian Art and the Herschel Museum of Astronomy.
- Spas. Thermae Bath Spa, the city's natural thermal spa, plus the spas at the Royal Crescent Hotel, the Gainsborough and the Bath Priory.
- Architecture. Pulteney Bridge, the weir, the Circus (a few minutes' walk from the Crescent) and the Royal Crescent itself.
Royal Victoria Park is right in front of the Crescent, with gardens, a playground and the walk into town.
There is also a hop-on hop-off sightseeing bus tour around the city.
```
