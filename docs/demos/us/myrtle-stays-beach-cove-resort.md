# Demo guide: Myrtle Stays, Beach Cove Resort (North Myrtle Beach, SC)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Myrtle Stays' condos *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Beach Cove Resort:** Myrtle Stays lists about 66 condos in this one oceanfront resort on their
own site (more than any other building they manage). The pools, lazy river, self check-in and
"what is supplied" list are the same across those units, so one guide fits dozens of their listings.
The example unit is Beach Cove Resort 1005 (2 bedrooms, sleeps 8, 10th floor).

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): myrtlestays.com/vacation-rentals/rental/467772/ (Beach Cove
Resort 1005, full description, amenity list and "A few additional details for your stay"),
myrtlestays.com/beachfront-rentals/ (unit count) and the site footer (Myrtle Stays, LLC, (843) 702-9979,
info@myrtlestays.com). Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not published:** check-in and
check-out times (the listing only says self check-in with a keypad), parking pass or wristband
details, and quiet hours. "No pets" comes from the listing's data (pets: 0), not a written rule;
confirm it. Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders, and you should say so in the email.

---

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Beach Cove Resort 1005 — North Myrtle Beach`
   - **City, country:** `North Myrtle Beach, South Carolina`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to your oceanfront condo at Beach Cove Resort in North Myrtle Beach, managed by Myrtle Stays. You're on the 10th floor with coastline views from your private balcony, a 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom home that sleeps up to 8 guests.

ARRIVAL: Your check-in time is in your booking confirmation. Check-in is self check-in with a keypad on the condo door, so you can skip the front desk. Your door code is sent in your arrival message from Myrtle Stays. Please don't share it.
We're not on-site, but we're always reachable through your booking platform for any question during your stay.
Before arrival, please complete the rental agreement and damage waiver through CHARGEautomation. The mandatory, non-refundable $45 Damage Protection Fee is paid within 1 hour of booking and covers up to $1,000 of accidental damage.

WI-FI: Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi (500+ Mbps). The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 8 guests.
This condo is not pet-friendly.
We provide starter essentials only. Additional supplies, and replacement towels and linens, are the guest's responsibility. Towels and linens are not refreshed during your stay.
The on-site restaurant, arcade and fitness center are for owners only and are not open to guests.
Off-season notice. After Labor Day and before Memorial Day, resorts along the Grand Strand may do maintenance or construction, so you may hear daytime construction noise. Off-season rates already reflect this.

THE CONDO: Main bedroom with a king bed, a 50-inch Smart TV, a private full bathroom and access to the oceanfront balcony.
Guest bedroom with two memory foam double beds and a 50-inch Smart TV.
A queen sofa sleeper in the living room. The sofa sleeper sheets are on the top shelf of the bedroom closet.
Full kitchen with granite countertops, modern appliances and a Keurig, plus a dining table for six and two seats at the kitchen bar.
Washer and dryer in the condo, plus an iron, ironing board and hair dryer.
What we supply. Hand soap, a sponge, a small dish soap, a starter roll of toilet paper plus one on the roll per bathroom, small bar soap, shampoo and conditioner, two dish towels, two make-up cloths, one bath towel and one washcloth per guest, two hand towels and a bath mat per bathroom.
Not supplied. Paper towels, spices, sweeteners, coffee or coffee filters. Bring your own pool and beach towels.

POOL: Guests can use the resort's three outdoor pools (seasonally heated), a kiddie pool, a 350-foot lazy river, two outdoor hot tubs, an indoor pool and hot tub, and a seasonal poolside bar.
There is direct beach access from the resort.
Beach chairs and umbrellas are not provided. You can rent them seasonally from the lifeguards on the beach.

PARKING: Free parking is available at the resort.
A car is highly recommended for exploring the wider Myrtle Beach area. Uber and Lyft are widely available, and Coast RTA buses run limited routes.

CHECKOUT: Your check-out time is in your booking confirmation. Thank you for staying with us!
Check-out is contactless, so there's no need to stop at a desk. Please lock the condo door when you leave.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the condo, contact Myrtle Stays through your booking platform, call (843) 702-9979 or email info@myrtlestays.com.

LOCAL TIPS: Driving times from Beach Cove Resort:
- 2 minutes to Barefoot Bistro (American classics)
- 4 minutes to Barefoot Landing (shops and family attractions) and The Marina Bar and Grill on the Intracoastal Waterway
- 5 minutes to Flying Fish Public Market and Grill (seafood), Blueberry's Grill (breakfast and lunch), House of Blues and the Alabama Theatre
- 6 minutes to 21 Main at North Beach (steaks and sushi), Alligator Adventure and Duplin Winery
- 8 minutes to Tanger Outlets and Molly Darcy's Irish Pub
- 9 minutes to OD Pavilion Amusement Park
- 10 minutes to Coastal North Town Center (shops and a grocery market)
- 12 minutes to Cherry Grove Fishing Pier
Barefoot Landing and many restaurants are close enough to walk. Golf cart and bike rentals are popular ways to get around North Myrtle Beach.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `Confirm` (not published; leave empty if the field needs a time) |
| Check-out | `Confirm` (not published) |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `(843) 702-9979` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples** (Myrtle Stays doesn't publish extras), so tell them they set their own prices.
The beach towel pack fits their own note that guests must bring pool and beach towels.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Beach towel pack | $25 | Pool and beach towels for your group, waiting in the condo. |
| Early check-in | $40 | More beach time on arrival day. Subject to the cleaning schedule. |
| Late checkout | $40 | One more slow morning on the balcony. Subject to availability. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| Is the pool heated? | Three outdoor pools, seasonally heated; plus an indoor pool and hot tub |
| Do you provide beach chairs? | No; rent them seasonally from the lifeguards on the beach |
| Do we need to bring beach towels? | Yes, bring your own pool and beach towels |
| Is there coffee? | There's a Keurig/coffee maker, but no coffee or filters supplied |
| Can we use the fitness center? | No, it's for owners only (same for the restaurant and arcade) |
| Where's the nearest grocery store? | Coastal North Town Center, about 10 minutes' drive |
| Can we bring our dog? | No, the condo isn't pet-friendly (or hands over to the host) |
| ¿Dónde están las sábanas del sofá cama? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: top shelf of the bedroom closet |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the beach chairs answer, the Spanish answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
