# Demo guide: OC Beachfront Rentals, Belmont Towers 607 (Ocean City, MD)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of OC Beachfront Rentals' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Belmont Towers 607:** Belmont Towers is a direct-oceanfront building at the start of the Boardwalk
where OCBR manages several units (402, 605, 607, 609 and a two-condo listing), and the listing itself
says "we have other beautiful condos in this building". One guide fits all of them: same gated parking,
sun deck, pool, gym and sauna, same walk to Thrasher's and Trimper's.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- booking.ocbeachfrontrentals.com/en/belmont-towers-607-boardwalk-ocean-front-pool-gym-sauna (their
  Lodgify direct-booking site: description, amenities, house rules, check-in 04:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM,
  address 2 Dorchester St, pool season, smoking fine, fireplace and fire pit notices)
- ocbeachfrontrentals.com/ (4.85 average rating from 20,000+ Airbnb and Vrbo reviews; amenities)
- ocbeachfrontrentals.com/contact/ (reception hours 7:00 AM–11:00 PM EST, phone, email)
- ocbeachfrontrentals.com/guest-experience/ (contactless check-in, binoculars, beach towels)
- ocbeachfrontrentals.com/about-ocbeach-front-rentals/ (family-owned, team names)
- ocbeachfrontrentals.com/preferredguest/ (3 PM early check-in and 12 PM late check-out exist as loyalty perks)

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not verified / left out:** the door code, Wi-Fi network and
password, the after-hours phone number guests receive, and the exact bathroom count (the page says both
"2 Bathrooms" and "3 b 2.5 ba"). The gas-fireplace and fire-pit notices are temporary; check they're
still current before sending.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Belmont Towers 607 — Ocean City`
   - **City, country:** `Ocean City, Maryland`
   - **Street address:** `2 Dorchester St` (published on the listing; or leave empty).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```

The huge balcony, reachable from the living room and every bedroom, faces the ocean sunrise and the sunset over the bay. Your hosts are OC Beachfront Rentals, a family-owned local team with a real person available 24 hours a day.

Belmont Towers is on the Boardwalk between Dorchester Street and 1st Street. There's an elevator in the building.
Need a pack 'n' play? Ask us as soon as your booking is confirmed.

WI-FI: Free high-speed wireless internet and cable TV are included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

THE CONDO: Three bedrooms. The primary bedroom has a king bed, the second a queen, and the third a bunk bed (two doubles) plus a single trundle. There's also a queen sleeper in the living area.

Washer and dryer, iron and board, hair dryer, air conditioning and heating are all in the condo.
Bed linens, towels, beach towels and a complimentary starter kit of toiletries and household basics are provided.
Binoculars, games and books are there for you to enjoy.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.

- Take a boat tour to spot dolphins, try the local seafood, or catch a live show at the Performing Arts Center.
- Summer brings concerts and fireworks. Fall has wine festivals and car shows, with temperatures from the high 60s to mid 70s. Winter is quiet, with holiday lights and events.
- Flying in? Salisbury-Ocean City Wicomico Regional Airport is about 40 minutes away.
Ask us for restaurant and activity recommendations any time. We love to help.
```

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples**: OCBR already gives 3 PM early check-in, 12 PM late check-out and a stocked "mini bar"
to loyalty members, but publishes no price for other guests, so tell them they set their own.
