# Demo guide: Myrtle Beach Destinations, Caravelle Resort 745 (Myrtle Beach, SC)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Myrtle Beach Destinations' condos *before*
contacting them, so the first message says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Caravelle Resort:** they list 13 condos at Caravelle Resort, joint-most of any building on their
site (with Ocean Forest Villas). Parking passes, keypad self check-in, beach access and supplies are
the same across those units, so one guide fits all of them. The example unit is Caravelle Resort 745
Sea View (1 bedroom, 1 bath, sleeps 6).

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): www.myrtledestinations.com/rentals/caravelle-resort-745-sea-view
(description, parking, entry, beach, cleaning and linens, amenity list, "Pets Not Allowed", address
6900 North Ocean Boulevard), www.myrtledestinations.com/all-properties (unit count),
www.myrtledestinations.com/about-us (veteran-owned, VRMA 2025 housekeeping award, team: Clayton,
Director of Operations; Austin and Makayla, Senior Operations Managers) and
www.myrtledestinations.com/contact-us (guest line 843-585-4373, office at 1580 Stockholder Ave Unit D).
Every fact below comes from those pages. **Note:** myrtlebeachdestinations.com (the domain in the
outreach batch) is now a parked page. Their real site is **myrtledestinations.com**. **Not published:**
check-in and check-out times, quiet hours, age rule, and resort amenity hours ("vary based on the
seasonal conditions"). Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders, and you should say
so in the message.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Caravelle Resort 745 — Myrtle Beach`
   - **City, country:** `Myrtle Beach, South Carolina`
   - **Street address:** `6900 North Ocean Boulevard, Myrtle Beach, SC` (published on their listing)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Caravelle Resort 745, a sea-view condo in an oceanfront resort in Myrtle Beach, managed by Myrtle Beach Destinations. It's perfect for a couple or a small family, sleeping up to 6 guests, with a private balcony and resort amenities for the whole crew.

ARRIVAL: Your check-in time is in your booking confirmation. Check-in is self check-in with a keypad, so there's no need to stop at a desk. Your door code is sent in your arrival message from Myrtle Beach Destinations. Please don't share it.
The building has an elevator.

WI-FI: Wireless internet is included, plus cable TV. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests.
No pets.
The HOA asks that guests don't bring golf carts, motorcycles, trailers, RVs or boats into the parking lot.
Each stay includes towels, linens, and a starter kit of toilet paper, paper towels, trash bags, shampoo and conditioner. You're responsible for any resupply during your stay, so we suggest bringing extra supplies.

THE CONDO: One bedroom with two queen beds, and a bathroom with a full bathtub and shower.
The living area has plenty of seating, a sleeper sofa and a murphy pull-out bed, with sliding glass doors to your private balcony with seating.
The kitchen is stocked with cookware, dishes, a dishwasher, oven, microwave, toaster and coffee maker.
A professional cleaning company deep cleans the condo before every booking.

RESORT POOLS: The resort has an indoor pool, an outdoor pool and a hot tub. The amenities are owned and run by the HOA, and opening hours vary with the season.

SAND AND SURF: The beach is a few minutes' walk through the resort. There are outdoor showers around the complex to rinse off sand.
Feel free to bring your own beach towels, toys and chairs.

PARKING: Free parking in a private lot with secured entry. You'll have 2 parking passes. Hang them on your rear-view mirror.
No golf carts, motorcycles, trailers, RVs or boats in the lot.

CHECKOUT: Your check-out time is in your booking confirmation. Thank you for staying with us!
Please lock the condo door when you leave.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the condo, call the Myrtle Beach Destinations guest line on 843-585-4373.

LOCAL TIPS: You're on North Ocean Boulevard, in an oceanfront resort right on the beach.
Laundromats are nearby if you need to wash beach towels.
Ask our local team for their favorite restaurants and things to do on the Grand Strand. We're happy to help.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `Confirm` (not published; leave empty if the field needs a time) |
| Check-out | `Confirm` (not published) |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `843-585-4373` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The prices
are **demo examples** (they don't publish extras), so tell them they set their own prices. The beach
gear fits their note that guests bring their own chairs and towels.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Beach chairs and umbrella | $45 | Two beach chairs and an umbrella, waiting in the condo. |
| Early check-in | $40 | More beach time on arrival day. Subject to the cleaning schedule. |
| Late checkout | $40 | One more slow morning on the balcony. Subject to availability. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| How many parking passes do we get? | 2, hang them on the rear-view mirror |
| Can we bring our golf cart? | No; the HOA doesn't allow golf carts, motorcycles, trailers, RVs or boats in the lot |
| Is there an indoor pool? | Yes, plus an outdoor pool and hot tub; hours vary by season |
| How far is the beach? | A few minutes' walk through the resort; outdoor showers to rinse sand |
| Do you provide beach chairs? | No, bring your own (or the beach chairs extra) |
| Will there be toilet paper? | A starter kit only; bring extra supplies |
| Can we bring our dog? | No pets |
| ¿Cuántas camas hay? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: two queens, a sleeper sofa and a murphy bed, up to 6 guests |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the parking answer, the Spanish answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up.
