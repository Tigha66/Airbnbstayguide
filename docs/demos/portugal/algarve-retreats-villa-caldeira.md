# Demo guide: Algarve Retreats, Villa Caldeira (Lagos, Algarve)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Algarve Retreats' villas *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Villa Caldeira:** a 4-bedroom, single-level family villa with a private heated pool, a 10-minute
walk from Meia Praia beach, Lagos Marina and the old town. It shows the classic Algarve guest
questions in one place: pool heating (charged extra), beach towels, cots, the bus station, the
supermarket, airport transfers from Faro, check-in times and fees. Their terms publish exact early
check-in and late checkout prices, so the extras are real.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- algarve-retreats.com/property-to-rent/villa-caldeira.htm (description, rooms, facilities, AL number 47821/AL)
- algarve-retreats.com/rent/rental-terms-and-conditions (check-in after 16:00, check-out 10:00, out-of-hours check-in charge, early check-in and late checkout prices, damage deposit pre-authorisation)
- algarve-retreats.com/rent/holiday-extras/airport-transfers (Faro to Lagos return €150 for up to 4)
- algarve-retreats.com/rent/emergency-numbers (112, Lagos Hospital, Lagos tourist office)
- algarve-retreats.com/rent/sef-accommodation-bulletin (online guest registration form, by booking reference)
- algarve-retreats.com/about/our-story (the 1996 sunrise story)

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not published:** maximum guest count for this villa
(the terms say occupancy follows the Alojamento Local licence), the pool heating price, and the
tourist tax amount. The listing also shows "AEM - 0024", which is unexplained, so it's left out.
The "24-hour helpline" from our prospect list wasn't found on the pages checked this time, so the email
doesn't mention it. Door codes, key collection and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders,
and you should say so in the email.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Villa Caldeira — Lagos`
   - **City, country:** `Lagos, Portugal`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Villa Caldeira, a 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom family villa on one level in a peaceful cul-de-sac in Lagos, managed by Algarve Retreats. It has enclosed landscaped gardens, a large private pool and a BBQ area, and Meia Praia beach, Lagos Marina and the historic old town are each about a 10-minute walk away. Licence (Alojamento Local) number 47821/AL.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is after 4:00 PM (16:00) on your day of arrival, unless you've arranged something else with us in advance.
Our normal working hours are Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 18:00. Check-ins outside these hours have an additional charge, so please tell us your arrival time.
Your key and access details are in your arrival message from Algarve Retreats. Please don't share them.
Early check-in is sometimes possible for a fee. It's £40 for check-in at 14:00, £80 at 12:00 and £120 at 10:00. Ask us and we'll check.
We need a damage deposit pre-authorisation on your card before you arrive. Without it we can't give access to the villa. It's a temporary hold, not a charge, and it's released after your stay if there's no damage.

GUEST REGISTRATION: Portuguese law requires us to register the passport or ID details of foreign guests with the authorities. Please complete the online accommodation bulletin on our website (algarve-retreats.com, under Rent, "SEF Accommodation Bulletin") with your booking reference and email before you arrive.
Some Algarve councils charge a tourist tax. We'll tell you if anything is due for your stay.

WI-FI: Full internet and Wi-Fi are included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.
The lounge has a 52-inch TV with IPTV and over 85 UK channels. Streaming can sometimes be intermittent, depending on supply.

HOUSE RULES: Only the guests named on your booking form may stay. The maximum number of guests is set by the villa's licence.
Please tell us when you book if your group is single sex or made up of adults under 30, because many owners don't accept these groups.
No parties or events without asking us first. Extra cleaning charges and a further deposit may apply.
Please report any breakage or damage as soon as possible, especially broken glass in or near the pool.

THE VILLA: Bedroom 1 is the master bedroom, with its own bathroom and patio doors straight onto the pool and gardens. Bedrooms 2, 3 and 4 are twin rooms, and bedrooms 3 and 4 have patio doors to the garden.
Bathrooms are the master en-suite, a second en-suite, and a shower room with WC off the kitchen.
The kitchen has a washing machine, dishwasher, oven, hob and coffee maker. The cooker may not have a grill, so use the BBQ or a toaster instead.
The lounge has an L-shaped sofa and a log-burning fire, plus a sun room with a second dining area.
Full air conditioning. Iron and ironing board, and a hair dryer.
Bed linen and towels are provided. Beach towels are available for a supplement.
Extra beds, cots and high chairs are available for a supplement. Ask us before you arrive.
A table tennis table is available for guests.
Being on one level, the villa suits young children and anyone with walking difficulties.

POOL: The large private pool is just for your group. Pool heating is available in the cooler months for a supplement. Please ask us for the price before you arrive.
Pools sometimes need maintenance or a filter change, and our team may visit to look after the pool and garden.

PARKING: If you hire a car, you can park directly outside the villa. You don't really need a car here, because the beach, marina, old town and supermarket are all walkable.
Airport transfers from Faro can be booked with us. A return transfer Faro to Lagos is €150 for up to 4 people, and child seats are available on request.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM on your day of departure.
Late checkout is sometimes possible for a fee. It's £40 until 12:00, £80 until 15:00 and £120 until 18:00. Ask us in advance.
Please turn off the air conditioning, lock the doors and leave the keys as described in your arrival message. Thank you for staying with us!

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 112 (the European emergency number).
Lagos Hospital is on 282 763 034. Lagos police (PSP) is on 282 762 930.
For anything about the villa, call Algarve Retreats on +351 282 183 798.

LOCAL TIPS: Meia Praia beach is a 10-minute walk. The beach is 6 km long with a boardwalk all along it, and you can rent a kayak there and explore the grottoes. Pack a picnic and find one of the many secluded coves.
Lagos Marina is about 5 minutes away, with colourful waterfront restaurants, bars and cafés. Cross the Marina footbridge to reach the town.
The bus station is on the other side of the Marina footbridge, with buses to smaller resorts and beaches nearby.
The old town is about 10 minutes along a palm-lined promenade. In the evening wander its cobbled streets for street markets, musicians and local food.
The nearest supermarket is Pingo Doce, about a 10-minute walk.
Water parks and theme parks are within a 25-minute drive.
Lagos tourist information centre is on 282 763 031.
We can also arrange car hire, trips, activities and excursions. Just ask.
```

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your phone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | After 16:00; early check-in from £40 (14:00) if available; out-of-hours arrivals cost extra |
| Is the pool heated? | Heating available in cooler months for a supplement; ask for the price |
| Do we need a car? | Not really; beach, marina, old town and supermarket are walkable; parking outside if you hire one |
| How do we get from Faro airport? | Return transfer Faro–Lagos €150 for up to 4, child seats on request |
| Why do you need our passports? | Portuguese law; fill in the online accommodation bulletin with your booking reference |
| Can we get a cot? | Yes, cots and high chairs for a supplement |
| Quanto custa a saída tardia? (Portuguese) | Answers in Portuguese: £40 until 12:00, £80 until 15:00, £120 until 18:00 |
| Wie lautet das WLAN-Passwort? (German) | Answers in German: it's in your arrival message |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Portuguese) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the pool heating answer, the Portuguese answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
