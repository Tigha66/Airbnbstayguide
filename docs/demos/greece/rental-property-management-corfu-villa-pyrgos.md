# Demo guide: Rental Property Management Corfu, Villa Pyrgos (Liapades, Corfu)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of RPM Corfu's villas *before* contacting them, so
the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Villa Pyrgos:** a renovated 2-bedroom villa with a private pool and sea and sunset views over
Paleokastritsa, in Liapades on Corfu's west coast. It's the most detailed of their guest listings
(layout, pool size, parking, house rules), and RPM Corfu already sells guest extras (early check-in,
late checkout, beach towels, car and boat hire, airport transfers) on rentalcorfu.com, which a guide
can surface to every guest.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- paleokastritsa.rentalcorfu.com/villa-pyrgos-liapades/ (description, renovated 2019–2020, 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms + separate toilet, sleeps 4/5, pool 7 m x 4 m, 4,000 m² garden, private parking, A/C, fireplace, heating, baby bed, "50 meters from beach", rules: animals on request only, no smoking inside, no party)
- rentalcorfu.com/ (guest extras: early check-in, late checkout, beach towels, welcome surprises, baby equipment, in-villa massage, car and boat rental; "a small family business based in Corfu")
- paleokastritsa.rentalcorfu.com/services/ (identity check and deposit before arrival, airport transfers, welcome packs, concierge)
- rentalpropertymanagementcorfu.com/ ("years of successful experience in renting out our own properties in Corfu and Belgium"; phones +30 690 646 92 49 and +32 496 43 37 50)

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not published:** check-in and check-out times, the
registration (AMA/MHTE) number, any prices for extras, the climate resilience fee amount, and a street
address. Capacity is stated as both "5 people" and "4/5 people"; the kit says up to 5. The page
also mentions an extra bedroom that's "not available at the moment", so it's left out. Door codes
and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Villa Pyrgos — Liapades`
   - **City, country:** `Liapades, Corfu, Greece`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the village).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Villa Pyrgos, a villa with an amazing sea view in a quiet spot in Liapades, near Paleokastritsa on Corfu's west coast. It was completely renovated in 2019–2020 and is looked after by Rental Property Management Corfu, a small family business based on the island. Expect a big private pool, more than 4,000 m² of garden over the olive grove, and exceptional sunsets.

ARRIVAL: Your check-in time is in your booking confirmation. Your key and door details are in your arrival message from RPM Corfu. Please don't share them.
Before you arrive we'll verify your identity and collect a damage deposit, as explained in your booking.
Arriving early? Ask us about early check-in, so you can drop your bags, take a shower and start your holiday straight away.
We can also arrange an airport transfer, or a car to pick up on arrival.

WI-FI: Wi-Fi is included. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Up to 5 guests.
No smoking inside the villa.
No parties.
Animals only on request. Please ask us before you book.

THE VILLA: The villa is on two floors.
- Ground floor. A large living room with a single and a double sofa bed, a fireplace, the kitchen area with dishwasher, washing machine and modern appliances, a dining area and a separate toilet.
- First floor. One double bedroom with an extra single bed, and a second double bedroom with magnificent sea and sunset views. Two bathrooms with showers.
Both bedrooms open onto balconies looking over the beach, the sea, the pool and the sunset. There are four balconies and two terraces in total.
Air conditioning in all rooms, plus a heating system.
Towels and sheets are provided. An extra baby bed is available. Please ask before you arrive.

POOL: The private pool is 7 m by 4 m, with an outside shower. The garden has hammocks and sun loungers among the olive trees.

PARKING: Private parking is available at the villa.
The villa is a short walk from the nearest beach and some lovely restaurants. Guests tell us shops and other amenities are 2 to 3 minutes away by car.

GREEK TAXES: Greece charges a climate resilience fee per night for holiday rentals. We'll tell you if anything is due for your stay.

CHECKOUT: Your check-out time is in your booking confirmation. Want to stay a bit longer? Ask us about late checkout.
Please turn off the air conditioning, close the windows and doors, and leave the keys as described in your arrival message. Thank you for staying with us!

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 112 (the European emergency number).
For anything about the villa, call or WhatsApp RPM Corfu on +30 690 646 92 49, or email info@rpmcorfu.com.

LOCAL TIPS: Paleokastritsa is often called the jewel of Corfu, with bays separated by small headlands. Its main beach is large and sandy and good for families, with sun loungers, umbrellas and watersports, and clear turquoise water for swimming and snorkelling.
There are excellent restaurants and cafés in the area.
Don't want to pack beach towels? We can provide them.
Travelling with kids? We can supply strollers, cots, beach games and even a small pool.
We can also arrange car or boat hire, welcome surprises (chocolates, champagne or roses) and an in-villa massage. Just ask.
```

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your phone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| How big is the pool? | 7 m x 4 m, private, with outdoor shower |
| Can we bring our dog? | Animals on request only; ask before booking |
| Is there parking? | Yes, private parking at the villa |
| Can we rent a boat? | Yes, they can arrange boat (and car) hire |
| Is there a cot for the baby? | Yes, extra baby bed on request |
| Can we have a party? | No parties; up to 5 guests |
| Υπάρχει κλιματισμός; (Greek) | Answers in Greek: yes, in all rooms, plus heating |
| Peut-on arriver plus tôt ? (French) | Answers in French: ask about early check-in |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to French or Greek) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the boat answer, the Greek answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
