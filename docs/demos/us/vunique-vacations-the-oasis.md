# Demo guide: Vunique Vacations, The Oasis (Siesta Key, FL)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Vunique Vacations' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why The Oasis:** Vunique manages large, single luxury homes rather than condo buildings, so there is no
"one building, many units" option. The Oasis is one of their four featured Siesta Key homes, has the most
detailed listing (7 bedrooms, sleeps 18, elevator, saltwater pool with waterslide and spa, rooftop deck,
pet friendly) and shows a 5.0 score from 116 reviews. Their house rules are company-wide (same terms for
every home), so the guide is easy to copy to their other homes.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): the property page
https://www.vuniquevacations.com/vacation-rentals/rental/the-oasis/ (listing description, address, beds,
sleeps, pet flag, reviews), the FAQ https://www.vuniquevacations.com/faq/ (check-in/out times, access codes
5 days before arrival, office hours, after-hours number, noise ordinance, thermostat ranges, starter kit,
beaches, pool heating), the Rental Terms & Conditions https://www.vuniquevacations.com/rental-terms-conditions/
(no early check-in or late checkout, smoking, pets, pool, trash/parking, beach towels, extra cleans),
https://www.vuniquevacations.com/vunique-vacations-environmental-faqs/, https://www.vuniquevacations.com/destinations/siesta-key/,
https://www.vuniquevacations.com/meet-our-team/ and https://www.vuniquevacations.com/contact/.
Every fact below comes from those pages. Door codes and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders.
Could not verify: the walking distance from The Oasis to the beach (not published, so not stated); the pool
heat setting (the FAQ says 85/86°F, the T&C says 84°F, the listing says heat up to 86°F is included, so the
guide only says "heated, no extra charge"); and the after-hours number (the FAQ says 941-877-0410, the T&C
says 941-702-4661; the guide uses 941-877-0410, which is also their main line).
Note the booking widget on the page currently says online booking is under maintenance (call to reserve).

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `The Oasis — Siesta Key`
   - **City, country:** `Siesta Key, Florida`
   - **Street address:** `105 Columbus Boulevard` (published on their listing).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to The Oasis, a 7-bedroom luxury home on Siesta Key cared for by Vunique Vacations. Every bedroom has its own en suite bathroom, an elevator serves all living levels, and the rooftop deck looks out over the Gulf of Mexico, Siesta Key Beach and the Village. Your dedicated property manager is available throughout your stay if you need anything.

ARRIVAL: Check-in begins after 4:00 PM (Eastern time). Please don't arrive, park in the driveway or unload before 4:00 PM, as our team needs the home until then to get it ready for you.
Your pre-arrival instructions and access codes are emailed to you about 5 days before arrival. Please read them in full. We're sorry, but early check-in is not available.
Your vacation home starter kit is waiting for you, with toilet paper, paper towels, dishwasher pods, dish soap and a bar of soap for each bathroom, so there's no need to rush to the store.
If anything isn't right when you arrive, please tell us the same day so we can fix it.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi covers the whole property, including the pool and outdoor areas. The network name and password are in your pre-arrival instructions.
Every bedroom has a smart TV, and the TVs have cable and streaming services.

HOUSE RULES: The Oasis sleeps up to 18 guests. Visitors beyond that number, parties and events need written approval from Vunique in advance.
An adult aged 25 or over must be staying in the home at all times.
Smoking is not permitted anywhere in the home. A $1,000 fee applies.
Firearms, ammunition and weapons of any kind (including airsoft and BB guns) are not allowed on the property.
The home is pet friendly for dogs only, up to 2 dogs and 50 pounds total, approved in advance (a pet fee applies). Dogs are never allowed in the pool, the spa or on furniture.
Quiet hours follow the local noise ordinance, 10 PM to 7 AM on weekdays and 10 PM to 10 AM on weekends and holidays. Sound carries over the pool, so please keep music and voices down outside.
Only toilet paper goes in the toilets. Please don't put peelings or fibrous food in the garbage disposal.
Please keep doors and windows closed while the air conditioning is running. The smart thermostat cools between 70 and 78 degrees and heats between 65 and 75 degrees, with the fan on auto.
Bath towels and linens stay inside. Use the beach and pool towels provided for the pool and beach.

POOL: The saltwater pool has a waterslide, beach entry, a sun shelf with loungers and a heated spa, all inside the fully fenced back yard.
The pool is heated from November 1 to May 1 at no extra charge. Very cool weather can stop it reaching the set temperature.
Please use only the pool remote and the instructions provided. Never change or tamper with the pool equipment.
The pool door alarms are there by Florida law for children's safety. Never disable them, and call us straight away if one isn't working.
Keep gates and doors to the pool area closed and locked, and always watch children in and around the water.

THE HOME: Around 4,000 sq ft over three levels plus the rooftop deck.
- Ground level has the covered pool patio with a sunken outdoor living room and TV, a bar seating 12, a natural gas grill, pool table, ping pong and outdoor Connect 4, plus a spare fridge and deep freezer.
- 2nd floor has the great room with the fully stocked kitchen, dining for 15, two balconies, the primary king suite, two more king bedrooms and the laundry room with two washer and dryer sets.
- 3rd floor has the media room with a wet bar, big smart TV and Nintendo Switch 2, three king bedrooms and the bunk room with two full-over-full bunks. Stairs from the media room balcony lead up to the rooftop deck.
The elevator serves every level except the rooftop deck.

BEACH: Your beach gear is stored on the pool patio, with 2 beach wagons, 2 coolers, 6 beach chairs and 4 bikes, all included.
Siesta Beach, the island's largest beach, is at 948 Beach Road, about 1.3 miles from Siesta Village. Turtle Beach is on the southwest side of the island at 8628 Turtle Beach Road.
The smaller beach access points around the island have no lifeguards, and parking varies from one to the next.
Before you go, you can check the Siesta Key beach webcam and the red tide status from Mote Marine or the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission.

PARKING: Free covered parking for 2 vehicles on the ground level, and the driveway fits about 2 more depending on size.
Boats, trailers, RVs and commercial vehicles need approval from Vunique before arrival.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM (Eastern time). We're sorry, but late checkout isn't available, and staying past 10:00 AM is billed as a full extra day.
Before you leave, please lock all doors and windows and leave any remotes and keys in the home.
If anything was damaged during your stay, please tell us before you leave. Thank you for staying with Vunique!

EMERGENCY: For any medical or life-threatening emergency, call 911.
For urgent problems with the home after hours (non-medical), call Vunique Vacations on 941-877-0410.
Our office is open every day from 9 AM to 7 PM on the same number, and your property manager can help with anything during your stay.
Please report any maintenance problem promptly so we can fix it.
In hurricane season, follow local official instructions. Sarasota County publishes evacuation maps and evacuation centers.

LOCAL TIPS: Siesta Key is about 8 miles long, with powdery white quartz sand that is regularly ranked among the best beaches in the country.
Siesta Key Village has boutiques, restaurants and nightlife, and the rooftop deck is perfect for sunrise over the Village or sunset over the Gulf.
Our Siesta Key Area Guide on vuniquevacations.com lists our vetted partners for restaurants, private chefs, boat tours, watersports, fishing, spas and grocery stores.
Your property manager can arrange grocery delivery, beach rentals or restaurant reservations for you, and a mid-stay clean on longer stays.
```

says the property manager can arrange; the prices are **demo examples** (Vunique doesn't publish them), so
tell them they set their own prices.
