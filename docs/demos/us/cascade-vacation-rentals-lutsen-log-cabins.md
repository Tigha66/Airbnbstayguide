# Demo guide: Cascade Vacation Rentals, Lutsen Log Cabins (Lutsen, MN)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Cascade Vacation Rentals' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Lutsen Log Cabins:** ten traditional 2-bedroom log cabins (formerly part of Lutsen Resort) on one
site overlooking Lake Superior, near the bottom of Ski Hill Road, all managed by Cascade Vacation Rentals.
Nine of them are listed on their site with the same layout (2 bed, 1 bath, sleeps 6), so one guide fits a
whole cluster of their listings. The demo uses **Lutsen Log Cabin 402** as the example unit.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- https://www.cascadevacationrentals.com/vrp/unit/Lutsen_Log_Cabin_402-357-15 (property page: description, bedrooms, amenities, dog policy, area info)
- https://www.cascadevacationrentals.com/resorts/lutsen-log-cabins/ (the ten-cabin complex)
- https://www.cascadevacationrentals.com/terms-conditions/ (check-in/out, early/late fees, quiet hours, fires, trash, pets, emergency phone)
- https://www.cascadevacationrentals.com/faq/ (check-in/out times, what's provided, cell service, office address)
- https://www.cascadevacationrentals.com/about-cascade-vacation-rentals/ and the homepage (175+ properties, "one of the largest selection" claim, owners)

Every fact below comes from those pages. Not verified: whether firewood is supplied for the wood-burning
fireplace, whether this cabin has trash pick-up (the terms say "refer to your check-in letter"), office
hours, and the cabin's street address. Things only the host knows (door codes, Wi-Fi details) are left
as placeholders, and you should say so in the email. Note: the homepage says "one of the largest", not
"the largest", selection on the North Shore, so the email uses their exact wording.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Lutsen Log Cabin 402 — Lutsen`
   - **City, country:** `Lutsen, Minnesota`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Lutsen Log Cabin 402, a handcrafted two-bedroom log cabin overlooking Lake Superior in Lutsen, Minnesota. It is one of ten traditional log cabins that were once part of Lutsen Resort, now managed by the local team at Cascade Vacation Rentals. The cabins are no longer associated with Lutsen Resort.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is at 4:00 PM. All of our homes are direct check-in, so you can go straight to the cabin.
Your check-in directions and door code come in a separate confirmation email about 7 days before your stay, together with your Guest Portal invite. Please don't share the code.
Early check-in is not possible between June 1 and October 31. From November 1 to May 31 we may be able to let you in 2 hours early for $50 plus tax. Ask no more than 48 hours before arrival.
Please don't enter before your check-in time without permission. Unapproved early entry is charged $150.

WI-FI: The cabin has Wi-Fi. The network name and password are in your arrival message.
Internet on the North Shore is slower than you may be used to, and bad weather can affect it.
The Smart TV has no services provided, so bring your own streaming logins if you want them.
Cell service from most carriers works along Highway 61 through Grand Marais, but it can be spotty elsewhere and short outages happen.

YOUR LOG HOME: A true log cabin with vaulted ceilings and picture windows looking out over Lake Superior.
The great room has a full galley kitchen, a dining area with room for the whole family, and a living room with a wood-burning fireplace.
Bedroom 1 has a king bed. Bedroom 2 has two full beds. One bathroom with a shower and tub.
The kitchen has dishes, pots and flatware, a microwave, toaster and coffee maker.
Bed, bath and kitchen linens are provided, plus a starter supply of trash bags, paper towels, toilet paper, dish soap and bar soap. Please bring your own toiletries.
Because of the health code we can't provide any food or seasoning, not even salt and pepper. Bring what you need.
There are board games and puzzles, a hair dryer, and smoke and carbon monoxide detectors and a fire extinguisher.
Step out onto the lake-facing deck for your morning coffee. Deck furniture may be seasonal.
The cabin has an electric grill. It may not work in cold weather.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests. Children aged 2 and up count as guests. No extra guests in sleeping bags, tents or RVs.
No smoking inside the cabin. If you smoke outside, mind the fire danger, keep smoke away from open windows and pick up all cigarette butts.
Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. House parties are not allowed.
Please don't rearrange the furniture. If you move something, put it back before you leave.
The shoreline next to other properties is private. Enjoy the shore by your cabin and don't cross onto neighbors' land.
No fireworks, no weapons, and please don't disturb the wildlife.
The cabin is on a septic system. Only flush toilet paper, and let us know if a toilet runs or an alarm sounds.
Cluster flies, ants and spiders are common here and harmless. If you see a mouse or signs of one, tell us.
Fires are welcome, done safely. For the wood-burning fireplace, please build safe, reasonably sized fires.
For campfires, use only the shared fire pit outside, don't use the indoor fireplace wood, and follow any seasonal fire bans.
Always put a campfire fully out. The Game Warden and Sheriff's Office give a $150 ticket for fires left unattended or not completely out.

DOGS: This cabin is dog-friendly, up to 2 dogs with prior approval. Sorry, no other pets.
The pet fee is $150 plus tax per reservation.
Please pick up after your dog, keep it on a leash outside, keep it off the furniture and beds, and kennel it if you leave it alone in the cabin.

PARKING: Free parking on site, right by the cabin.
In winter we recommend a 4WD or AWD vehicle. The road to the cabin and the driveway may be icy.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is at 10:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
Late checkout is not possible between June 1 and October 31. From November 1 to May 31 we may be able to offer 2 extra hours for $50 plus tax. Ask 24 hours before departure.
You don't need to clean, but please leave the cabin ready for the cleaner.
- Load and run the dishwasher if there is one, or wash up.
- Take all trash with you. The dumpster and recycling are behind the Cascade Vacation Rentals office at 5335 West Highway 61 in Lutsen. Never leave trash outside, because animals will spread it.
- Clean up after your dog.
- Check for personal items before you go.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For urgent problems after hours (no heat, no water, a septic alarm), call the Cascade Vacation Rentals emergency phone on 218-264-0507.
For everything else, call the office on 877-415-8974 during business hours.
If you lock yourself out outside business hours, there is a minimum $40 lockout fee.
Hiking, swimming and boating are at your own risk. Watch out for slippery rocks and fast rivers, keep a close eye on children near water and cliffs, and always wear a life jacket when boating. No water sports equipment is provided.

LOCAL TIPS: Right on your doorstep
- Lutsen Mountains is up Ski Hill Road. Skiing in winter, and the Alpine Slide and sightseeing gondola in summer.
- Superior National at Lutsen, a 27-hole golf course, is right across the street.
- The Gitchi Gami State Trail for biking is just across Highway 61.
- Temperance River State Park is about 10 miles west.
- Cascade River State Park is about 10 miles east.
- Grand Marais is a 20-minute scenic drive, with the North House Folk School, the Grand Marais Art Colony and dozens of small shops and restaurants.
Fall colors bring the busiest weeks of the year, so plan restaurant visits early.
Ask the Cascade team for hiking trail ideas, restaurant tips, and snow and snowmobile trail reports.
```

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The first
two prices are **Cascade's real published fees** (terms and conditions); the dog fee is their published
pet fee. Treat all three as **demo examples** and tell them they can set or change their own.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Early check-in (from 2:00 PM) | $50 | Get to the lake 2 hours sooner. November to May only, request within 48 hours of arrival, plus tax. |
| Late checkout (until 12:00 PM) | $50 | Two more hours of Lake Superior views. November to May only, request 24 hours before departure, plus tax. |
| Bring your dog (up to 2) | $150 | Your dogs are welcome at Cabin 402 with prior approval. Per reservation, plus tax. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

| What do we do with the trash? | Take it with you; dumpster and recycling behind the office at 5335 West Highway 61 |
| Do we need 4WD in January? | 4WD or AWD recommended in winter; the road and driveway may be icy |
