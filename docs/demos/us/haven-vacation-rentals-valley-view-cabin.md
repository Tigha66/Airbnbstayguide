# Demo guide: Haven Vacation Rentals, Valley View Cabin at Starr Crest Resort (Pigeon Forge, TN)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Haven Vacation Rentals' cabins *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Valley View Cabin:** a classic Smokies cabin with every question cabin guests ask: why isn't
the hot tub hot yet (it takes about 6 hours), do we need 4WD, can we check in early (no), who brings
the charcoal (you do), and spotty cell signal. Haven's listings share the same notes, so the guide
is easy to copy to their other cabins.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): their direct booking site
stay.havenvacationrentals.com/listings/349839 ("Valley View Cabin: View, Hot Tub, Fireplace, Grill":
description, amenities, distances, notes, check-in 4 pm, check-out 10 am, pets not allowed, no smoking
inside) and havenvacationrentals.com (phone +1-865-263-7366, info@havenvacationrentals.com). Every fact
below comes from those pages. **Not published:** quiet hours, maximum vehicle count beyond "free
parking for 2 vehicles", trash or bear rules, and the address (only "Starr Crest Resort"). Door codes
and the Wi-Fi password are left as placeholders, and you should say so in the email.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Valley View Cabin — Pigeon Forge`
   - **City, country:** `Pigeon Forge, Tennessee`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Valley View Cabin in Starr Crest Resort, managed by Haven Vacation Rentals. Enjoy Great Smoky Mountain views from the rocking chairs or the private hot tub, 5.5 miles from Dollywood and minutes from Pigeon Forge and Gatlinburg. The cabin sleeps up to 7 guests.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 4:00 PM. Your door code is sent in your arrival message from Haven Vacation Rentals. Please don't share it.
Because of our agreement with our cleaning team, we can't offer early check-in or late check-out.
The cabin is in the mountains. 4WD may be needed in snowy or icy conditions, but isn't necessary in normal conditions. We recommend having a vehicle for your stay.
Cell phone reception can be spotty because of the rural location.

WI-FI: Free Wi-Fi. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 7 guests.
No pets.
No smoking inside the cabin.
No RV or trailer parking.
We supply a limited starter kit of toilet paper, soap, paper towels, trash bags and dishwasher detergent. There are several shops nearby for anything else you need during your stay.

HOT TUB: The private hot tub takes about 6 hours to heat up. Depending on when the cabin was cleaned, it may not be hot yet when you arrive. Give it a few hours.

INSIDE THE HOME: Main level master bedroom with a king bed, a 43-inch Roku TV and an en-suite bathroom with a shower and tub.
Upper level second bedroom with a king bed, a 50-inch Smart TV and an electric fireplace. The upper bathroom has a standing shower and a jacuzzi tub.
A queen sleeper sofa in the living area and a twin pullout on the upper level.
Electric fireplaces in the living room and master bedroom, and a 70-inch Smart TV.
Game room with a pool table, foosball, air hockey, a poker table and a multicade arcade game.
Fully equipped kitchen with an island, cookware and dinnerware, and a single and multi-serve coffee maker. Indoor dining for 6, plus an outdoor table.
Free washer and dryer, central air conditioning and heat, starter toiletries, linens and towels.
Outside, a charcoal BBQ grill and an outdoor lounge with rocking chairs. Please bring your own charcoal.

PARKING: Free parking for 2 vehicles. No RVs or trailers.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM. Late check-out isn't possible because of our cleaning schedule. Thank you for staying with us!
Please turn off the fireplaces and lights and lock the door when you leave.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911. Cell signal can be spotty here, so if a call fails, try moving to another spot or use Wi-Fi calling.
For anything about the cabin, call Haven Vacation Rentals on 865-263-7366 or email info@havenvacationrentals.com. We're available if you need us!

LOCAL TIPS: Distances from the cabin:
```

## Step 3: Property details

| Check-out | `10:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| Why isn't the hot tub hot? | It takes about 6 hours to heat; may not be hot on arrival |
| Can we check in at 1 PM? | No early check-in; from 4:00 PM |
| Do we need 4WD? | Only in snowy or icy conditions |
| Do you provide charcoal? | No, bring your own (or the charcoal extra) |
| Can we bring our dog? | No pets |
| How far is Dollywood? | 5.5 miles |
| My phone has no signal, is that normal? | Yes, reception can be spotty in this rural location |
| ¿Hay lavadora y secadora? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: yes, free washer and dryer |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the hot tub answer, the Spanish answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
