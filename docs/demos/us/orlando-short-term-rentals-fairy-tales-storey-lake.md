# Demo guide: Orlando Short Term Rentals, Fairy Tales at Storey Lake (Kissimmee, FL)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Orlando Short Term Rentals' homes *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Fairy Tales at Storey Lake:** a 6-bedroom private-pool home with Frozen and Star Wars themed
rooms in the Storey Lake resort (they list more than one home there). It has every classic Orlando
guest question in one place: pool heat fees, the spa that only heats with the pool, the 72-degree A/C
lock, twice-daily trash pickup and the resort water park. Their FAQ answers most of these already,
so the guide is rich.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): orlandoshortterm.com/vacation-rentals/fairy-tales-at-storey-lake/
(description, policies, address 2827 Bookmark Dr, Kissimmee), orlandoshortterm.com/faq/ (pool heat,
early check-in, late checkout, baby package, BBQ, Wi-Fi reset, checkout steps, supplies) and
orlandoshortterm.com/terms-and-conditions/ (quiet hours, smoking, parking, thermostat, office hours).
Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not published:** the standard check-in and check-out times
(the FAQ only says late checkout runs "until 12 pm"), and the maximum guest count is "ask the host"
in their terms (the listing says 15 guests). Door codes, gate access and the Wi-Fi password are left
as placeholders, and you should say so in the email.

---

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Fairy Tales at Storey Lake — Kissimmee`
   - **City, country:** `Kissimmee, Florida`
   - **Street address:** `2827 Bookmark Dr, Kissimmee, FL` (published on their listing page)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Fairy Tales at Storey Lake, a 6-bedroom, 5-bathroom vacation home in the gated Storey Lake resort in Kissimmee, managed by Orlando Short Term Rentals. It sleeps up to 15 guests, with a private screened-in pool and jacuzzi, a game room, and hand-painted Frozen and Star Wars themed bedrooms for the kids.

ARRIVAL: Your check-in time is in your booking confirmation. Your door code and gate access details are sent in your arrival message from Orlando Short Term Rentals. Please don't share them.
Early check-in is free when available. Contact us one day before arrival and we'll confirm.
Our guest team is available 7 days a week from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM. We don't have a 24-hour front desk.

WI-FI: Free high-speed Wi-Fi. The network name and password are in your arrival message.
If the Wi-Fi stops working, unplug the modem, wait 5 minutes and plug it back in, then wait a few minutes for it to come back online. If that doesn't work, email guest@orlandoshortterm.com.
The TVs have basic cable. Most of our homes have smart TVs, so you can sign in to Netflix, YouTube or Disney Channel with your own accounts.

HOUSE RULES: The minimum age to book is 18.
No pets.
No smoking inside the home. Smoking is allowed outside.
Quiet hours start at 10:00 PM. Please keep pool and outdoor noise to a minimum and be good neighbors.
The air conditioning can't be set below 72 degrees, and heat can't be set above 78. Keep the fan on "Auto" and keep doors and windows closed while the A/C or heat is running. If it's set to 72 but the house is warmer, send us a photo of the thermostat and we'll send maintenance.
There are surveillance cameras outside the property.
No landline phone is provided.

POOL: The private screened-in pool and jacuzzi are just for your group.
Pool heat is an extra, at $29 plus tax per day ($32.92), for stays of 3 nights or more. Please order it at least 7 days before arrival, because orders placed later have a $50 service fee. Pool heat is not available for 2-night stays.
We turn the pool heat on the day you arrive, and it takes a few hours to warm the water.
The jacuzzi shares the pool's heating equipment, so it can only be heated when pool heat is on.

THE HOME: First floor bedroom with a king bed and TV. Upstairs, the Star Wars room (bunk bed and twin bed), two king suites with private bathrooms, a queen room with TV, and the Frozen room (bunk bed and twin bed).
Upstairs game room with a large TV and a soccer table.
Fully equipped kitchen with dishwasher, ice maker, coffee maker and a high chair, plus a washer and dryer.
Baby package (high chair, pack-n-play and stroller) and a gas BBQ grill are available to order, even at the last minute.

STOREY LAKE RESORT: All guests get free access to the amenities inside the gated resort. The Hideaway Club has a resort-style pool with a splash water park, a lazy river, sports courts, beach volleyball, outdoor basketball, a full gym, children's playgrounds, mini-golf and kayaking, plus a full-service restaurant and bar.
Storey Lake also has nature trails and conservation areas.

PARKING: Parking is included with the home. Street and sidewalk parking are not allowed, and you are responsible for any towing fees.
No trailers, RVs, boats or company trucks on the property or in the community.

TRASH: Please put your trash in the trash bin outside. Don't leave it all until checkout, or the bin will overflow. Trash is picked up twice a day, at about 8:30 AM and 12:00 PM.

CHECKOUT: Your check-out time is in your booking confirmation. Late checkout until 12:00 PM is $75 when available. Please request it at least one week in advance.
Before you leave, please follow these steps.
- Put the trash in the trash can or bench outside.
- Leave the dirty towels by the laundry.
- Take all your belongings. If we have to ship something you left behind, there's a $25 service charge and you send us a shipping label.
- Lock the front door using the keypad.
Thank you for staying with us!

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the home, contact Orlando Short Term Rentals at guest@orlandoshortterm.com, call 1-321-999-7926 or WhatsApp +1 407-955-0051. Our team is available from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM, 7 days a week.

LOCAL TIPS: The home is close to the theme parks, restaurants and shopping centers of Kissimmee and Orlando.
Inside Storey Lake, the Hideaway Club is the place to start, with the splash water park, lazy river and a restaurant and bar.
Florida sun is strong all year. Use sunscreen, drink plenty of water, and always supervise children around the pool.
```

| Can we park on the street? | No; street and sidewalk parking not allowed, towing at guest's cost |
