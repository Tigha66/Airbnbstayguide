# Demo guide: Booe Realty, A Place at the Beach (Myrtle Beach, SC)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Booe Realty's condos *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why A Place at the Beach:** it's the complex where Booe has the most rentals on its own site
(16 units in its sitemap, from 9520-1E to 9660-208), in the Shore Drive area of northern Myrtle Beach.
One guide covers all of them; only the unit number, bedrooms and view change. The listing for unit
9660-120 plus Booe's detailed Rental Policies page give almost everything a guest asks.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- booerealty.com/myrtle-beach-vacation-rentals/place-beach-9660-120 (2 BR / 2 BA, sleeps 6, 3 queen beds, resort pool, grilling area, direct beach access, pet-free, motorcycle-free, no smoking, one parking pass in July, Shore Drive location, Free Fun Program)
- booerealty.com/rental-policies-info (check-in after 3:00 PM, check-out by 10:00 AM, checkout steps, keys back to office, age 25+, ID and guest list at check-in, office hours, after-hours line 843-497-3475, linens optional, starter pack, what to bring, grilling, fireworks, hurricane policy, parking)
- booerealty.com/free-myrtle-beach-adventures (Xplorie free daily activities)
- booerealty.com/about-us (since 1971, Miller Hawkins, "Booe Crew", 500+ rentals)
- booerealty.com/myrtle-beach-condo-complexes (35+ resorts and condominiums)
- booerealty.com/contact-us (contact form, 7728 N. Kings Highway, 800-845-0647, local 843-449-4477)

Not verified: whether keys are collected at the office or by code for this unit (the policy says keys
are returned to the office at checkout), pool and hot tub hours, the linen and equipment rental
prices, and which washer/dryer setup this unit has (the listing shows both "Washer Dryer" and
"Coin Operated Washer/Dryer"). These are left vague or marked as examples.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `A Place at the Beach 9660-120 — Myrtle Beach`
   - **City, country:** `Myrtle Beach, South Carolina`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to A Place at the Beach 9660-120, a 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom condo in the popular Shore Drive area of northern Myrtle Beach, managed by Booe Realty, a local Myrtle Beach company since 1971. The complex has direct beach access, so you can walk straight from the pool to the sand.

ARRIVAL: Check-in begins after 3:00 PM, as properties become available. In busy summer weeks, cleaning can take a little longer, so thank you for your patience.
Please bring a valid photo ID and a complete list of everyone in your group to check-in.
Your check-in instructions, including how to get your keys and any door code, are in your arrival message from Booe Realty. Please don't share them.

WI-FI: The condo has Wi-Fi and a smart TV. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests. Occupancy is limited to the beds in the condo, which are 3 queen beds (infants excluded).
Everyone in the rental party must be at least 25 years old, except children under 16.
Overcrowding, unauthorized guests and house parties are strictly prohibited and lead to eviction without refund.
A Place at the Beach is a pet-free and motorcycle-free complex.
The complex has a strict no-smoking policy on the premises.
Grilling is allowed only in the designated grilling area. Absolutely no grilling on balconies or porches.
Fireworks are strictly prohibited by local law.
Please follow the complex's HOA rules. Any fines for violations are charged to the guest.

THE CONDO: 2 bedrooms with 3 queen beds, and 2 bathrooms.
Fully equipped kitchen with refrigerator, oven, stove, microwave, dishwasher, coffee maker, toaster, dishes and silverware.
Living room and dining area with a smart TV and cable, air conditioning and heating, an iron and ironing board, and a private balcony with ocean views.
Bedspreads, blankets and pillows are included. Sheets and towels are not included unless you rent a linen package, so bring your own or ask us at least two weeks before you arrive.
A starter pack is waiting for you with paper towels, trash bags, dish detergent and a sponge, a dishwasher pod, and one bar of soap and one roll of toilet paper per bathroom.
Please bring beach towels, chairs, coolers and toys, plus extra paper products, soaps and toiletries. There are plenty of stores nearby.

POOL AND HOT TUB: The complex has resort swimming pools, a hot tub, sun decks, a picnic and grilling area, and direct beach access.
Pools, hot tubs, air conditioning and internet can't be guaranteed. Please report any problem promptly so we can fix it.

PARKING: Free parking is included. Please note that only one parking pass is provided in July.
Parking is limited and can't be guaranteed. Boats, trailers, motorcycles, campers and golf carts are often restricted by the HOA. Booe Realty isn't responsible for parking tickets or towing.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is no later than 10:00 AM. Before you leave, please
- wash, dry and put away all dishes, and empty the dishwasher
- take all trash to the outside bins or dumpster
- put any rented sheets, pillowcases and towels on the kitchen floor
- leave all blankets, comforters, pillows and mattress pads in place
- take all your belongings and any opened food with you
- lock all doors and return all keys to the Booe Realty office
Unopened non-perishable food can be donated when you drop off your keys.
Want the same week next year? You can reserve it while you're here, before you check out.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For urgent problems outside office hours, call the Booe Realty after-hours line on 843-497-3475. Non-emergency calls to this line may incur charges.
The office is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and until 6:00 PM on summer Saturdays (June to August). Call 843-449-4477 or 800-845-0647.
During hurricane season (June 1 to November 30), always follow mandatory evacuation orders.

LOCAL TIPS: Shore Drive is close to Tanger Outlets, Pirates Voyage and The Carolina Opry. Barefoot Landing is about a 10-minute drive.
Your stay includes free daily activities through Booe's Free Fun Program, powered by Xplorie. Each day you get one complimentary adult admission to attractions like mini golf, live shows or a round of golf, and from May to September Myrtle Waves Water Park and the SkyWheel. Book them in advance with the link Xplorie emails you after booking, or call Xplorie on 850-353-7324.
Spring and early fall bring warm, swim-friendly weather with fewer crowds.
```

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| What do we need to do before checkout? | Dishes, trash out, rented linens on the kitchen floor, keys back to the office by 10 AM |

| Who do I call at 11 PM if the AC breaks? | After-hours line 843-497-3475 |
