# Demo guide: Forever Destin Beach Rentals, Jade East 1640 (Destin, FL)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Forever Destin Beach Rentals' homes *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Jade East 1640:** a Gulf-front 3-bedroom condo (sleeps 10) in the gated Jade East resort, the
listing with the most guest-facing detail on their site: check-in/out times, the late-departure fee,
parking passes, the elevator to the beach level, pool, gym, grills and HOA rules. They also manage
Jade East 1530 in the same building, and most of their other 55 listed homes share the same company
rental agreement, so this guide is a template for the rest.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Jade East 1640 — Destin`
   - **City, country:** `Destin, Florida`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Jade East 1640, a Gulf-front condo perched above the dunes in the gated Jade East resort in Destin, cared for by the local team at Forever Destin Beach Rentals. Enjoy emerald water and sugar-white sand, a beachfront pool with a heated spa and waterfall, a private beach, a fitness center, tennis and pickleball.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is after 4:00 PM (Central time). Check-in and check-out times are strictly enforced, because our maintenance crew and cleaners use the parking spot between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM.
So the team can finish preparing the condo for you, please don't park or unload before 4:00 PM.
Your access details are sent in your arrival message from Forever Destin Beach Rentals. Please don't share them.
Unit 1640 has a reserved parking spot near the elevators. Take the elevator up to the condo.

WI-FI: The network name and password are in your arrival message. If you have any trouble connecting, call or text us and we'll help.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 10 guests. Occupancy is strictly limited to this number.
No pets or animals are allowed at Jade East. Service animals need a written request at booking.
No smoking or vaping inside the condo.
Only guests over 25 may book this home, and no party or college groups are allowed.
We keep quiet hours from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. Music inside must not be heard outside with the doors and windows closed, and all visitors who aren't staying must leave by 10:00 PM.
Jade East is a gated community with HOA rules, and they are strictly enforced. Guests are responsible for any HOA fines.
Weddings and events need our written approval in advance.
The parking passes, amenity items and keys must stay in the condo. Replacement charges apply, for example $50 for an amenity bracelet and $75 for a door key.

THE CONDO: Three bedrooms and three bathrooms. Bedroom 1 has a king bed and its own bathroom overlooking the Gulf of Mexico. Bedroom 2 has two queen beds. Bedroom 3 has a king bed. Two rollaway cots sleep two more.
The open-plan living, dining and kitchen area has Gulf views throughout. The kitchen is stocked with cooking equipment and a coffee maker.

We provide high thread count hypoallergenic linens, towels and a starter set of soaps, shampoo and lotion. Please bring extra supplies and your own beach towels, as the condo towels and linens must stay inside.
Linens are set up once for your stay. Fresh linens and extra cleaning are available for a fee. Just ask us.

POOL AND SPA: Take the elevator down to the beach level for the fitness center, the beachfront pool with its heated spa, waterfall and pool bathrooms, and the grilling area.
- No diving or jumping into the pool or hot tub.

PARKING: Use the reserved spot for unit 1640 near the elevators. Extra parking is on the north side of the lot, next to the tennis court.

A maximum of two vehicles may be parked at any time. Please ask us before bringing a boat, trailer, RV or jet ski.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is before 10:00 AM (Central time). Thank you for staying with us!
Late departures stop our team from finishing the clean and affect the next guests, so there is a minimum $200 fee for leaving after 10:00 AM.
Before you go, please wash the dishes, bag all trash and remove it, and leave the parking passes and keys in the condo. A $250 fee applies if garbage is left behind.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
For anything about the condo, call or text Forever Destin Beach Rentals on +1 (850) 396-1802, or email rentals@foreverdestinbeachrentals.com. Our maintenance team works seven days a week.
If a mandatory hurricane or tropical storm evacuation is ordered, follow the official instructions and contact us. Nights affected by a mandatory evacuation are refunded in full.

LOCAL TIPS: Big Kahuna's water park is right across the street.
Walk or take a short ride to award-winning restaurants, beachside bars, golf and family fun centers. We highly recommend a sunset stroll along the beach to nearby restaurants.
As our guest you get exclusive discounts with our local partners:

Our team speaks several languages, so message us in yours.
```

rental agreement says **no early check-in or late check-out is permitted**, so don't offer those.
The prices below are **demo examples**: Forever Destin publishes that mid-stay cleaning with fresh

| Can we bring our dog? | No pets or animals at Jade East (service animals by written request) |
| Is the hot tub heated? | Yes, the beachfront pool has a heated spa; take the elevator to the beach level |
| Can we grill? | Yes, BBQ grills alongside the beach |
| What's near us for the kids? | Big Kahuna's water park across the street |
