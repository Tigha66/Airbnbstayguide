# Demo guide: YOUROPO Apartments, Ribeira Porto 1 (Porto)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of YOUROPO's apartments *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Ribeira Porto 1:** a 1-bedroom river-view apartment with a balcony in the Ribeira, right on
the Douro, in their "YOUROPO - Ribeira Porto" building (four AL-licensed units). It's their
signature location (their Instagram calls the balcony "the very first stop when you arrive"), and one
guide can be copied to the sister units with small changes.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- youropo.com/rentals/apartment-porto-ribeira-porto-1-278676.html (1 double bed, sleeps 2, 45 m², river view, balcony, kitchen equipment, A/C, 24-hour reception, check-out before 12:00, no smoking, no pets, no groups of young people up to 18, breakfast €20 per person per night, cot and high chair included, tourist tax included in the price, distances, AL number 87496/AL)
- youropo.com/rnal-rnet/ (list of AL numbers; Ribeira Porto building 87491/AL, 87496/AL, 98174/AL, 114582/AL)
- youropo.com/about-youropo/ (started with one apartment, now "more than fifteen people")
- youropo.com/general-conditions/ (cancellation and payment only)

Every fact below comes from those pages. **Not published:** the check-in time (the listing's check-in
field is blank), the apartment's description text, luggage storage, parking and lift details. The
listing shows "Rua de Sá da Bandeira, 393, 4000-435 Porto" under "Other relevant information" on every
apartment page, so it's probably their office, not this apartment (whose postcode is 4050-080);
leave the street address empty. Door codes, key collection and the Wi-Fi password are left as
placeholders, and you should say so in the email.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Ribeira Porto 1 — YOUROPO`
   - **City, country:** `Porto, Portugal`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the city).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `Confirm` (not published on the listing) |
| Check-out | `12:00 PM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `+351 919 472 801` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. Breakfast
is **their real published price**; the other two are **demo examples** (they don't publish early
check-in, late checkout or transfer prices), so tell them they set their own.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|

| Late checkout (until 3:00 PM) | €30 | One more slow morning on the balcony over the Douro. Subject to availability. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your phone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time is checkout? | Before 12:00 noon |
| Do we have to pay the tourist tax? | No, it's already included in the price |
| How do we get to São Bento station? | About 800 m (train and metro) |
| Where's the nearest beach? | Praia da Luz, about 6.2 km |
| Can we have breakfast? | Yes, €20 per person per night, ask in advance |
| Is there a cot? | Yes, free; high chair too |
| A que horas é o check-in? (Portuguese) | Answers in Portuguese: it's in your booking confirmation; 24-hour reception |
| Est-ce qu'il y a la climatisation ? (French) | Answers in French: yes |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Portuguese) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the tourist tax answer, the Portuguese answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
