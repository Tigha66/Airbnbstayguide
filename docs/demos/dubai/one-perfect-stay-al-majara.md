# Demo guide: One Perfect Stay, Dubai Marina 1BR Waterfront in Al Majara (Dubai Marina)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of One Perfect Stay's apartments *before* contacting
them, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Al Majara:** a 1-bedroom waterfront apartment for 2 in Al Majara Towers, one of the "top
properties" on their direct-booking site. The listing is clear and complete: check-in and check-out
times, pool, gym and health club, covered parking, pets welcome, a guest relations officer, and the
Tourism Dirham at AED 10 per night. They already offer guests a guest book service, so a digital
guide that answers back is a natural next step.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): booking.oneperfectstay.com/listings/277848 ("Dubai
Marina 1BR Waterfront | Pool, Gym, Marina Walk": description, house rules, cancellation policy,
additional charges), booking.oneperfectstay.com (guest contact +971 4 320 0313,
bookings@oneperfectstay.com) and oneperfectstay.com (homepage: "Over 50k guests have stayed with us
since 2016", "hundreds of hand-picked homes", 24/7 guest support, guest book service; contact
homes@oneperfectstay.com and WhatsApp +971 56 566 3842). Every fact below comes from those pages.
**Not published:** passport/ID requirements for this unit, deposit (the booking engine shows none),
pool and gym hours, Wi-Fi password, unit number, access details, and prices for extras.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Al Majara 1BR Waterfront — Dubai Marina`
   - **City, country:** `Dubai Marina, Dubai, United Arab Emirates`
   - **Street address:** `Al Majara Towers, Dubai Marina` (building name as published)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 3:00 PM.
A dedicated guest relations officer is available throughout your stay, for check-in, check-out and any questions along the way.
Your access details and arrival instructions are in your arrival message from One Perfect Stay. Please don't share them.

GUEST REGISTRATION: Dubai holiday homes must register every guest, so please have passports (or Emirates ID for residents) ready for all guests.
Additional charges may apply for the Tourism Dirham (AED 10 per night) and 5% VAT. We brief you on these at booking.

WI-FI: High-speed Wi-Fi with an ethernet backup. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

No smoking inside the apartment.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `3:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `12:00 PM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `+971 4 320 0313` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. One Perfect
Stay lists airport pick-up, baby crib and deep cleaning as paid add-ons but publishes no prices, so
these are **demo examples**; tell them they set their own.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Airport pick-up | AED 200 | A driver meets you at DXB and brings you to Al Majara. |
| Baby crib and high chair | AED 75 | Ready in the apartment when you arrive. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time is checkout? | 12:00 PM (noon) |
| Can we bring our dog? | Yes, pets are welcome |
| Is the Tourism Dirham included? | AED 10 per night may be added, plus 5% VAT; briefed at booking |
| Is there a gym? | Yes, gym, pool, health club and kids' play area |
| Can I work from the apartment? | Yes, a dedicated workspace behind a closed door, Wi-Fi with ethernet backup |
| How do I get to the metro? | Tram and metro are a 5-minute walk |
| هل يمكنكم ترتيب استقبال من المطار؟ (Arabic: can you arrange an airport pick-up?) | Answers in Arabic: yes, ask the team |
| Есть ли детская кроватка? (Russian: is there a baby crib?) | Answers in Russian: yes, for an additional charge |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; access details are in the arrival message / hands over |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Arabic) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the Tourism Dirham answer, the Arabic answer, the Extras tab) to use in a follow-up email.
