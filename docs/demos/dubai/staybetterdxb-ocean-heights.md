# Demo guide: StayBetterDXB, Large Marina 2BR in Ocean Heights (Dubai Marina)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of StayBetterDXB's apartments *before* contacting
Taimur, so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Ocean Heights:** it's the first listing in their Property Portfolio ("Best Views in Dubai Lux
Marina Beach Large 2 BR"), a 71st-floor, 2-bedroom, about 1,800 sq ft apartment in Ocean Heights
for up to 8 guests. The listing is unusually detailed: self check-in with a lockbox, two access cards,
a free parking spot with instructions, passport copies, lost-card and smoking fees, during-stay
cleaning prices and a chauffeur service. Exactly the questions guests ask at 2am.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): staybetterdxb.ae/property-portfolio/ and
staybetterdxb.ae/apartments-for-rent-dubai-marina/ (their portfolio page, which sends guests to the
listing below), the linked listing airbnb.com/h/oceanheights2br7107 (host Taimur; description,
house rules, additional rules, check-in after 3:00 PM, checkout before 11:00 AM, quiet hours
10:00 PM–8:00 AM, "managed by Stay Better Vacation Homes Rental LLC"), staybetterdxb.ae/about-us/
(Taimur, ex-Wayfair and Booz Allen Hamilton; team of 10) and staybetterdxb.ae/contact-us/
(phone and WhatsApp +971 50 572 6114). **Names differ:** their site calls it "Large Marina 2 BR With
Panoramic Sea & Palm Views"; the Airbnb title is "71st Floor Views Marina 2BR Penthouse w/
Balconies". The listing publishes the parking spot number (49); it's included because it's public.
**Not published:** pool and gym hours, Wi-Fi password, lockbox code, Tourism Dirham amount, an
email address (the contact page shows an "Email" label but no address in the page text).

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Ocean Heights 2BR, 71st Floor — Dubai Marina`
   - **City, country:** `Dubai Marina, Dubai, United Arab Emirates`
   - **Street address:** `Ocean Heights, Dubai Marina` (building name as published)
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to your 2-bedroom apartment on the 71st floor of Ocean Heights, Dubai Marina, managed by StayBetterDXB (Stay Better Vacation Homes Rental LLC) on behalf of the owner. It's around 1,800 sq ft, with two balconies and unobstructed views of the Marina, Palm Jumeirah, the sea, Ain Dubai and more.

You'll find two building access cards for the apartment, giving you access to all the building amenities.
If you're arriving by car, tell the building through the audio box at the parking entrance that you're checking in and will collect the parking card from the apartment. They'll let you in.

There's a washer and dryer, plug adaptors, bath products, and an initial supply of bottled water, coffee and tea.

No parties or events. No commercial photography.

For anything about the apartment, call or WhatsApp StayBetterDXB on +971 50 572 6114. We're very responsive. If we can't pick up, leave a voicemail or send a WhatsApp and we'll get back to you.

LOCAL TIPS: Metro stations are a 10 to 20 minute walk.
Need a driver? Our chauffeur service can arrange it. Let us know at least a day in advance. We organise everything, and you pay the driver directly.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `3:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `11:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your arrival message` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `+971 50 572 6114` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. The
mid-stay cleaning price is from their listing ("around AED 175 – AED 350"); the early check-in and
late checkout prices are **demo examples**, so tell Taimur he sets his own.

| Name | Price | Description |

1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| Where do I park? | Free spot 49 on podium 3; parking card in the lockbox, on the top of the windshield |
| Can I smoke on the balcony? | Yes (cigarettes and vapes); inside costs AED 1,500 |
| Can we bring our dog? | One pet allowed; no large breeds, ask if unsure |
| Can someone clean mid-stay? | Yes, around AED 175–350; message the team |
| Is there a pool and gym? | Yes, plus jacuzzi, spa, kids' play area; use the two access cards |
| I lost an access card | AED 500 per lost card or key |
| هل تحتاجون نسخة من جواز السفر؟ (Arabic: do you need a passport copy?) | Answers in Arabic: yes, for all guests, as soon as possible after booking |

| What's the lockbox code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the arrival message / hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Arabic) and the **Extras** tab.
