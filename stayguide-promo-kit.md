## 3. Platform-specific posting notes

- **Reddit:** Post during the sub's designated self-promo window if one exists; otherwise lead with a *question* ("How do you handle repetitive guest questions?") and mention StayGuide in a reply rather than the original post — far less likely to get removed.
- **Facebook groups:** These tolerate founder posts well if you engage in the comments afterward (answer every question, don't drop-and-ghost). Post native text + 1-2 screenshots of the guest chat UI, not just a bare link.
- **BiggerPockets:** Put this exact post in their **Marketplace forum**, not the general Vacation Rentals discussion board, and keep the disclosure line — it's required by their rules.
- **AirHosts Forum / STR Forum:** Smaller, friendlier to genuine builder posts placed directly in general discussion.

## 4. Checklist before you post anywhere

- [ ] Read the community's pinned rules / sidebar for self-promotion policy
- [ ] Disclose that you're the builder/founder (required by BiggerPockets, good practice everywhere)
- [ ] Attach 1-2 real screenshots (guest chat, guide view) where image posts are allowed
- [ ] Plan to reply to every comment in the first 24 hours
- [ ] Don't cross-post identical text to multiple subs/groups same day — space it out
- [ ] Use the community's designated promo thread/section if one exists, instead of the main feed
