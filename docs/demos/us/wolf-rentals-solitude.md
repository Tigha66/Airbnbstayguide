# Demo guide: The Wolf Rentals, Solitude Cabins (Estes Park, CO)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of The Wolf Rentals' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Solitude Cabins:** 45 near-identical 2-bedroom cabins on one 9-acre site (plus 7 lodge rooms), all
managed by The Wolf Rentals. One guide fits dozens of their listings, so it's the most convincing demo.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026): estesparkvacationrental.com/solitude/ and their Hostaway
listings on book.thewolfrentals.com ("Family friendly mountain cabin with stunning Twin Sisters views").
Every fact below comes from those pages. Things only the host knows (door codes, Wi-Fi password) are
left as placeholders, and you should say so in the email.

---

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Solitude Cabins — Estes Park`
   - **City, country:** `Estes Park, Colorado`
   - **Street address:** leave empty (Open in Maps will use the town).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Solitude Cabins, a peaceful 9-acre community of 45 mountain cabins in the pines near Lake Estes, managed by the local team at The Wolf Rentals. Elk, mule deer, wild turkeys and hummingbirds visit the neighborhood, and Rocky Mountain National Park is about 10 minutes away.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is from 4:00 PM. Drive up, park in your cabin's spaces and settle in. Your door code is sent in your arrival message from The Wolf Rentals. Please don't share it.
Early check-in may be possible for a small fee. It can only be confirmed 24 hours before arrival, and only if the cleaning schedule allows. Ask us and we'll check.
Estes Park is at about 8,000 feet. Drink plenty of water on your first day and take it easy.

WI-FI: Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi, good for streaming and remote work. The network name and password are in your arrival message.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 6 guests.
Strictly no smoking, inside or outside.
No fires of any kind. Outdoor fire pits are illegal in Estes Park vacation homes, and charcoal BBQs are banned. The gas grill on the deck is fine.
Do NOT leave food in your car. Bears are often in this area and can break into cars.
Give wildlife space, especially elk with calves in spring and during the fall rut. Watch from a distance.
Solitude is a quiet, family-friendly community. Please keep noise down in the evenings.

CABIN AND APPLIANCES: About 1,200 sq ft with two king bedrooms and two bathrooms. The upstairs bedroom has its own bathroom with a shower; the downstairs bathroom has a tub and shower and connects to the downstairs bedroom.
Gas fireplace in the living room for cool mountain evenings.
Full kitchen with range and oven, microwave, dishwasher, coffee maker, cookware and spices. Seating for six inside, plus outdoor dining on the deck.
Washer and dryer in the cabin, so you can pack light.
Smart TV with streaming, family games, books and toys.
There is no air conditioning, like nearly all homes in Estes Park. Portable fans are provided, and evening mountain breezes cool the cabin quickly.

HOT TUB: The shared community hot tub is a short walk from the cabins. It closes strictly at 9:00 PM.

PARKING: Free parking for two vehicles right by your cabin.
From October to May we recommend an AWD or 4WD car: we are at 8,000 feet and it can snow a lot. Estes has a great plow team and our parking area is plowed during storms, but in a big blizzard it may not be cleared exactly when you need it.
The town's free summer shuttle (July to September) stops in the Solitude neighborhood and runs to downtown, where you can connect to other shuttles around the Estes Valley.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is by 10:00 AM. Thank you for staying with us!
Please turn off the gas fireplace and the lights, close the windows, and lock the door.

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911. The nearest hospital is Estes Park Health, in town.
For anything about the cabin, call or text The Wolf Rentals local team on 970-480-1112.

LOCAL TIPS: Driving times from Solitude:
- 2 minutes: Estes Park Community Center (gym, pool, courts)
- 4 minutes: downtown Estes Park shops and the Riverwalk
- 7 minutes: Mustang Mountain Coaster
- 10 minutes: Fall River Entrance of Rocky Mountain National Park
- 15 minutes: Lily Lake boardwalk loop
- 25 minutes: Hidden Valley (sledding and backcountry ski area)
- 50 minutes: Boulder (Pearl Street, breweries)
- 90 minutes: Red Rocks Amphitheatre and Denver International Airport
Lake Estes is a 5–10 minute walk, with a lovely path all around it. You can also paddle or stand-up paddleboard on the lake.
Spring: lower trails clear first (Lily Lake, Sprague Lake, Moraine Park, Deer Mountain); waterfalls peak at Alberta Falls and Chasm Falls.
Summer: start early for Bear, Nymph, Dream and Emerald Lakes (strong hikers add Sky Pond). Drive Trail Ridge Road, or the seasonal Old Fall River Road. Evenings need a jacket.
Fall: golden aspens along Bear Lake Road, Moraine Park and the Peak-to-Peak Scenic Byway. Listen for elk bugling, from a distance.
Winter: snowshoe to Nymph, Dream or Emerald Lake, or sled at Hidden Valley (bring your own sled).
In busy months Rocky Mountain National Park may require a timed-entry reservation. Check nps.gov/romo before you go.
```

they set their own prices.

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| What time can we check in? | 4:00 PM; early check-in possibly, for a small fee, confirmed 24h before |
| What time does the hot tub close? | 9:00 PM, strictly |
| Can we make a campfire? | No: fire pits illegal, charcoal banned, gas grill OK |
| Do we need 4WD in November? | AWD or 4WD recommended from October to May |
| Is there air conditioning? | No, but fans are provided and evenings cool fast |
| How far is Rocky Mountain National Park? | 10 minutes to the Fall River Entrance; check timed entry |
| Can I leave snacks in the car? | No, because of bears |
| ¿Cuál es la contraseña del wifi? (Spanish) | Answers in Spanish: it's in your arrival message |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to Spanish) and the **Extras** tab.
