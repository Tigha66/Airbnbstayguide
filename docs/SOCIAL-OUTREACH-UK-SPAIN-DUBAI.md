Coverage note: UK and Spain have well-indexed host communities. Dubai/UAE does not — general web
search did not surface a dedicated, active holiday-home-host Facebook group or subreddit for the
UAE specifically (regulation there is dominated by DTCM licensing, and host discussion seems to
happen in private WhatsApp groups and general property/expat communities rather than public,
searchable ones). The Dubai section below reflects that gap honestly, with the best available
fallbacks rather than invented links.

## Message variants to use

- **Facebook groups (after permission):** the "Facebook host groups" message, disclosure-first.
- **Reddit:** the "r/airbnb_hosts" variant — disclosure inline, ends on a genuine question.
- **Associations / federations (Spain) and DTCM-adjacent bodies (Dubai):** these are trade
  bodies, not casual communities — a cold product pitch is the wrong register. Approach as a
  partnership/sponsorship or "would a member resource/demo be useful" inquiry, not a consumer ad.
- **Spanish-language copy:** translate the Facebook/Reddit message variants rather than
  posting English copy into Spanish-language groups — translate, don't just paste.
