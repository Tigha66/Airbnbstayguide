# Demo guide: Beach Retreats, Sunshine and Good Times (Holmes Beach, FL)

**Goal:** build a real StayGuide guide for one of Beach Retreats' homes *before* contacting them,
so the first email says "I built one of yours already, open it on your phone."

**Why Sunshine and Good Times:** it's one of the four homes featured on their homepage, and its page
is the most complete: 4 bedrooms plus a bonus room, heated pool and spa, rooftop deck with Gulf views,
2-minute walk to the beach, parking for 4 cars, quiet hours. Their FAQ adds the company-wide rules
(check-in 4 PM, check-out 10 AM, no early or late, contact-free arrival, 24/7 on-call manager), so
the same guide is a template for their ~50 other Anna Maria Island homes.

**Sources** (public, checked October 2026):
- beachretreatsfl.com/sunshine-and-good-times/ (home details, amenities, parking, quiet hours, age rule, pets 0, trolley and restaurant tips)
- beachretreatsfl.com/faq/ (check-in/out times, no early/late, linens, Wi-Fi info, contact-free access, 24/7 support, office hours 9–6 Mon–Sat, pet fee per pet)
- beachretreatsfl.com/concierge/ (partner services: beach equipment set-up, baby gear, chefs, charcuterie, boat charters)
- beachretreatsfl.com/activities/kids-stuff/ and /activities/island-eats/ (local tips)
- beachretreatsfl.com/contact-us/ (office address, phones, reservations@beachretreatsfl.com)

Not verified: smoking policy, pool/spa hours, trash days, and the exact pet fee (the FAQ only says
there is one, per pet). These are left out. Their FAQ says Wi-Fi details are in a "Guest View Guide",
so they may already use a digital guidebook; see the note in Step 7.

## Step 1: Create the property (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **https://www.getstayguide.com/dashboard** and log in.
2. Click **Add property**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Property name:** `Sunshine and Good Times — Holmes Beach`
   - **City, country:** `Holmes Beach, Florida`
   - **Street address:** `3702 5th Ave, Holmes Beach, FL 34217` (published on their listing page).
   - **Your house manual:** paste the whole block from **Step 2**.
4. Click **Create my guide**.

## Step 2: House manual to paste

Copy everything inside the box. Each `LABEL:` line becomes its own guide section.

```
Welcome to Sunshine and Good Times, a 4-bedroom beach house in Holmes Beach on Anna Maria Island, managed by Beach Retreats Vacation Rentals. The beach is only a 2-minute walk away, and the rooftop deck has sweeping views of the Gulf of Mexico.

ARRIVAL: Check-in is at 4:00 PM. We don't allow early check-in, so please plan to arrive from 4:00 PM.
Check-in is contact-free. You don't need to come to the office to pick anything up. Your door code is sent with your check-in information before you arrive. Please don't share it.
All linens are provided, including bath and beach towels, and every bed is made before you arrive. You'll also find a bathroom starter kit with toilet paper, paper towels, garbage bags, dish soap and hand soap.

WI-FI: High-speed Wi-Fi throughout the house, good for working from home. The network name and password are in your check-in information and in the white information binder in the house.

HOUSE RULES: Maximum 8 guests.
Guests must be 25 or older to book, or be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
Quiet hours are from 10 PM to 7 AM. You are vacationing in a residential area, so please keep noise to a respectful level during the day and night.
No pets at this home. Many other Beach Retreats homes are pet friendly, so ask us if you'd like to bring your dog next time.

THE HOUSE: 4 bedrooms plus a bonus room with 2 extra twin beds, and 4.5 bathrooms.
An elevator reaches all three floors of the home.
The custom kitchen has a bar fridge and a huge pantry, a Keurig, a coffee pot and a blender. The top level has a wet bar and a grill.
A second family room on the upper level works as an office or study.
Washer and dryer, iron and ironing board, and a hair dryer are in the house. For little ones there is a high chair and a pack and play.

POOL AND SPA: The large private pool is heated and has a spa. There are lounge chairs, a patio table and chairs, and an outdoor TV.
Beach chairs, beach towels, beach and pool toys and a beach wagon are provided, so you can head to the sand straight away.

PARKING: Covered and uncovered parking is included. The home can park up to 4 vehicles.

CHECKOUT: Check-out is at 10:00 AM. We don't allow late check-out, because our team gets the home ready for the next guests.
Thank you for staying with us!

EMERGENCY: For any emergency, call 911.
Beach Retreats has 24/7 support if an urgent issue comes up at the house. Call the on-call manager on the number in your information binder.
For anything that isn't urgent, call the office during business hours, 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday, on 941-270-3846, or text 941-299-6877.

LOCAL TIPS: Take the free trolley up to Bean Point, or down to historic Bridge Street.
Eat at Chateau Anna Maria, grab a burger at Skinny's, or watch the sunset at the Sandbar.
Go parasailing, take a sunset cruise, or paddleboard on the clear waters of the Gulf of Mexico.
For the kids, The Fish Hole in Bradenton Beach is a fun mini golf course with a koi pond, and West Coast Surf Shop in Holmes Beach, open since 1964, has skimboards and beach gear.
Our concierge partners can deliver and set up beach equipment, rent baby gear and car seats, cook for you at home, or book a boat charter. Ask us and we'll put you in touch.
```

## Step 3: Property details

Open the new property. In **Property details** (changes save automatically), set:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Check-in | `4:00 PM` |
| Check-out | `10:00 AM` |
| Wi-Fi network | `In your check-in information` |
| Wi-Fi password | leave empty |
| Host phone | `941-270-3846` |

New guides get a default cover photo; it can't be changed in the editor yet. That's fine for a demo.

## Step 4: Add 3 extras

Click the extras button (the window titled **"A thoughtful little extra"**) and add these. Beach
Retreats says it does **not** allow early check-in or late check-out, so don't offer those. These
extras match their own concierge partners. The prices are **demo examples**; tell them they set
their own.

| Name | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Beach set-up | $60 | Chairs and umbrella delivered and set up on the sand before you get there. |
| Baby gear package | $45 | Crib, high chair and car seat ready when you arrive, so you can pack light. |
| Welcome charcuterie board | $75 | A local charcuterie board waiting in the fridge for your first sunset. |

## Step 5: Publish and copy the link
1. Click **Publish**.
2. Copy the guide link (`https://www.getstayguide.com/g/<slug>`) using the share button.

## Step 6: Test it on your phone (and screenshot the answers)

Open the link on your iPhone, tap the **Concierge** tab and ask:

| Question | What it should say |
|---|---|
| Can we check in at 1 PM? | No: check-in is 4:00 PM, early check-in isn't allowed |
| Can we stay until noon on Saturday? | No: check-out is 10:00 AM, no late check-out |
| Is the pool heated? | Yes, heated pool with a spa |
| How far is the beach? | About a 2-minute walk; beach chairs, towels and a wagon are provided |
| How many cars can we bring? | Up to 4, covered and uncovered parking |
| Can we bring our dog? | Not to this home; other Beach Retreats homes are pet friendly |
| How do we get to Bridge Street? | Take the free trolley |
| Où est le mot de passe du Wi-Fi ? (French) | Answers in French: in your check-in information and the white binder |
| What's the door code? | Doesn't make one up; it's in the check-in information, or hands over to the host |

Also try **switching the guide's language** (for example to German) and the **Extras** tab.

**Save 2–3 screenshots** (the "Can we check in at 1 PM?" answer, the French answer, the Extras tab)
to use in a follow-up email.
